## Not built yet

- **Automatic nesting is an assistant, not an optimiser:** {{cmd:MARKERNEST}} has not been compared with a marker made by a pattern maker yet, and it does not split pieces across the fold or match plaids.
- **Plaids and stripes** in markers (matching pieces to a repeat), and **splitting** a marker across fabric widths.
- **Adding or removing points** on a piece's sew line: draw the outline again and use {{cmd:PIECE}}.
- **Pleats, darts that open and close, and walking a seam round a corner** onto the next edge.
- **Notch depth and width per notch kind** in DXF-AAMA files: the format does not carry them, so a file read in uses your settings.
- **Buttons and buttonholes in DXF-AAMA:** the format has no layer for them; they are written as internal lines with a note.
- **Opening our DXF-AAMA files in another pattern program** has not been tried yet: check one piece in the receiving program before sending a whole style.
- **Very large graded pieces:** a piece with very many points and sizes is more than one CadCraft piece holds. On reading, its curves are thinned slightly, or it comes in its sample size only, and CadCraft says so.
