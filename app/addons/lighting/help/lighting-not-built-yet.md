## Not built yet

- **A light cone in 3D.** Fixtures are drawn in the plan only; ModelCraft cones come later.
- **A maker's fixture library.** The fixture types are generic kinds of instrument with typical wattage and address counts. Set a fixture's own wattage and DMX footprint as you need them.
- **Circuit and patch sheets.** The hookup lists channel, address and load; a patch of dimmers into circuits and racks is not made.
- **Gel and gobo schedules.** The colour is a text you type; it is listed but not counted by stock.
- **Section views.** Trim heights are kept on a position but a section through the rig is not drawn.
