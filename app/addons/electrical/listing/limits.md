Electrical is a drafting and planning aid, a design aid, not an engineering approval. The engineer of record decides cable sizing, protection coordination, selectivity and code compliance.

**What it does not do yet:**

- Wires are not objects with their own colour or cross-section; use the layer name.
- A potential that continues on another sheet is two nets; there is no "continued on" arrow that joins them.
- No whole PLC cards or multi-level terminal blocks; PLC points and terminals are placed one by one.
- No editor for a symbol's connection points; blocks you import become symbols without one.
- Cable cores are numbered in the order the mark crosses them, not by colour.
- The panel schedule gives current and length, not voltage drop or cable size.
- Installation symbols are 2D.
- An RCD is a symbol and a line of text on a circuit. The add-on does not check which circuits it protects, its type (A, F, B), its selectivity or its trip time. Fire detection is symbols only: no loops, addresses or zones.
- Only English is written so far.
