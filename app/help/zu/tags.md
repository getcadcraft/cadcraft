I-**tag** iyisici sebhulokhi esinencazelo: ukuthi siphethe uhlobo luni lwenani, yimaphi amanani avunyelwe, nokuthi yimaphi amabhulokhi asebenza kuwo. Amanani ayizimpawu zebhulokhi ezijwayelekile, ngakho noma yiluphi olunye uhlelo luwafunda, futhi {{cmd:ATTEDIT}} iwabonisa. Izincazelo kuphela ezintsha, futhi zigcinwa emdwebeni.

## Chaza futhi usethe

Thayipha {{cmd:TAGS}} bese ukhetha:

- {{ui:Define}}: igama (izinhlamvu, izinombolo, isikhala, `_ . -`), uhlobo (`text`, `number`, `integer`, `yesno`, `length`, `angle`), amanani avunyelwe ahlukaniswe ngu-`;` (shiya kungenalutho ukuze kube noma yini), namagama amabhulokhi asebenza kuwo (`DOOR*` ifanisa yonke ibhulokhi eqala nge-DOOR; okungenalutho kusho wonke amabhulokhi).
- {{ui:Set}}: ngezinkomba zamabhulokhi ezikhethiwe, thayipha `FireRating=60 min; Width=850`. Inani elingaphandle kohlu olunikeziwe, noma elingesona inani lesibalo sohlobo lwenani, liyenqatshwa futhi liyatshelwa. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Ibhulokhi elingenaso isici lithola kona njengesici esifihliwe.
- {{ui:sChedule}}: yonke inkomba yebhulokhi i-tag esebenzela kulo, ngokuhlelwa eqenjini ngetag noma ngegama lebhulokhi, iphrintwa futhi ilondolozwe njenge-CSV.
- {{ui:Remove}}: ikhohlwa incazelo; amanani asala ezinkombeni.

Ukuqamba kabusha i-tag kuqamba kabusha namanani ayo kuyo yonke inkomba. Amagama e-tag awabhekwa kusayizi wezinhlamvu, njengoba namagama ezimpawu engenzi.
