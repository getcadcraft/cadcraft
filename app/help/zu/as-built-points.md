{{cmd:ASBUILT}} (i-alias ASB) iyingxenye yesibili ye-{{cmd:EXPORTPOINTS}}: uthumela amaphoyinti omdwebo ensimini, ithimba liwabeka ngokulandelana bese lilinganisa okwakhiwe, ASBUILT iqhathanisa ifayela labo nomdwebo.

1. Thayipha {{cmd:ASBUILT}} bese ukhetha amaphoyinti namabhulokhi afanayo owathumela ngaphandle (Enter ikhetha wonke ama-POINT, njengoba kwenza i-EXPORTPOINTS).
2. **Hlanganisa amaphoyinti okwakhiwe ngokuthi** **Inombolo** (ithimbaligcine izinombolo zakho zamaphoyinti: nikeza inombolo yokuqala owathumela kuyo) noma **Eduze** (zibhalile izinhlamvu zazo: iphoyinti ngalinye lomklamo lithatha eliseduze kakhulu).
3. Nikeza amayunithi nokuhlelwa kwamakhonkco njengoba wawathumela ngaphandle (Umdwebo, Mitha noma Feet; World noma UCS), bese unikeza i-**tolerance** ngamayunithi omdwebo.
4. Khetha ifayela le-CSV noma le-TXT lethimba. I-CadCraft iyifunda njengoba i-CivilCraft iyifunda: amakhoma, amathebhu, amasemikholoni noma izikhala, umugqa wekhanda onemibhalo yamakholomu, noma i-PNEZD ngokubala amakholomu.

Umugqa womyalo utshela ukuthi mangaki amaphoyinti ahlanganisiwe, mangaki angaphakathi kwe-tolerance, angaphandle kwayo, angakalinganiswa nengeziwe, kanye nebanga elikhulu kakhulu. **Yimaka emdwebeni?** Yebo kubeka wonke kumgqa **AS-BUILT** njengenyathelo elilodwa lokuhlehlisa: iphoyinti ngalinye lokudwetshwa, inombolo yalo nebanga lalo eduze kwalo, nomugqa obomvu usuka kuphoyinti lomklamo uye kuwo wonke amadwetshwa angaphandle kwe-tolerance. Umbiko we-CSV uyalandwa nawo wonke amaphoyinti: ukuxhumanisa komklamo nokwakhiwe, umehluko empumalanga, enyakatho nasemuva ukuphakama, nomphumela.
