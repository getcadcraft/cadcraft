## Okubhalwa yi-CadCraft

{{ui:Save}} ibhala i-DWG yangempela, enguqulweni idrowing yafika ngayo noma eyoyikhetha ku-{{ui:Save as}}: AutoCAD 2000, 2004,
2007, 2010, 2013 noma 2018. Akukho okuguqulwa ngemuva kwakho: umlando ulanda noma yini eshintshile ngenkathi (isibonelo umbala weqiniso obhalwe njengombala ojwayelekile oseduze kwi-DWG ye-2000).

## Lapho ivulwe khona

Yonke i-DWG eyalondolozwa yi-CadCraft ivulwa, kuyo yonke inguqulo, yizinhlelo ezintathu ezingeyona i-CadCraft: i-ODA File Converter (ehlola ifayela futhi ayilungisi lutho kulo), i-LibreDWG ne-libdxfrw. Ukufunda kwazo kuqhathaniswa nokwe-CadCraft: izinto, izingqimba, amabhulokhi nezici, izilinganiso, imigqa yokufiphaza, amashidi nemibonakalo yawo.

Ukuvulwa kwamafayela e-DWG e-CadCraft ku-AutoCAD nakwi-DWG TrueView yamahhala kuhlolwa ku-Windows, uhlobo ngalunye lwento (AUDIT, izingqimba, amashidi, izilinganiso, imigqa yokufiphaza, izici, amabhulokhi aguquguqukayo, izinkomba ezisikiwe, izithombe). Imiphumela izobhalwa lapha lapho isiphelile.

## Lapho enye inhlelo iphikisa

1. Gcina futhi njenge-DXF ({{ui:Save as}} › {{ui:DXF 2000}}) bese uyivula: izinhlelo eziningi zifunda i-DXF.
2. Londoloza i-DWG kuguqulo endala (2013 noma 2010).
3. Sitshele ku-Discord ukuthi yiyiphi inhlelo nenguqulo, nokuthi yathini; ifayela lisiza kakhulu.
