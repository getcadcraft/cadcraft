I-CadCraft isebenzisa amagama emiyalo namagama amafushane abantu abawaziyo kwezinye izinhlelo zokudweba: bhala `L` ukuze uthole umugqa, `TR` ukuze ucwecwe, `E` ukuze ususe. Ngakho akudingeki uthole ithebula lokuhumusha eliningi lazo: igama oliwaziyo ngu-igama lapha. Uhlu olulandelayo yiyo yonke imiyalo ene-CadCraft, eyenziwe yiyo ngokwayo. {{key:Enter}} iphinda eyokugcina; `HELP` emugqeni womyalo ibonisa uhlu olufanayo.

Eyimbalwa engafani, noma engakhiwanga:

- **Londoloza** ilanda ikhophi kusiphequluli ({{cmd:SAVE}}, {{cmd:SAVEAS}}); ku-CadCraft Studio ibhala ifayela. Bheka [Okuvela kwezinye izinhlelo zokudweba](topic:coming-from).
- **PLOT** yenza i-PDF (futhi i-PNG ne-SVG), ngethebula lezitayela zokuphrinta; alikho isiphathi sokusetha ikhasi.
- **DIMSTYLE** no **PEDIT** zakhiwe; i-**PEDIT** alinayo inketho ethi Edit-vertex (izibambisi zenza lokho).
- **TABLE** akakakhiwa; {{cmd:XREF}}, {{cmd:SPLINE}} no {{cmd:WBLOCK}} sekakhiwe.
- Imiyalo yohlelo oluthile (i-AutoLISP `C:NAME`) ivela ngemva kokuthi uyilayishe nge-{{cmd:APPLOAD}}; ayikhombiswa lapha.
