I-CadCraft ithatha amagama emiyalo namagama amafushane abantu abawaziyo kwezinye izinhlelo zokudweba: bhala `L` womugqa, `TR` ukuze uthrime, `E` ukuze ukhiphe. Ngakho awukho umbhalo wokuhumusha okufanele ufunde kuningi lawo: igama olaziyo ngulo lapha. Uhlu olungezansi yimiyalo yonke i-CadCraft enayo, eyenziwe yuhlelo ngokwalo. {{key:Enter}} iphinda owokugcina; `HELP` ku-umugqa womyalo ibhala lolu hlu.

Ezimbalwa ezingafani, noma ezingakakhiwa:

- **Londoloza** ilanda ikhophi kule browser ({{cmd:SAVE}}, {{cmd:SAVEAS}}); ku-CadCraft Studio ibhala ifayela. Bheka [Uvela kwezinye izinhlelo zokudweba](topic:coming-from).
- **PLOT** yenza i-PDF (futhi i-PNG ne-SVG), ngethebula lesitayela sokuphrinta; alikho umphathi wokumisa ikhasi.
- **DIMSTYLE** no **PEDIT** akhiwe; i-**PEDIT** ayinayo inketho ye-Edit-vertex (izibambi zenza lokho).
- I-**TABLE** ayikakhiwa; {{cmd:XREF}}, {{cmd:SPLINE}} ne-{{cmd:WBLOCK}} zikhona.
- Imiyalo yohlelo oluthile (AutoLISP `C:NAME`) iyavela uma uyilayisha nge-{{cmd:APPLOAD}}; ayikhonjwa lapha.
