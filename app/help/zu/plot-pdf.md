## Okuzokwenza

I-PDF yeshidi ngosayizi ogcwele, bese i-PDF esheshayo yemodeli ku-1:100, nesiphrintwa.

I-PDF yenziwa ngaphakathi kwesiphequluli sakho; akukho okulayishwa. Iyi-PDF ye-vector: imigqa ihlala icacile kunoma yisiphi isikali, futhi umbhalo ungasesha.

## Shicilela ishidi

1. Chofoza ithebhu yeshidi ngaphansi komdwebo.
2. Cindezela {{key:Mod+P}}, chofoza {{ui:Dweba}}, noma bhala `PLOT` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Ingxoxo {{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}} iyavula. Ithi ishicilela leshidi, "ngosayizi ogcwele … njengoba uwubona". Ukubukwa okusesokunene kubonisa leshidi.

3. Ku-{{ui:Isitayela sokuphrinta}}, khetha {{ui:Okumnyama kuphela (mnyama)}} imigqa emnyama, {{ui:Umbala (njengoba udwetshiwe)}} ukugcina imibala, {{ui:Igreyi}}, noma ithebula lakho (bona [Amathebula esitayela sokuphrinta](topic:plot-pdf#plot-style-tables)). Ishidi ligcina lokhu kukhetha: kulondolozwa kanye nomdwebo.
4. Ku-{{ui:Ubukhulu bomugqa}}, khetha ububanzi bepeni kusuka ku-0.13 mm kuya ku-0.70 mm. Imigqa enobukhulu bokuzenzakalela iphrintwa kulobu bubanzi; imigqa enobukhulu bayo (isendlalelo noma into, noma i-peni yombala wayo kwethebula) iphrintwa kulobo.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Landa i-PDF}}.

Isiphequluli silanda i-PDF egcinwe ngegama lomdwebo nelishidi, isibonelo `Drawing1-A-101 Plan.pdf`. Umlando ubika usayizi wayo ngamabhithi nosayizi wephepha ngamamilimitha.

Ishidi lihlala liphrintwa ngo-1:1 ephepheni layo. Ukuze ushintshe iphepha, shintsha kuleshidi uqobo (bona [Lungisa ishidi](topic:sheets-and-viewports)). Izendlalelo ezibekwe ukuthi zingaphrintwa ziyashiywa, kanjalo nezinxande zama-viewport.

## Onke amashidi ku-PDF eyodwa

Uma kunamashidi amabili noma ngaphezulu, ingxoxo {{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}} inayo {{ui:Zonke izishidi kwi-PDF eyodwa}}; noma bhala {{cmd:PUBLISH}}. {{key:Enter}} ithatha wonke amashidi; `S` (Some) icela amagama awo, ahlukaniswe ngamakhefa. I-PDF eyodwa iyalandwa, inekhasi ngalinye leshidi, ngokulandelana kwamathebhu, ngalinye ngosayizi wephepha lalo, nesimaka ngasinye ngegama lalo. Ishidi ngalinye lishicilela ngethebula lalo lesitayela sokuphrinta; ishidi elingakakhethi lisebenzisa elokugcina oliliKhethile engxoxweni, nobubanzi bepeni bokugcina obukhethile.

## Shicilela imodeli

1. Chofoza ithebhu {{ui:Imodeli}}.
2. Cindezela {{key:Mod+P}}.

Ingxoxo ithi ishicilela imodeli ephepheni, futhi ibonisa ezinye izilungiselelo:

3. Ku-{{ui:Indawo}}, khetha okuzoshicilelwa:
   - {{ui:Konke (imikhawulo)}}: umdwebo wonke.
   - {{ui:Okusesikrinini}}: indawo oyibona emdwebeni manje.
   - {{ui:Iwindi…}}: bese uchofoza {{ui:Khetha}}, uchofoze amakhona amabili emdwebeni, futhi ingxoxo iyabuya.
4. Ku-{{ui:Iphepha}}, khetha usayizi: ISO A4 kuya ku-A0, Letter, Legal, Tabloid kanye ne-ANSI C no-D, noma ARCH C, D no-E.
5. Ku-{{ui:Ukuma}}, khetha {{ui:Okuthe tshe}} noma {{ui:Okumile}}.
6. Ku-{{ui:Isikali}}, khetha {{ui:Lwanele iphepha}} noma isikali njenge `1:100`.

Ngaphansi kwezilungiselelo, umugqa uyatshela isikali nokuthi indawo iphuma ngosayizi muni ephepheni. Uma inkulu kunephepha, uyatshela: imiphetho iyasikwa. Khetha isikali esincane, iphepha elikhulu, noma {{ui:Lwanele iphepha}}.

7. Chofoza {{ui:Landa i-PDF}}.

Ukushicilelwa kwemodeli kushiya umbheda we-5 mm ozungeze umphetho, ngoba iphrinta eningi ayikwazi ukuphrinta kuze kube sephethweni. {{ui:Lwanele iphepha}} inikeza isikali esingajwayelekile (umlando nomugqa wolwazi kuthi "(fitted)"); khetha isikali esijwayelekile uma umdwebo kumele ulinganise ngempela.

:::note
Izikali zifundwa ngeyunithi zangempela. Ukuze 1:100 ibe yiyo, setha {{ui:Amayunithi}} ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}} ibe yilokho iyunithi eyodwa yomdwebo okuyona. Umdwebo ongenayo iyunithi uthathwa njengamamilimitha.
:::

## Amathebula esitayela sokuphrinta

Ithebula lesitayela sokuphrinta lisho ukuthi ngabe ngayinye yemibala engu-255 yomdwebo ejwayelekile iphrintwa njenganini, njengoba ithebula lesitayela elincike embaleni (CTB) lenza kwezinye izinhlelo ze-CAD. Kukhona okuthathu okwakhelwe ngaphakathi:

| Ithebula | Liphrinta |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Umbala (njengoba udwetshiwe)}} | Yonke into ngombala wayo. |
| {{ui:Okumnyama kuphela (mnyama)}} | Yonke imibala imnyama: okujwayelekile kumadrowing. |
| {{ui:Igreyi}} | Umbala ngamunye njengegreyi wawo, ukuze imibala ihlukane kuphrinta emnyama nomhlophe. |

Ukwenza eyakho, isibonelo imigqa ebomvu ibe mikhulu futhi imnyama, konke okunye kube mncane:

1. Engxoxweni {{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}}, chofoza {{ui:Hlela amathebula…}} (noma bhala {{cmd:PLOTSTYLES}}).
2. Chofoza {{ui:Okusha}}, bese ubhala igama njenge `office` ku-{{ui:Igama}}. Ukuze uqale kwelakhelwe ngaphakathi, khetha kuthi {{ui:Ithebula}} bese uchofoza {{ui:Kopisha}}.
3. Chofoza umbala ku-grid. {{key:Shift}}+chofoza ikhetha uhla lwemibala, {{key:Mod}}+chofoza yengeza enye, futhi {{ui:Khetha yonke imibala}} ibathatha bonke.
4. Setha okuphrintwa ngemibala ekhethiwe:
   - {{ui:Iphrinta njenge}}: {{ui:Umbala wayo}}, {{ui:Mnyama}} noma {{ui:Omunye umbala…}}.
   - {{ui:Njengegreyi}}: umbala njengegreyi wawo.
   - {{ui:Inki (%)}}: 100 kuyinki egcwele, 50 kuyisigamu esiya emhlophe (screening), kwimigqa yangemuva.
   - {{ui:Ubukhulu bomugqa}}: {{ui:Ubukhulu bomugqa wayo}} kugcina ubukhulu bento noma besendlalelo; inani liphrinta yonke imigqa yalowo mbala ngalobo bubanzi.
   - {{ui:Uhlobo lomugqa}}: phrinta yonke imigqa yalowo mbala ngohlobo olunye lomugqa (isibonelo konke okubomvu okuqhephuzelayo); {{ui:Sebenzisa uhlobo lomugqa wento}} kuwushiya.
   - {{ui:Ukuphela komugqa}} kanye {{ui:Ukuhlangana komugqa}}: izimbobo, amaskwele noma okuzungezile, nama-miter, ama-bevel noma okuzungezile (okuzungezile, njengakuqala, uma ungasethi lutho).
   - {{ui:Isitayela sokugcwalisa}}: ukugcwaliswa okuqinile kombala walowo kuphrinta njengemigqa yokufiphaza (amabha aqondile noma athambekile, amadayagonali, okukhulu, amachashaza noma amachashaza amancane) esikhundleni sokuphrinta okugcwele, kwamaphrinta namapulani lapho i-ink eqinile inzima kakhulu.
   Ithebula ngokwalo linezilungiselelo ezimbili ezengeziwe: {{ui:Ububanzi bemigqa}}, ububanzi ethebula elabeni (umugqa ophrintiwe uya ebubanzini obuseduze kakhulu kwalobu), kanye {{ui:Isikali sohlobo lomugqa}}, isisekeli samadashi walo nawo wonke amahlobo omugqa angewona ama-ISO.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Kwenziwe}}. Ithebula ligcinwa emdwebeni, futhi ishidi (noma imodeli) manje liyaphrinta ngalo. {{ui:Khansela}} ilahla izinguquko.

Umbala onepeni wawo ubonisa ichashaza engxenyeni yawo engakwesobunxele, nechashaza ngaphansi uma usetha ubukhulu bomugqa. Into enombala weqiniso (hhayi enye yama-255) isebenzisa peni yombala ojwayelekile oseduze kakhulu.

Umdwebo ugcina amathebula alo nokukhetha kweshidi ngalinye lapho ulondoloza njenge-DWG (2000 kuya ku-2018) noma i-DXF (R12 noma 2000), futhi uvula nawo. Ezinye izinhlelo zibona igama kukhasi lokulungiselela leshidi (`office.ctb`), hhayi ithebula uqobo. Umdwebo ovela kwenye inhlelo ogama ithebula elifana ne-`monochrome.ctb` ushicilela ngethebula elakhelwe ngaphakathi lalelo gama; kwelinye igama, ingxoxo iyatshela ukuthi umdwebo awunalo, futhi ushicilela imibala yomdwebo kuze kube yilapho ukhetha ithebula noma wenze elinalelo gama.

Ukuze usebenzise ithebula kolunye umdwebo, chofoza {{ui:Thumela}} ku-{{ui:Amathebula esitayela sokuphrinta}} (igcina `office.plotstyle.json`), bese {{ui:Ngenisa…}} kumdwebo omunye. {{ui:Ngenisa…}} ithatha nefayela le-`.ctb` kwenye inhlelo: lifundwa kusuka encazelweni eshicilelwe yalolo hlobo futhi **alikahlolwa ngefayela langempela**. Umbala, ukuhlanganisa, nobubanzi bomugqa ngamunye kuyafundwa; igreyi, izinombolo zepeni, amahlobo omugqa, izimbobo nokuhlangana kwemigqa kanye nezitayela zokugcwalisa akufundwa. Ifayela elinegama (`.stb`) liyenqatshwa. Qhathanisa ukuqala nalokho olulindele, futhi sithumelele ifayela uma kungalungile.

## Phrinta

**Kusiphequluli**, chofoza {{ui:Phrinta…}}. I-CadCraft yenza i-PDF bese ivula ingxoxo yokuphrinta yesiphequluli kuyo. Khetha "Actual size" noma 100% (hhayi "Fit") lapho ukugcina isikali. Uma isiphequluli singaphrinti kulelikhasi, i-PDF ivula ethebhini entsha: yiphrinte kuleyo ngo-{{key:Mod+P}}.

Ungachofoza futhi {{ui:Vula ukuze uphrinte}}: i-PDF ivula ethebhini entsha yesiphequluli, futhi uyiphrinta kuleyo.

:::safari
I-Safari ingabonisa i-PDF futhi ilinde ukuthi usebenzise **File**, bese **Print**.
:::

Uma isiphequluli sivimbela ithebhu entsha, i-CadCraft iyilanda i-PDF esikhundleni salokho futhi itshela lokho emlandweni. Vumela ama-pop-up kulesi sayithi ukuze uyivule ethebhini ngesikhathi esilandelayo.

**Ku-CadCraft Studio**, ingxoxo {{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}} ibala amaphrinta ekhompyutha. Khetha {{ui:Iphrinta}}, {{ui:Iphepha esiphrintini}} nenani {{ui:Izikhopi}}, bese uchofoza {{ui:Phrinta…}}. {{ui:Same as the plot}} ikhetha iphepha lephrinta elifana nosayizi weshidi (A3 weshidi le-A3); uma iphrinta ingenalo, isebenzisa iphepha layo bese ilinganisa leshidi kulo. Ku-Windows ishidi lithunyelwa njengesithombe se-300 dpi, ngoba i-Windows ingaphrinti i-PDF ngaphandle kohlelo lwe-PDF; ku-Linux nasku-macOS i-PDF uqobo iya kwisevisi yokuphrinta yesistimu. Akukho okuphuma ekhompyutheni.

## Amafayela esithombe ne-SVG

{{ui:Landa i-PNG}} igcina ishidi elishicilelwe njengesithombe ku-300 dpi (lincishiswa kuphela uma ishidi elikhulu kakhulu lingaba likhulu kunalokho isiphequluli singakwenza; umlando uyatshela lokho). {{ui:Landa i-SVG}} igcina njengomdwebo okwazi ukukhula ngamamilimitha, nemigqa nombhalo wokushicilela. Zombili zivela ekushicilelweni okufanayo njenge-PDF, nesitayela sokushicilela, ubukhulu bomugqa nendawo efanayo. Izithombe ezibekwe emdwebeni zisebhalwe ku-PDF kuphela; umlando uyatshela ukuthi zingaki ezishiywe.

## Izithombe

Izithombe ziphrinta ngokucaca kwazo, ngaphansi kwemigqa. {{ui:Izithombe}} engxoxweni ziyazikhawulela ku-600, 300 noma 150 dpi ukuze ifayela libe ncane. Imigqa, umbhalo kanye nezigcwalisi zihlala zicacile (i-vector), nokukhanya kuhlala kukukhanya. Bona [Izithombe emdwebeni](topic:pictures).

## Izilungiselelo ezikhunjulwayo

Ingxoxo ikhumbula iphepha lakho lokugcina, ukuma, isikali nobubanzi bomugqa kulesi siphequluli. Ithebula lesitayela sokuphrinta ligcinwa neshidi ngalinye emdwebeni; ishidi elingakakhethi liqala kwelokugcina oliliKhethile. Iwindi elikhethiwe ligcinwa kuze kube yilapho uvuselela ikhasi; ngemva kwalokho ingxoxo iqala ku-{{ui:Konke (imikhawulo)}}.

## Izinkinga ezivamile

- **I-PDF ayinanto noma izinkinobho zimpunga.** Akukho okushicilelwayo (imodeli engenalutho), noma {{ui:Iwindi…}} likhethiwe kanti iwindi alikakhethwa ("Khetha iwindi lokushicilela.").
- **Umbhalo ubonisa "?" ku-PDF.** Umbhalo wesiPolish, isiCzech, isiCyrillic, isiGreek nezinye izinhlelo zezinhlamvu zase-Europe uphrintwa njengoba unjalo: i-PDF ithwala izinhlamvu eziyidingayo (kusuka kufonti i-Liberation Sans, enobubanzi bofonti be-Helvetica), futhi umbhalo ungakhethwa futhi usheshwe. Umbhalo wesiShayina, isiJapanese nesiKorea nawo uphrintwa njengoba unjalo: uma umdwebo unawo, ukushicilelwa kulanda ifonti enkulu (cishe ama-MB ayi-10, kanye kuphela) futhi i-PDF ithwala izinhlamvu ezisetshenzisiwe kuphela, ngakho idinga uxhumo ekuqaleni (hhayi ikhophi engaxhunyiwe eyagcinwa ngaphambili). Umbhalo wesiHebheru uphrintwa ngefonti efanayo nombhalo wesiLatin, futhi umbhalo wesiArabhu, wesiPheresiya nowesiUrdu wenza ukushicilela kulanda enye ifonti (ngaphansi kwe-MB eyi-1, kanye kuphela); imigqa esuka kwesokunxele iyahlelwa futhi izinhlamvu zayo ziyahlanganiswa (bona [Umbhalo](topic:text)). Izinhlamvu ezingavamile ngaphandle kwalezo fonti, nezinhlelo ezidinga ukwakheka okuyinkimbinkimbi njenge-Hindi, ziphrintwa njenge-"?", futhi umlando uyazibala.
- **Yonke imigqa inobukhulu obufanayo.** Ubukhulu bayo bokuzenzakalela: nikeza izendlalelo noma izinto i-{{ui:Lineweight}} ephaneli yesokudla (bona [Ubukhulu bomugqa nohlobo lomugqa](topic:units-papers-scales#line-weights-and-linetypes)), noma setha ubukhulu bemigqa ngombala ku-[ithebula lesitayela sokuphrinta](topic:plot-pdf#plot-style-tables).
- **"(akukho kulo mdwebo)" eduze kwesitayela sokuphrinta.** Umdwebo uvela kwenye inhlelo futhi ubiza ithebula ongenalo. Khetha elinye lamathebula, noma wenze elinalelo gama.
- **Imigqa yokwakha iphrintiwe.** Setha isendlalelo sayo singaphrinti: chofoza inkinobho yayo yephrinta ephaneli {{ui:Izingqimba}}.
- **Iphrinta iphuma ngosayizi ongalungile.** Iphrinta ilinganise. Phrinta ngosayizi wangempela.
- **{{key:Mod+P}} ivule ingxoxo yokuphrinta yesiphequluli.** Ikhesa lalisebhokisini ephaneli yesokudla, noma kwakuvulekile enye ingxoxo. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} noma {{key:Esc}} ukuphuma kubhokisi, bese uzama futhi, noma chofoza {{ui:Dweba}}.
- **{{key:Mod+P}} ayenzanga lutho.** Umyalo uyasebenza. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} kuqala.
