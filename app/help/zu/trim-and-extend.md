## Okuzofunda

Ukuthi {{cmd:TRIM}} no-{{cmd:EXTEND}} asebenza kanjani, nezindlela ezisheshayo zokusebenzisa: donsa ngezingqephu eziningi ngesikhathi esisodwa, bamba {{key:Shift}} ukushintsha phakathi kokusika nokwelula, futhi ubone okuzonyamalala ngaphambi kokuchofoza.

## Sika isiqephu esisodwa

1. Dweba umugqa omude oqondile ophansi kanye nemigqa emibili emifushane eyiwela.
2. Thayipha `TR` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Umlando uthi `Current settings: Quick mode, edges: No extend`.
3. Phumula isikhombi emgqeni omude phakathi kwemigqa emibili emifushane. Lelo siqephu liba grey futhi lichashazwe: yilo elizonyamalala.
4. Chofoza. Isiqephu siyanyamalala.
5. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda umyalo.

Kwimodi esheshayo (okuzenzakalelayo) yonke into ebonakalayo iyimiphetho yokusika, ngakho awukaze ukhethe imiphetho kuqala. Chofoza isiqephu ofuna ukusinyamalalisa. Into ongaqanjwa yilutho iyasulwa yonke, futhi umlando uyakusho lokho.

## Sika izingqephu eziningi ngokudonsa okukodwa

1. Dweba umugqa omude ophansi kanye nemigqa emifushane engu-ishumi eyiwela ngokuqondile.
2. Thayipha `TR` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Cindezela inkinobho yembewu ngaphezulu komgqa omude, ngakwesobunxele komgqa wokuqala omile, bese udonsa uwele kuzo zonke ezingu-ishumi phezulu komgqa omude.

Ngenkathi udonsa, indlela ibonakala njengomugqa onamachashaza, futhi yonke isiqephu eliwela lapho lishintsha libe grey. Khipha: zonke izingqephu eziyishumi eziphezulu zisikwa ngesinyathelo esisodwa. Thayipha `U` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze ubuyisele zonke ngesikhathi esisodwa.

:::tip
Ungachofoza nezindawo ezimbili ezingenalutho: umugqa phakathi kwazo usebenza njengendlela edonsiwe. Thayipha `F` (Fence) ukuze uchofoze uchungechunge lwamaphuzu esikhundleni salokho, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
:::

:::touch
Donsa umunwe ngendlela efanayo. Ukuthinta isiqephu kukusika.
:::

## Yelula esikhundleni, ngo-Shift

1. Dweba umugqa oma ngokuqondile, nemigqa emihlanu emifushane ngakwesobunxele kwawo emile phambi kwawo.
2. Thayipha `TR` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Bamba {{key:Shift}}. Phumula isikhombi emgqeni omfushane: ingxenye ayoyikhulisa ngayo ibonakala igrey futhi ichashazwe.
4. Usabamba {{key:Shift}}, donsa phansi kuzo zonke ezinhlanu ezimfushane.

Zonke ezinhlanu manje ziyafika emgqeni oqondile. Ku-{{cmd:EXTEND}} (`EX`) {{key:Shift}} usebenza ngendlela ephambene: ukuchofoza kuyelula, ukuchofoza nge-{{key:Shift}}+ kuyasika. I-Extend ikhulisa ukuphela okuseduze nalapho uchofoza khona noma lapho indlela iqamba khona.

## Sika ngaphakathi kwewindi

1. Thayipha `TR` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Thayipha `C` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Chofoza amakhoneli amabili azungeze izingqephu ezizosuswa.

Yonke isiqephu esingaphakathi kewindi ngokungenani ngengxenye siyasikwa.

Ukuthola iwindi ngokudonsa nje, ngaphandle kokuthayipha `C`: {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:CadCraft}} › {{ui:Ukudonsa ukusika nokwelula}} › {{ui:Iwindi}}. Bese ucindezela endaweni engenalutho bese udonsa ukuze udwebe ibhokisi, futhi yonke isiqephu lapho ithinta sisikwa (noma siyelulwa, ku-{{cmd:EXTEND}}, noma nge-{{key:Shift}}). {{ui:Indlela}} (okokuqala) ibuyisela indlela yesandla. Umugqa wezilungiselelo ekuqaleni kwe-TRIM usho ukuthi yiyiphi esebenzayo.

## Khetha imiphetho yokusika (imodi ejwayelekile)

Ngezinye izikhathi umugqa oyodwa kuphela okufanele usike, njengobuso bodonga olunemigqa eminingi eyiwela.

1. Thayipha `TR` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Thayipha `O` (imodi) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese uthayipha `S` (ejwayelekile) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Ku-`Select cutting edges` chofoza imigqa yemiphetho (noma donsa iwindi), bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ukucindezela {{key:Enter}} kungakhethiwe lutho kwenza yonke into ibe yimiphetho.
4. Chofoza noma donsa ngezingqephu ozosuka.

Imiphetho ekhethiwe kuphela yisika, futhi into engawelwa yimiphetho ayishintshwa. Imodi igcinwa kuze kuvalwe ikhasi; thayipha `O`, bese `Q` ukubuyela emodini esheshayo. {{cmd:TRIMEXTENDMODE}} iyisetha ngaphambi kokuqala, njengokuguquguquka okunalegama elifanayo kwezinye izindawo: `0` ejwayelekile, `1` esheshayo. `T` (cuTting edges) ikhetha imiphetho emisha noma nini; ku-{{cmd:EXTEND}} kuyi-`B` (Boundary edges).

## Imiphetho ezoqamba

1. Thayipha `TR` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Thayipha `E` (Edge) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese `E` (Extend) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Manje umugqa wokusika noma i-arc emile esefushane ubalwa sengathi uyaqhubeka: umugqa omfushane usika lapho bekuyoqamba uma ubude. Thayipha `E`, bese `N` (Ayelulwa) ukuze ubuyele emuva. Lokhu kufana ne-EDGEMODE 1 no-0.

## Sula ngaphandle kokuphuma

Thayipha `R` (eRase) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, khetha izinto ozosula, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. I-TRIM iyaqhubeka.

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Umugqa wonke unyamalele.** Kwimodi esheshayo into engawelwa yilutho iyasulwa. Thayipha `U` ukuze uyibuyisele, noma shintsha imodi ibe ejwayelekile (`O`, `S`).
- **"Akukho okusikwayo lapho: akukho mphetho ekhethiwe eqamba lesi sinto."** Imodi ejwayelekile isika kuphela emiphethweni okhethile. Thayipha `T` ukuze uyikhethe futhi.
- **"Akukho mkhawulo kuleyo ndlela."** I-Extend ayithola lutho phambili. Chofoza eduze kwaleso siphetho okufanele sikhule, noma vula `E` (Edge, Extend) kumikhawulo emile esefushane.
- **Ukudonsa kudwebe isisongo esikhundleni.** Ukudonsa kusika kuphela ngenkathi i-TRIM noma i-EXTEND icela into. Lapho umyalo ungasebenzi, ukudonsa kukhetha (bheka [Ukukhetha ngesisongo](topic:lasso-selection)).
- **Imigqa, ama-arc, izindilinga nama-polyline kuphela ongasika.** Qala uhlukanise ibhlokhi ukuze usike imigqa yayo.
