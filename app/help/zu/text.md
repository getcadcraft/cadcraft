## Okuzokwenza

Amalebula amagumbi amabili, amiswe maphakathi emagumbini awo, inothi elihlanganisa ububanzi bawo, bese kulungiswa elinye lamalebula.

## Ukuphakama kombhalo nesikali sodwebo

Ukuphakama kombhalo kungokwamayunithi wodwebo. Kudwebo olusemamilimitheni olu zobhalwa ku-1:50, umbhalo ophakeme ngu-2.5 mm ephepheni kumele ube ngu-2.5 × 50 = 125 amayunithi ephakeme kwimodeli. Kwithebhu yeshidi odweba ngayo ngamayunithi ephepha, ngakho u-2.5 lapho ungu-2.5 mm ephepheni.

## Beka ilebula elimaphakathi

1. Thayipha `DT` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} (noma uchofoze {{ui:Umbhalo}} ku-toolbar). Umyalo ubonisa: `Specify start point of text [Justify]:`.
2. Thayipha `J` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Ku-`Enter an option [Left/Center/Right/Middle/TL/MC] <L>:` thayipha `MC` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
4. Ku-`Specify insertion point of text` chofoza maphakathi egumbi.
5. Ku-`Specify height` thayipha `250` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
6. Ku-`Specify rotation angle of text <0>` cindezela {{key:Enter}}.
7. Ku-`Enter text` thayipha `KITCHEN` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Ilebula liyavela, limiswe maphakathi naphuzu olichofoze.

8. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ku-`Enter text` engenalutho ukuze uphethe umyalo.

## Bhala inothi elihlanganisayo

Umbhalo wesigaba ({{cmd:MTEXT}}) uhlanganisa ububanzi bawo futhi uthayipha ngqo kudwebo.

1. Thayipha `T` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma uchofoze {{ui:Isigaba}} ku-toolbar.
2. Ku-`Specify first corner`, chofoza lapho ikhoneli elingakwesobunxele ngaphezulu kwenothi kuzohlala khona.
3. Ukuze ushintshe ukuphakama kuqala, thayipha `H`, cindezela {{key:Enter}}, thayipha `250` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
4. Ku-`Specify opposite corner [Height/Justify/Line spacing/Rotation/Width]`, chofoza ngakwesokudla ekhoneleni lokuqala. Ibanga eliwela ububanzi bombhalo ozogoqa kuwo; ukuthi uchofoza kude kangakanani phansi akukhawulelwa.
5. Ibhokisi livula phezu kodwebo ikhesa lingaphakathi kulo. Thayipha `ALL DIMENSIONS ARE IN MILLIMETERS UNLESS NOTED`. Amagama agoqa ngobubanzi njengoba uthayipha.
6. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqala isigaba esisha bese uthayipha `CHECK ON SITE`.
7. Cindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}}, chofoza {{ui:OK}}, noma uchofoze noma yikuphi ngaphandle kwebhokisi. Inothi libe yinto eyodwa.

Ibha ephezu kwebhokisi ishintsha {{ui:Ukuphakama}}, {{ui:Ukuhlukaniswa kwemigqa}} (1 kujwayelekile, 1.5 kuyisigamu esengeziwe), {{ui:Ububanzi}} (0 = akugoqi) no-{{ui:Linganisa}} ngenkathi uthayipha. {{key:Esc}} noma {{ui:Khansela}} livala ngaphandle kokungeza lutho.

Izinketho ekhoneleni lesibili: Ukuphakama (`H`), Linganisa (`J`: `TL` ikhoneli elingakwesobunxele ngasenhla … `BR` ikhoneli elingakwesokudla ngaphansi, ikhoneli noma uhlangothi lebhokisi olihlala lisesimeni), Ukuhlukaniswa kwemigqa (`L`), Ukujikeleza (`R`), Ububanzi (`W`) obuthayiphwa esikhundleni sekhoneli lesibili.

:::note
Ngenkathi ibhokisi livulekile, {{key:Space}} uthayipha isikhala futhi {{key:Enter}} uqala isigaba esisha. I-DWG elondoloziwe igcina umbhalo wesigaba njenge-MTEXT; i-DXF R12 ayinawo umbhalo wesigaba, ngakho umugqa ngamunye ulondolozwa njengombhalo wawo.
:::

### Imigqa eminingi eyodwa esikhundleni

{{cmd:TEXT}} (`DT`) ibuza umugqa owodwa ngesikhathi: ngamunye {{key:Enter}} uqala umugqa olandelayo, ophansi ngokuphindwe ka-1.667 kokuphakama kombhalo, futhi umugqa ngamunye uyinto yawo. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} kumugqa ongenalutho ukuze uqede.

## Hlela umbhalo

1. Chofoza kabili into yombhalo. Ivula kubhokisi elifanayo nombhalo wesigaba omusha, ikhesa liphethe ekugcineni.
2. Shintsha amagama.
3. Cindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}} noma uchofoze ngaphandle kwebhokisi. Umbhalo kudwebo uyashintsha; {{key:Esc}} uwushiya njengoba wawunjalo.

{{cmd:TEXTEDIT}} (`ED`) yenza okufanayo: khetha umbhalo owodwa ngemuva kwelinye, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} lapho sekuphelile.

Khetha umbhalo ukuze uwushintshe kunalokho ephaneli esekhoneni: {{ui:Umbhalo}}, {{ui:Ukuphakama}}, {{ui:Ububanzi bokugoqa}} (0 = akugoqi), {{ui:Ukuhlukaniswa kwemigqa}}, {{ui:Ukujikeleza}}, isimo se-X ne-Y no-{{ui:Linganisa}}. Uhlu lwe-{{ui:Linganisa}} luphinde lunikeze i-Ngezansi kwesobunxele, esetshenziswa umbhalo wezinye izinhlelo ngezinye izikhathi.

## Ukwakheka ngaphakathi kombhalo wesigaba ovela ezinhlelweni ezinye

Umbhalo wesigaba ku-DWG noma ku-DXF ungaba nemiyalo ngaphakathi kwawo: imibala ngamagama, okugqamile nokuthambekile, umugqa ongaphansi, umugqa ongaphezulu nomugqa ophakathi, izingxenye ezibekwe phezu kwenye njengo-1/2, nokuphakama okuhlukile kwegama. I-CadCraft igcina lezo miyalo, iyidwebe (esikrinini nasePDF eshicilelwe) futhi iyilondolozele njengoba injalo.

Uma uhlela umbhalo onjalo (chofoza kabili, {{cmd:TEXTEDIT}}, insimu {{ui:Umbhalo}} ephaneli esekhoneni, noma {{cmd:FIND}} ne-Replace), uhlela amagama awo alula. Igama olithayipha lingena kusegimenti eliseduze nalo futhi lithatha umbala nesitayela sawo; eminye imiyalo ihlala lapho ibikhona. Uma uphinda uthayipha izinombolo zesigaba esibekwe phezu kwenye, leso sigaba siba umbhalo ovamile. Ukusetha umbhalo wonke ngensimu ka-Text kugcina imiyalo kuphela lapho kungaziwa khona ukuthi yimaphi amagama ahlala.

Amagama wamafonti (`\F`) ayagcinwa futhi ayalondolozwa, kodwa konke kudwetshwa ngefonti eyodwa efana ne-Helvetica. Ayikho i-toolbar yokuthayipha imiyalo okwamanje.

## Ukukhomba into: imigqa eminingi eqondisayo

{{ui:Umugqa oqondisayo}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Chaza}} ({{cmd:MLEADER}}, `MLD`) idweba umcibisholo, umugqa oqondisayo, ukufika okufushane nenothi, njengento eyodwa.

1. Chofoza {{ui:Umugqa oqondisayo}}. Ku-`Specify leader arrowhead location`, chofoza lapho inothi limayelana khona.
2. Ku-`Specify leader landing location`, chofoza lapho inothi liyokhona. Ukufika kuphendukela ohlangothini ochofoze kulo.
3. Thayipha inothi ebhokisini eliyovula, bese ucindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}}.

I-`A` ne-`L` embuzweni wokuqala zisetha usayizi womcibisholo nobude bokufika; ukuphakama kombhalo kuvela ku-{{ui:Ukuphakama kombhalo}} wezilinganiso ezintsha, kuphindwe ngo-{{ui:Isikali sonke}}. Chofoza kabili umugqa oqondisayo ukuze ushintshe amagama awo. Izibambi zihambisa umcibisholo nokufika; inothi lilandela ukufika. Ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}: {{ui:Umbhalo}}, {{ui:Ukuphakama}}, {{ui:Ububanzi bokugoqa}}, {{ui:Usayizi wekhanda lomcibisholo}} kanye {{ui:Ibanga lokufika}}.

:::note
Imigqa eqondisayo igcinwa njengemigqa eqondisayo yangempela ku-DWG. I-DXF ayinayo imigqa eqondisayo ku-R12 noma ku-2000, ngakho lapho zigcinwa njengemigqa, umcibisholo nombhalo wazo odwetshwayo.
:::

## Makha izinguquko ngomjikelo wokuqaphela

{{ui:Chaza}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Umjikelo wokuqaphela}} ({{cmd:REVCLOUD}}, `RC`) idweba ifu lama-arc ezungeze okushintshile. Umlando ukhombisa ubude be-arc nohlobo olizosetshenziswa.

- **Ka-rectangular** (okokuqala): chofoza amakhoneli amabili.
- `P` **Polygonal**: chofoza amakhoneli, {{key:Enter}} ivala ifu.
- `F` **Freehand**: chofoza lapho kuqala khona bese uhambisa isikhombi uzungeze indawo ngaphandle kokuchofoza; i-arc yengezwa njalo ngobude be-arc. Chofoza noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uyivale.
- `O` **Object**: khetha i-polyline evaliwe, indilinga noma i-ellipse (noma umugqa, i-arc noma i-polyline evulekile); ibe ifu. `Reverse direction?` `Y` ijikisa ama-arc angaphakathi.
- `A` **Arc length**: ubude ngayinye ye-arc. Khetha obufanele isikali sokushicilela.

Ifu liyi-polyline evaliwe yama-arc, ngakho ilondolozeka kuyo yonke ifomethi.

## Thola futhi ushintshe amagama

{{ui:Hlela}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Thola futhi ushintshe}} ({{cmd:FIND}}) ihlola umbhalo, amanani wezici zebhlokhi nombhalo wezilinganiso ethebhini elivulekile (noma kuphela ezintweni ezikhethiwe).

1. Thayipha amagama ku-{{ui:Thola}}. Uhlu lukhombisa ukuthi atholakala kuphi nenani lawo.
2. Khetha {{ui:Fanisa ukukhulu nokuncane}}, {{ui:Amagama aphelele kuphela}} noma {{ui:Sebenzisa izimpawu (* no-?)}} uma udinga, beseususa okungafuneki ukubheka kukho.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Khetha okutholakele}} ukukhetha lezo zinto, noma uthayiphe amagama amasha ku-{{ui:Shintsha ngalokhu}} (uhlu lukhombisa ukuthi ngamunye uba yini) bese uchofoza {{ui:Shintsha konke}}. Ukucindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} kanye kuhlehlisa konke ukushintshwa.

Inani elilinganisiwe lesilinganiso akusona umbhalo, ngakho alikaze lishintshwe: ku-`<> TYP.`, kuphela i-`TYP.` ingashintshwa.

## Fihla imigqa ngemuva kwelebula

1. Khetha imibhalo, thayipha {{cmd:TEXTMASK}} bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. `B` upenda ingemuva lodwebo ngemuva kwawo; `C` icela inombolo yombala. `O` isusa imaski.
3. `Border offset factor`: {{key:Enter}} igcina i-1.5, uhhafu wokuphakama kombhalo ozungeze umbhalo.

Imaski imboza imigqa engaphansi kombhalo, esikrinini nalapho ushicilela. I-DWG 2004 noma ngaphezulu ne-DXF 2000 iyayigcina; ifomethi ye-DWG 2000 ne-DXF R12 azikwazi, futhi ukulondoloza kuyakusho lokho.

## Ifonti

Konke umbhalo udwetshwa ngefonti efana ne-Helvetica, esikrinini nasePDF. Umbhalo wodwebo ovulekile ugcina amagama, ukuphakama nesimo sawo, kodwa hhayi ifonti yawo yokuqala (igama lefonti ngaphakathi kombhalo wesigaba ligcinwa kufayela, bheka lapho ngenhla).

Kuma-PDF ashicilelwe, umbhalo wanoma yisiphi isikripthi saseYurophu, isiSirili noma isiGreek uphrinta njengoba unjalo (i-PDF iphethe izinhlamvu ezidingekayo, futhi usakwazi ukukhetha nokusesha umbhalo). Umbhalo wesiShayina, wesiJapane nowesiKorea nawo uphrinta njengoba unjalo (ukushicilela kuthatha ifonti enkulu ngokuqala lapho idingeka). Umbhalo we-Arabic, we-Persian, we-Urdu ne-Hebrew nawo uphrinta njengoba unjalo (bheka isigaba esilandelayo). Izinhlamvu ezingenaso isinhlamvu sefonti ziphrintwa njenge-"?", futhi umlando ukhombisa ukuthi zingaki.

## Umbhalo wesokudla kuya kwesobunxele: isiArabhu, isiPheshiya, isiUrdu, isiHebheru

Thayipha umbhalo njengoba ngabe ungakwenza noma yiphi enye indawo: {{cmd:TEXT}} ne-{{cmd:MTEXT}} zidweba umugqa ngamunye ngokulandelana kokufunda. Umugqa okuthi uhlamvu lwawo lokuqala lube yisiArabhu noma isiHebheru ufundeka kusuka kwesokudla kuya kwesobunxele; izinombolo namagama aseLatin angaphakathi kuwo agcina ukulandelana kwawo kusuka kwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla, futhi izinhlamvu zesiArabhu ziyahlanganiswa. Umugqa oqala ngohlamvu lwaseLatin uhlala usuka kwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla namagama asuka kwesokudla kuya kwesobunxele ahlala endaweni yawo ngaphakathi kwawo. Isigaba ngasinye sombhalo wemigqa eminingi sinendlela yaso, futhi isigaba esihlanganisiwe siyayigcina kuwo wonke umugqa.

Umbhalo ugcinwa njengoba uwuthayipha, ngakho amafayela e-DWG ne-DXF awushintshanisi nezinye izinhlelo unganqunywanga. Indawo yokuhlanganisa nokulinganisa ({{ui:Kwesobunxele}}, {{ui:Isikhungo}}, {{ui:Kwesokudla}}) isho uhlangothi olufanayo nakunoma yimuphi umbhalo: inothi elisuka kwesokudla kuya kwesobunxele elifuna ukuncika ekugcineni kwalo kwesokudla lidinga {{ui:Kwesokudla}}.

Esikrinini izinhlamvu zivela kumafonti esistimu yakho. KuPDF eshicilelwe, isiHebheru sisebenzisa ifonti efanayo efakiwe njengombhalo waseLatin; umbhalo wesiArabhu wenza ukuthi ukushicilela kulande enye ifonti (ngaphansi kwe-1 MB, kanye), futhi i-PDF yonke ibe ishicilelwa ngaleyo fonti. Leyo fonti ayinayo isimo sayo sezinhlamvu ezintathu zesiUrdu (heh goal, yeh barree nechashazi lesiUrdu): ziphrintwa ngesimo sohlamvu lwesiArabhu olusedlule eduze kakhulu. Udwebo olunombhalo wesiArabhu nowesiShayina, wesiJapane noma wesiKorea luphrinta enye yezimbili njenge-"?", ngoba ukushicilela kuthwala ifonti eyodwa. Umbhalo kufonti ye-SHX elayishiwe uyahlelwa futhi uyahlanganiswa uma ifonti iphethe izinhlamvu endaweni yazo ye-Unicode; kumafonti amadala ezinhlamvu zawo sezinesimo, sebenzisa {{ui:Phendula}} ku-{{cmd:SHXFONTS}} njengakuqala.

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Umbhalo mncane kakhulu ukuthi ubonakale.** Ukuphakama kuseyamayunithi wodwebo. Kudwebo olukhulu, sebenzisa ukuphakama okukhulu (bheka lapho ngenhla), noma sondeza.
- **Umyalo uphele ngemuva komugqa owodwa.** Ucindezele {{key:Enter}} kumugqa ongenalutho ku-{{cmd:TEXT}}. Qala kabusha, noma sebenzisa umbhalo wesigaba (`T`), lapho {{key:Enter}} eqala isigaba esisha.
- **`T` ivule ibhokisi esikhundleni sokubuza umugqa.** Njenge-AutoCAD, `T` ingumbhalo wesigaba ({{cmd:MTEXT}}); umbhalo womugqa owodwa ngu-`DT`.
- **Umbhalo uhlanganisa kwenye indawo ku-AutoCAD.** I-CadCraft ihlanganisa ngobubanzi be-Helvetica; enye ifonti ingalifaka kangcono noma ibe kabi igama emugqeni.
- **Umbhalo kwikhophi elibonakala ngemuva.** Ubekwa ubukiwe kodwa uhlala ufundeka kusuka kwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla.
