## Okuzokwenza

Bhala inothi, yenza igama elilodwa libe lijiyile futhi libe bomvu, shintsha usayizi walo, engeze uphawu lwedigri, bese uqeda.

## Izinyathelo

1. Thayipha {{cmd:MTEXT}} bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma chofoza kabili umbhalo osuvele, noma thayipha {{cmd:TEXTEDIT}} bese uyikhetha. Ibhokisi lombhalo liyavuleka linebha yokwenza ifomethi phezulu kwalo.
2. Bhala inothi lakho. {{key:Enter}} iqala isigaba esisha.
3. Khetha igama nge-imawuse noma nge-{{key:Shift+Right}}.
4. Ebhalweni yokwenza ifomethi sebenzisa {{ui:Font}} ukukhetha ifonti, {{ui:Size}} ukushintsha usayizi wokukhethiwe, izinkinobho {{ui:Bold}}, {{ui:Italic}}, {{ui:Underline}} kanye {{ui:Strike-through}}, kanye {{ui:Colour}} ukukhetha umbala. Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, {{ui:Size}} ishintsha wonke umbhalo.
5. Sebenzisa {{ui:Align left}}, {{ui:Centre}} noma {{ui:Align right}} ukulinganisa.
6. Ukuthayipha uphawu, beka ikhesa lapho lidinga khona bese uchofoza {{ui:Degree sign}}, {{ui:Plus-minus sign}} noma {{ui:Diameter sign}}.
7. Chofoza {{ui:OK}} noma cindezela {{key:Ctrl+Enter}} ukulondoloza umbhalo. {{ui:Cancel}} noma {{key:Esc}} ushiya umbhalo njengoba wawunjalo.

## Amanothi

- Ibhokisi linayo futhi {{ui:Height}}, {{ui:Line spacing}}, {{ui:Width}} (0 = akukho ukugoqa) kanye {{ui:Justify}}.
- Ifomethi ibhalwa njengamakhodiombhalo wesigaba kanye namagama ajwayelekile, ngakho ukuthola, ukubamba, DXF kanye DWG kuyaqhubeka nokusebenza. Ukusetha ifonti yazo zonke izinothi, sebenzisa [isitayela sombhalo](topic:text-styles).
- Akukho okwamabullet noma uhlu olunamanani, ithuluzi lamathebhu noma izigaba ezilinganisiwe okwamanje.
- Ama-PDF ashicilelwe asetha umbhalo nge-Helvetica.
