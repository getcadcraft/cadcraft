## Okuzokufunda

Ukukhetha izinto ngokuzungeza ngomugqa, nokuthi ungakubuyisa kanjani unxantathu uma ufuna.

## Dweba i-lasso

1. Dweba imigqa nezindilinga ezimbalwa eduze kwenye.
2. Ngaphandle kokuthi kukhona umyalo oqhubeka, cindezela inkinobho yemouse endaweni engenalutho, bese udonsa uzungeze ezinye zazo, uhamba **ngakwesokudla** kuqala.
3. Khipha.

Izinto ezisemngceleni ophelele kuphela zikhethwa. Umugqa udwetshwa uqinile ngesikhathi udonsa: lona i-lasso yewindi.

## Iwindi, ukuwela nefensi

Izinto ozithola zincike ekunyakazeni kokuqala kokudonsa kwakho, nakwe-{{key:Space}}:

| Udonsa | I-lasso | Ikhetha |
|---|---|---|
| Ngakwesokudla kuqala | Iwindi (umugqa oqinile) | Izinto **ezisemngceleni ophelele** kuphela |
| Ngakwesobunxele kuqala | Ukuwela (oyidashi, oluhlaza) | Izinto **ezisemngceleni** **nezinto umugqa uzithinta** |
| Cindezela {{key:Space}} ngesikhathi udonsa | Iwindi, bese ukuwela, bese ifensi | Ifensi (indlela eyidashi, engavaliwe): izinto indlela edlula kuzo kuphela |

1. Cindezela endaweni engenalutho bese udonsa uzungeze ezinye izinto, uhamba **ngakwesobunxele** kuqala. Konke umugqa ukuthintayo nakho kuyakhethwa.
2. Cindezela {{key:Esc}}.
3. Cindezela bese udonsa futhi; uqhubeka ubamba inkinobho, cindezela {{key:Space}} kanye. Umugqa uguquka ube ukuwela; cindezela {{key:Space}} futhi ube yifensi.

{{key:Shift}} uma ukhipha kuthatha izinto ekukhethweni kunalokho (ne-"Ukuchofoza izinto: Engeza" ezilungiselelweni).

## Ngaphakathi komyalo

I-lasso isebenza noma kuphi lapho i-CadCraft icela `Khetha izinto`: yidwebe, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda. {{cmd:STRETCH}} isebenzisa i-lasso yokuwela njengewindi yayo, ngakho ungelula ingxenye enesimo se-L yomdwebo ngokuhamba kunye.

{{cmd:TRIM}} no-{{cmd:EXTEND}} basebenzisa ukudonsa ngendlela ehlukile: lapho inqamula (noma yelula) yonke ingxenye indlela edlula kuyo. Bheka [Sika bese welula](topic:trim-and-extend).

## Irektangile

Ukuchofoza endaweni engenalutho, ukunyakaza, nokuchofoza kwesibili kudweba njalo iwindi lonxantathu (ngakwesobunxele kuya ngakwesokudla) noma ukuwela (ngakwesokudla kuya ngakwesobunxele).

Ukuze udonsa udwebe unxantathu futhi, vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:CadCraft}} › {{ui:Ukudonsa ukuze ukhethe}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Unxantathu}}, noma thayipha {{cmd:LASSOMODE}}, bese `0`. Thayipha {{cmd:LASSOMODE}}, bese `1` ye-lasso. Ukukhetha kugcinwa kulesi siphequluli.

:::touch
Donsa ngomunwe noma ngepeni ngendlela efanayo. Ukuthinta okulodwa kusakhetha into eyodwa.
:::

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Akukho okukhethiwe.** I-lasso kwakuyiwindi futhi akukho okwakusemngceleni ophelele. Qala ukudonsa ngakwesobunxele, noma cindezela {{key:Space}} ngesikhathi udonsa.
- **Ukudonsa kuhambise isibambo.** Ukucindezela isibambo sento ekhethiwe kuhambisa isibambo. Qala i-lasso endaweni engenalutho.
- **I-Space iqedile umyalo.** {{key:Space}}ishintsha i-lasso kuphela ngenkathi inkinobho ibanjiwe; ngaphandle kwalokho iyi-{{key:Enter}}.
