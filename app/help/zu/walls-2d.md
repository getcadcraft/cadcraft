Izindonga lapha zinto zokudweba ze-2D. Azenzi imodeli ye-3D, ngakho ipulani ihlala ilula njengomdwebo womugqa. Ngemodeli yesakhiwo, sebenzisa BimCraft.

## Dweba izindonga

Thayipha `WALL` (`WA`), noma khetha {{ui:Udonga}} kwimenyu {{ui:Okuningi}} yeqembu {{ui:Dweba}}, bese uchofoza amaphoyinti njengoba wenza nge-polyline. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda, noma uthayipha `C` ukuvala ikamelo.

- `T` isetha ukujula.
- `J` ichaza okulandelwa yizinkinobho zakho: isikhungo sodonga, noma uhlangothi lwesobunxele noma lwesokudla.
- `F` isetha ukugcwaliswa: lutho, okumile noma ukufiphaza.

Amakhona ahlanganiswa ngokwawo njenge-mitre. Udonga oluphela ngaphakathi kobukhulu bodonga olunye luhlanganiswa njenge-T ehlanzekile, nezindonga ezidlula zona ziyavulana. Izixhumo zigcinwa kanye nezindonga. Ngamarektangili, izinxantathu, ama-arc, ukuhlukanisa, ukukhiya nokunye, bona "Izimo zodonga, ukusikwa, ukuphambanisa nokukhiya kodonga be-2D".

## Engeza iminyango namawindi

Thayipha `DOOR` (`DR`) noma `WINDOW` (`WN`) bese uchofoza udonga. Umnyango uvulwa ohlangothini lodonga oluchofozile. `W` isetha ububanzi, `Y` uhlobo, `H` uhlangothi lwesibambo. Qhubeka uchofoza ukuze ubeke ezinye, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda. Iminyango ingaba yodwa, emibili, eshelelayo noma ingena evulekile. Amawindi angaba ahlala njalo, avulwa ngezigcawu noma ashelelayo.

## Shintsha wona

Khetha udonga ukuze ubone izibambi zawo:

- Izibambi zasekugcineni zelula udonga. Izindonga ezixhunyiwe kulo ziyalandela.
- Isibambi esiphakathi sihambisa udonga.
- Isibambi esisekuvulekeni siyishelelisa ngodongeni.
- Imicibisholo ijika isibambo somnyango nokugoba kwawo.

Properties ibonisa ukujula, ukugcwaliswa, nokuvuleka ngakunye: uhlobo, ububanzi, indawo, isibambo, ukugoba, kuboniswe kuvuliwe noma kuvaliwe, kanye no-{{ui:Khipha}}. `DELOPENING` ikhipha ukuvuleka okuseduze kwapho uchofoza khona.

`MOVE`, `ROTATE`, `MIRROR`, `SCALE`, `COPY` ne-`ERASE` zisebenza njengokujwayelekile. Iminyango namawindi ahamba nodonga lwabo, izindonga ezixhunyiwe zigcina izixhumo zazo, futhi i-Hlehlisa eyodwa iyahlehlisa konke.

## Kwezinye izinhlelo

Udonga ngalunyeulondolozwa njengebhulokhi evamile, ngakho ezinye izinhlelo ze-CAD ziyawubonisa kahle futhi zingawuhambisa, kodwa azikwazi ukuwuhlela njengodonga. `EXPLODE` iguqula udonga ube imigqa namaarc avamile, futhi awusaba udonga.
