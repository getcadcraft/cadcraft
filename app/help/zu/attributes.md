## Izici ziyini

Isici yinani elibizwa igama elihamba nereferensi yebhulokhi: inombolo yesinto yesiphawuli sempahla, igama lesiphawuli segumbi, izinga lomlilo lesivalo. Inchazo yebhulokhi itshela ukuthi iyiphi izici inazo (ngayinye inesiphawuli, **umyalezo**, **okuzenzakalelayo** nendawo, usayizi kanye ne-engeli yombhalo wayo). Yonke ireferensi yebhulokhi ithwala amanani ayo.

Ezinye izici **ziyongabonakali**: zigcina idatha, njengomenzi noma inombolo yomodeli, kodwa azidwetshwa ngakoze. Ezinye zi**okungaguquki**: zifana kuyo yonke ireferensi, zilungiswe yibhulokhi.

## Lapho izici zivela khona

- Amabhulokhi kumafayela e-DWG nase-DXF oyavulayo agcina izici zawo, namanani ayo ngayinye yereferensi.
- Amabhulokhi oyawalethayo nge-{{cmd:INSERT}} nenketho ye-{{ui:Ifayela}} agcina izici zawo. Bona [Faka kusuka kufayela noma kulabhulari](topic:insert-from-file-and-library).
- Izinto zelabhulari zifika njengamabhulokhi anezici: inombolo yesinto ebonakalayo nedatha yesinto, engabonakali.
- Amabhulokhi owenza wena: chaza izici nge-{{cmd:ATTDEF}} kuqala (ngezansi), bese uzikhetha kanye nezinye izinto lapho wenza ibhulokhi.

## Chaza izici zamabhulokhi akho

1. Dweba imigqa yebhulokhi, isibonelo isiphawuli segumbi.
2. Khetha {{ui:Amabhulokhi}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Chaza isici}}, noma thayipha `ATT` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Umlando ubonisa izimo (`Ongabonakali=Cha Okungaguquki=Cha Okusethiwe=Cha`). Thayipha `[Ongabonakali (I)]`, `[Okungaguquki (C)]` noma `[Okusethiwe (P)]` ukuze uguqule eyodwa, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} uma zilungile. Amanani angabonakali agcinwa kodwa awadwetshwa; ayodwa okungaguquki unani elifanayo kuyo yonke ireferensi; eyodwa esethiwe ayicelwa lapho ufaka ibhulokhi.
4. Thayipha isiphawuli (isibonelo `ROOM_NO`: amacala aphezulu, ngaphandle kwezikhala), umyalezo (`Inombolo yegumbi`) nenani lokuzenzakalelayo (`101`).
5. Chofoza lapho kuyophela khona inani, bese unikeza ukuphakama nokujikeleza. Isiphawuli siyabonakala emdwebeni.
6. Yenza ibhulokhi nge-{{cmd:BLOCK}}, ukhetha imigqa nezichazo zezici ndawonye. Ireferensi evala lezo izinto ithola amanani okuzenzakalelayo; i-{{cmd:INSERT}} ngayinye emisha iyawacela.

Isichazo sesici esikhethiwe sibonisa {{ui:Isiphawuli}}, {{ui:Umyalezo}}, {{ui:Okuzenzakalelayo}} nezimo ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}.

## Shintsha ibhulokhi: umhleli webhulokhi

{{ui:Amabhulokhi}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Hlela ibhulokhi}} ({{cmd:BEDIT}}, `BE`) ivula ibhulokhi iyodwa: thayipha igama layo (noma khetha ireferensi kuqala). Ibha phezulu ibonisa ukuthi ihlela iyiphi ibhulokhi. Dweba, susa futhi ushintshe izinto zayo ngemiyalo ojwayelekile, bese wengeza noma ushintshe izichazo zezici. Bese uchofoza {{ui:Londoloza ibhulokhi}} (noma uthayipha {{cmd:BCLOSE}} no `S`): yonke ireferensi ibonisa ushintsho, nokukodwa kwe-{{key:Mod+Z}} kuyahlehlisa konke. {{ui:Lahla izinguquko}} (`BCLOSE`, `D`) ishiya ibhulokhi njengoba kwakunjalo.

Lapho wengeza,ususa noma ushintsha izichazo zezici, ukulondoloza kuphinde kuhlele zonke izinkomba: amanani owathayipha ayagcinwa, isici esisha sithola inani lokuzenzakalelayo, isici esisusiwe siyasuswa, futhi isici ngasinye sibuyela endaweni echazwa yisichazo. Umlando utshela ukuthi zingaki izinkomba ezibuyekeziwe.

## Hlela ibhulokhi yesihloko lapho ikhona (REFEDIT)

Ibhulokhi yesihloko eshidini ivame ukuba phakathi kwebhulokhi. Ukuze ushintshe imigqa noma imibhalo yayo ngaphandle kokushiya ishidi:

1. Khetha ibhulokhi yesihloko, bese {{ui:Amabhulokhi}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Hlela endaweni yayo}} (noma thayipha {{cmd:REFEDIT}}).
2. Okuqukethwe kwayo kuba izinto ezivamile lapho zikhona; konke okunye kuyafiphala futhi akushintshwa.
3. Shintsha, susa noma dweba: izinto ezintsha zingene ebhulokhini.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza ibhulokhi}} ebhalweni eliphezulu (noma uthayipha {{cmd:REFCLOSE}} no `S`). Yonke ireferensi ibonisa ushintsho, nokukodwa kwe-{{key:Mod+Z}} kuhlehlisa lonke uhlelo. {{ui:Lahla}} ishiya ibhulokhi njengoba yayinjalo.

Isithombe esixhunyiwe, ibhulokhi eguquguqukayo, noma ireferensi eskalwe ngokuhlukile ngokuthwala nangokuphakama ayikwazi ukuhlelwa endaweni yayo: sebenzisa {{cmd:BEDIT}} (noma vula umdwebo oxhunyiwe).

:::note
Londoloza noma lahla kumhleli webhulokhi ngaphambi kokulondoloza umdwebo: ifayela elilondolozwe lapho umhleli evulekile linebhulokhi njengoba yayinjalo ngaphambili.
:::

## Gcwalisa lapho ufaka ibhulokhi

1. Thayipha `I` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, thayipha igama lebhulokhi bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Chofoza lapho kuyophela khona.
3. I-CadCraft icela inani ngalinye, isibonelo `Faka inombolo yesinto (ITEM) <>`. Thayipha inani bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukugcina okuzenzakalelayo okukhonjiswe phakathi kwamabhulaketi ama-engeli.

Amanani ebhulokhi ababeka njengasethiwe, nalawo angaguquki, awacelwa. Athatha okuzenzakalelayo kwawo.

## Bona futhi uguqule amanani

1. Chofoza ireferensi yebhulokhi.
2. Kuphaneli elingakwesokudla, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izici}}, isiphawuli ngasinye sinenani layo. Ezingabonakali zithi `ifihliwe`.
3. Chofoza inani, thayipha elisha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Umdwebo ubonisa inani elisha ngokushesha. Ushintsho ngalunye luyinyathelo elilodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyalihlehlisa.

**Umhleli wezici.** Phinda ushaye ngaphezulu kwereferensi enezici (noma {{cmd:EATTEDIT}}): ingxoxo {{ui:Hlela izici}} ibhala zonke iziphawuli ngomyalezo nenani lazo. Thayipha amanani amasha; chofoza umugqa ukuguqula {{ui:Ukuphakama}}, {{ui:Ukujikeleza}}, {{ui:Width factor}}, {{ui:Linganisa}}, {{ui:Ungqimba}}, {{ui:Umbala}} ne-{{ui:Ongabonakali}} zalesi sici kuphela kuleyo referensi.
{{ui:Sebenzisa}} igcina ingxoxo ivulekile; {{ui:KULUNGILE}} isebenzisa bese iyavala. Ngayinye {{ui:Sebenzisa}} noma {{ui:KULUNGILE}} iyinyathelo elilodwa lokuhlehlisa.

**Komugqa womyalo**, {{cmd:ATTEDIT}} icela inani ngalinye ngokulandelana: thayipha elisha, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukugcina elikhona.

## Shintsha izichazo zezici (BATTMAN, ATTSYNC)

{{ui:Amabhulokhi}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Umphathi wezici}} ({{cmd:BATTMAN}}) ibhala izici zebhulokhi: shintsha isiphawuli, umyalezo noma okuzenzakalelayo, khonza {{ui:Ongabonakali}}, {{ui:Okungaguquki}} noma {{ui:Okusethiwe ngaphambili}}, bese uzinyusa noma uzehlisa (odeni amanani abuzwa ngalo). Ngokuthi {{ui:Buyekeza izinkomba (amanani agcinwe)}} ikhethiwe, {{ui:Sebenzisa}} iphinde ihlele zonke izinkomba; isiphawuli esisha sigcina amanani aso. Ngayinye {{ui:Sebenzisa}} iyinyathelo elilodwa lokuhlehlisa.

{{cmd:ATTSYNC}} ihlela izinkomba ngezichazo zebhulokhi zazo ngesandla: `[Khetha (S)]` ikhethe ireferensi, `[Igama (N)]` iqamba ibhulokhi, `[Konke (A)]` yenza yonke ibhulokhi. Amanani agcinwa.

Izinkomba zezinkambu zento yelabhulari (inombolo yayo, inguqulo, ilayisense nokubhalwa kwayo) ziyabonakala kodwa azishintshwa, ukuze ukuhlola izibuyekezo kusebenze. Inombolo yayo nobuningi bayo ngokwakho ziyashintsheka.

## Zibale

Khetha izinkomba bese uthayipha `LI` (ye-{{cmd:LIST}}). Umlando ubonisa ngayinye isici njenge-`TAG = inani`, no-`(ongabonakali)` noma `(okungaguquki)` lapho kusebenza khona.

## Ukuhambisa, ukujikeleza nokuqothula

- {{cmd:MOVE}}, {{cmd:ROTATE}}, {{cmd:SCALE}} no {{cmd:MIRROR}} agcina amanani; umbhalo uhamba nebhulokhi futhi usalungele ukufundeka.
- {{cmd:EXPLODE}} iguqula izici ezibonakalayo ibe ngumbhalo ojwayelekile namanani awo. Ezingabonakali ziyalahlwa.

## Ukulondoloza

I-DWG elondoloziwe ne-DXF elondoloziwe zombili zigcina yonke isici: izichazo zebhulokhi ngayinye (nezingabonakali, ezingaguquki nezisethiwe ngaphambili) namanani ngayinye yereferensi. Ezinye izinhlelo ze-CAD zizifunda njengezici zebhulokhi ezivamile.

Ku-DXF R12, inani eliphezu kwezinhlamvu ezingu-255 linqunywa libe ngu-255, nomlando uyasho lokho.

## Izinkinga ezivamile

- **Inani alibonisi emdwebeni.** Lingabonakali, noma lingenalutho. Amanani angabonakali asabonakala ku-iphaneli elingakwesokudla.
- **Inani engithayipha lingene komugqa womyalo.** Chofoza ebhokisini lenani kuphaneli elingakwesokudla kuqala.
