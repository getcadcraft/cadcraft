## Okuzakufunda

Indlela yokubheka umdwebo owodwa ngamaphaneli amaningi ngesikhathi esisodwa, ngamunye unezoom yawo, nendlela yokuvula imidwebo eminingi nokuyibona ngokuhlangothi nohlangothi.

## Hlukanisa umdwebo owodwa ngamaphaneli

1. Vula umdwebo, noma udweba imigqa nezindilinga ezimbalwa.
2. Vula {{ui:View}} › {{ui:More}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Split side by side}} (noma uthayipha {{cmd:VPORTS}}, bese `2`, bese {{key:Enter}}).

Indawo yomdwebo ihlukana ibe amaphaneli amabili, zombili zibonisa umbono ofanayo. Iphaneli elisebenzayo linoluhlaka oluluhlaza, futhi linekhosi lokudweba.

3. Chofoza kwenye iphaneli. Iba yisebenzayo. (Lokho kuchofoza kokuqala kushintsha amaphaneli kuphela; chofoza futhi ukuze ukhethe.)
4. Zoom ngevili. Iphaneli leyo kuphela ezoom.
5. Hudula umugqa phakathi kwamaphaneli ukuze wenze elinye libe banzi. {{key:Home}} esihlukanisini esigxilile ilibuyisela phakathi.

{{cmd:VPORTS}} ithatha futhi `3` (amaphaneli amabili ohlangothini olulodwa nelikhulu kolunye: `L` noma `R`), `4`, kanye ne-`SI` ukuze kubuyele iphaneli elilodwa. Umbono wephaneli elisebenzayo ugcwalisa indawo. Ishidini, {{cmd:VPORTS}} yengeza i-viewport kuleshidi esikhundleni salokho (njenge-AutoCAD); thayipha khona {{cmd:SPLIT}}, noma sebenzisa imenyu ye-View.

## Hudula kusuka kephaneli kuya kwenye

1. Hlukanisa ngokuhlangothi nohlangothi, bese uzoom ngamunye iphaneli kwelinye ikhona lomdwebo.
2. Thayipha `L` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese uchofoza iphoyinti lokuqala kuphaneli lesobunxele.
3. Chofoza kanye kuphaneli lesokudla ukuze liyisebenzayo, bese uchofoza iphoyinti lesibili lapho.

Umugqa uhamba phakathi kwalezi zindawo ezimbili. Umyalo uyaqhubeka njengoba ushintsha amaphaneli, futhi isibambi sokuhudula sibonakala kuwo wonke amaphaneli.

## Imodeli eceleni kweshidi

1. Hlukanisa ngokuhlangothi nohlangothi.
2. Chofoza iphaneli lesokudla, bese uchofoza ithebhu yeshidi ngaphansi komdwebo.

Iphaneli lesokudla libonisa ishidi; iphaneli lesobunxele libonisa imodeli. Iphaneli ngalinye ligcina isikhala salo, ngakho ungadweba kumodeli bese ubona i-viewport yeshidi ilandela.

## Imidwebo eminingi ewindini elilodwa

1. Vula umdwebo. Bese uvula enye (noma cindezela {{ui:New}}).

Umdwebo ngamunye uvulekela ethebhini layo phezu kwendawo yokudweba. Chofoza ithebhu ukuze ushintshe; umugqa womyalo, amaphaneli nokuhlehlisa kulandela umdwebo oshintshela kuwo. {{key:Mod+C}} kumdwebo owodwa no-{{key:Mod+V}} kwelinye kukopisha izinto phakathi kwawo.

2. Chofoza {{ui:Tile the open drawings side by side}} (isithonjana sokuhlukanisa ekugcineni kwamathebhu), noma isithonjana esiyisitaki eduze kwaso.

Iphaneli ngalinye manje libonisa omunye wemidwebo evulekile, kuze kube mine. Chofoza iphaneli ukuze usebenze kulowo mdwebo.

3. Chofoza {{ui:×}} kwithebhu ukuze uvale lowo mdwebo. Uma unezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, i-CadCraft iyabuza kuqala.

:::tip
Ukuvula umdwebo ngenkathi iwindi libamba umdwebo omusha, ongakaguquki kuphela, kusebenzisa lelo thebhu esikhundleni sokungeza elinye.
:::

## Hambisa futhi uzoom ndawonye

Ukuqhathanisa izinguqulo ezimbili, zihlelele ngokuhlangothi nohlangothi, bese uchofoza {{ui:Link the panes' views}} (isithonjana sokuxhuma ekugcineni kwamathebhu), noma ukhethe kwi-{{ui:View}} › {{ui:More}}, noma uthayipha {{cmd:LINKVIEWS}} bese `ON`. Manje ukushelelisa noma ukuzoom kephaneli eyodwa kuhambisa wonke amaphaneli abonisa imodeli (noma wonke amaphaneli abonisa ishidi) endaweni efanayo, ukuze imidwebo emibili ihlale ihlelekile. {{cmd:LINKVIEWS}} `OFF` ivumela ngamunye iphaneli ukuthi ihambe yodwa futhi.

## Izinkinga ezivamile

- **Ukuchofoza kwami akwenzanga lutho.** Kwenze leyo phaneli isebenze. Chofoza futhi ukuze udwebe noma ukhethe.
- **Ivili lezoom lazoom iphaneli engeyona.** Ivili lizoom iphaneli ephansi kwesikhombi futhi lenze ukuthi isebenze.
- **Amaphaneli abuyele kokukodwa.** Ukuvula umdwebo kubonisa njengoba wagcinwa; umdwebo ogcinwe ngaphandle kwamaphaneli uvulwa ngodwa.
- **Ukushiya ikhasi kuxwayisa ngezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga engingaziboni.** Enye ithebhu iphethe umdwebo onezinguquko. Yiya kuyo bese uyilondoloza, noma uphume ungayilondolozi.
