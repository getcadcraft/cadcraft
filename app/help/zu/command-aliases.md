## Okuzofunda

Indlela yokwengeza amagama akho amafushane emiyalo, njengo `LL` ye-{{cmd:LINE}}, konke kufayela lombhalo elilodwa.

## Engeza i-alias

1. Vula {{ui:Ukubuka}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Hlela ama-alias…}}, noma bhala {{cmd:ALIASEDIT}} bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Engeza umugqa onalo i-alias, ikhoma, nomyalo ngemva kwenkanyezi:

```
LL,     *LINE
```

3. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}. Umugqa womyalo utshela ukuthi ama-alias akho angaki asebenzayo.
4. Bhala `LL` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: i-LINE iyaqala.

Ama-alias akho asebenza phezu kwawe-CadCraft. Umugqa wakho uyanqoba i-alias ekhona enegama elifanayo; uma usususa umugqa wakho, leyo ekhona iyabuya.

## Imigqa i-CadCraft eyenqaba

Umhleli udweba inkinga ngayinye ngenombolo yomugqa wayo lapho uthayipha:

- umyalo awukho;
- i-alias yiyona egama lomyalo (`LINE, *CIRCLE` bekuzofihla i-LINE);
- umugqa awuwona `ALIAS, *COMMAND`;
- i-alias efanayo ikhona emigqeni emibili (eyokugcina iyanqoba).

Imigqa eqala uhlelo kukhompiyutha yakho, njengo `NOTEPAD, START, 1, *File to edit: ,`, iyadlulwa nenothi: uhlelo oluku-browser alukwazi ukuqala izinhlelo.

## Letha ama-alias akho kolunye uhlelo

1. Kumhleli we-alias, chofoza {{ui:Ngenisa i-.pgp…}} bese ukhetha ifayela. Ungaphinda ulahle ifayela `.pgp` emdwebweni.
2. Hlola izinkinga ezidweshiwe (imiyalo i-CadCraft ingayinaki iyenqatshwa, eseleyo iyasebenza).
3. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}.

{{ui:Thumela .pgp}} ilondoloza ama-alias we-CadCraft namawakho njengefayela elifanayo ngefomethi.

## Layisha kabusha ifayela

Bhala {{cmd:REINIT}} bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze usebenzise kabusha ifayela lakho lama-alias. Ligcinwa kule browser; uhlelo lwedeskithophu luzoligcina kwifolda yalo, nensiza yefu izolivumelanisa.

## Izinhlamvu zezinketho zomyalo

I-alias ibiza umyalo. Ukuze ushintshe izinhlamvu eziphendula izinketho zomyalo (`P` ye-Polar ku-{{cmd:ARRAY}}), bheka [Nika izinketho zomyalo izinhlamvu zakho](topic:option-letters).

## Izinqamuleli zekhibhodi zanoma yimuphi umyalo

I-alias ibhalwa; isinqamuleli siyinkinobho. Ukuze unike umyalo inkinobho yakho:

1. Chofoza {{ui:Ukubuka}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Izinqamuleli zekhibhodi…}}, noma bhala `KEYS` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Chofoza ibhokisi le-{{ui:Ukhiye}} bese ucindezela okhiye, isibonelo {{key:Alt+Shift+C}}.
3. Kwi-{{ui:Iqalisa}}, bhala umyalo, nokuthi uyathanda yini ukuthayipha okuthile ngemva kwawo: `CIRCLE`, noma `ZOOM E`.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Engeza}}.

Inkinobho idinga {{key:Mod}} noma {{key:Alt}} nohlamvu noma inombolo, noma inkinobho yomsebenzi ekhululekile ({{key:F2}}, {{key:F4}}, {{key:F5}}, {{key:F6}}, {{key:F9}}). Okhiye abasetshenziswa yi-CadCraft, uhlelo noma i-browser, nokhiye enenkinobho yami yamathuluzi (bheka [Izinkinobho zakho](topic:my-tools)), ayamukelwa, nebhokisi lichaza ngani. Izinqamuleli zigcinwa kule browser (noma kuhlelo lwedeskithophu); {{ui:Khipha}} iyazisusa.
