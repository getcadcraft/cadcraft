## Okuzofunda

Indlela yokungeza amagama amafushane akho emiyalo, njengo `LL` ye-{{cmd:LINE}}, konke kufayela lombhalo elilodwa.

## Engeza i-alias

1. Vula {{ui:View}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Edit aliases…}}, noma uthayipha {{cmd:ALIASEDIT}} bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Engeza umugqa onama-alias, ikhoma, nomyalo ngemva kwenkanyezi:

```
LL,     *LINE
```

3. Chofoza {{ui:Save}}. Umugqa womyalo utshela ukuthi ama-alias akho angaki asasebenza.
4. Thayipha `LL` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: i-LINE iyaqala.

Ama-alias akho asendaweni engaphezulu kwawe we-CadCraft. Umugqa wakho uyanqoba i-alias yangaphakathi enegama elifanayo; susa umugqa wakho bese eyangaphakathi ibuyela.

## Imigqa i-CadCraft ayiyamukeli

Umhleli ukhomba inkinga ngayinye nenombolo yomugqa ngesikhathi uthayipha:

- umyalo awukho;
- i-alias iyigama lomyalo uqobo (`LINE, *CIRCLE` ingafihla i-LINE);
- umugqa awuwona `ALIAS, *COMMAND`;
- i-alias efanayo ikhona emigqeni emibili (eyokugcina iyanqoba).

Imigqa eqala uhlelo oluqalisa uhlelo kwikhompyutha yakho, njenge-`NOTEPAD, START, 1, *File to edit: ,`, iyadlulwa ngesaziso, ngoba uhlelo esiphequluludweni alukwazi ukuqalisa izinhlelo.

## Letha ama-alias akho kwenye inhlelo

1. Kumhleli we-alias, chofoza {{ui:Import .pgp…}} bese ukhetha ifayela. Ungaphinda ulisuse ifayela le-`.pgp` emdwebeni.
2. Hlola izinkinga ezidwetshiwe (imiyalo i-CadCraft ingayinaki iyenqatshwa, okunye kuyasebenza).
3. Chofoza {{ui:Save}}.

{{ui:Export .pgp}} igcina ama-alias e-CadCraft namawakho njengefayela elilodwa ngefomethi efanayo.

## Layisha kabusha ifayela

Thayipha {{cmd:REINIT}} bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze usebenzise kabusha ifayela lakho lama-alias. Ligcinwa kule sisekeli, inhlelo yasedeskithophu izoligcina kwifolda yayo, nensiza yefu izolilungelelanisa.

## Izinqamuleli zekhibhodi zanoma yimuphi umyalo

I-alias ithayiphwa; isinqamuleli ngukhiye. Ukuze unikeze umyalo ukhiye wakho:

1. Chofoza {{ui:View}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Keyboard shortcuts…}}, noma uthayipha `KEYS` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Chofoza ibhokisi le-{{ui:Key}} bese ucindezela okhiye, isibonelo {{key:Alt+Shift+C}}.
3. Ku-{{ui:Runs}}, bhala umyalo, nokuthi uthayiphe ini ngemva kwawo uma ufisa: `CIRCLE`, noma `ZOOM E`.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Add}}.

Ukhiye udinga {{key:Mod}} noma {{key:Alt}} nohlamvu noma inombolo, noma ukhiye we-function ongasetshenziswa ({{key:F2}}, {{key:F4}}, {{key:F5}}, {{key:F6}}, {{key:F9}}). Okhiye abasetshenziswa yi-CadCraft, uhlelo noma isiphequluli, nalabo abasenkinobhweni yamathuluzi ami (bona [Izinkinobho zakho](topic:my-tools)), ayenqatshwa, nebhokisi liyachaza ngani. Izinqamuleli zigcinwa kulesi siphequluli (noma kuhlelo lwedeskithophu); i-{{ui:Remove}} iyasisusa.
