I-CadCraft iyi-app yokudweba ye-2D. Uhudula izimugqa, imisnsalo, amaphola-layini, umbhalo, izilinganiso namabhulokhi, ulungiselele amashidi nezibuko zokubuka, futhi uwashicilele ku-PDF. Ivula imidwebo ye-DWG ne-DXF futhi iwalondoloza njenge-DWG noma i-DXF.

Isebenza kwisiphequluli sakho sewebhu. Akukho okufakayo: iwebhusayithi inikeza ikheli, nayo yonke i-app ilayishela ekhasini. Imidwebo yakho ivulwa kusuka kukhompyutha yakho futhi ibuyiselwa kuyo. **Akukho okulayishwayo.**

Uma usuke wadweba ngaphambili, funda [Uvela kolunye uhlelo lokudweba](topic:coming-from) ngemva kalo khasi. Iningi lezinkambo ziyaphindeleka, nezimbalwa ezingaphindeleki zidwetshwe lapho.

## Ufuna ukuqondisiwa?

Bhala `TOUR` (noma chofoza {{ui:Thatha uhambo}} ekhadini lokuqala, noma {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:CadCraft}} › {{ui:Thatha uhambo futhi}}). Izinyathelo ezi-6 ezimfushane ebhokisini phezu komdwebo: dweba umugqa nendilinga, uwuhambise, uwusike, ususe okuthile bese uwulinganisa. Inyathelo ngalinye liqeda lapho uyenza, futhi ungadlula inyathelo noma uhambo lonke. Liqala lodwa kuphela ngokuqala. [Ishidi lesisheshayo](topic:cheat-sheet) linemiyalo engu-40 ojwayele kakhulu ukuyisebenzisa, nemephu [yomyalo](topic:command-map) ngalo lonke umyalo okhona.

## Okudingayo

- I-Chrome, i-Edge, i-Firefox noma i-Safari yakamuva kukhompyutha ye-Mac, Windows noma Linux. Bona [Iziphequluli namapulatifomu](common:browsers).
- I-mouse enesondo yisona esilula kakhulu. I-trackpad iyasebenza; bona [I-mouse, i-trackpad nokuthinta](topic:mouse-trackpad-touch).
- Ikhibhodi. I-CadCraft iqhutshwa yimiyalo ebhalwayo, njengezinhlelo eziningi zokudweba.

## Umqondo ngomusho owodwa

Ubhala umyalo (noma uchofoze inkinobho yawo), futhi umugqa womyalo ongaphansi ukubuza imibuzo ngayinye ngesikhathi: "Khetha iphoyinti lokuqala", "Khetha iphoyinti elilandelayo". Uyiphendula ngokuchofoza kumdwebo noma ngokubhala inani, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ukucindezela {{key:Esc}} kuyakhansela. Yilokho konke ukusebenzisana; konke okunye yimininingwane.

## Umdwebo wakho wokuqala

Uzodweba unxantathu ka-4000 ngo-3000 ngemigqa, ufake indilinga phakathi, bese ulondoloza ifayela.

1. Vula i-CadCraft. Umugqa womyalo ongaphansi ubonisa `Umyalo:` nendawo yomdwebo ibonisa ikhadi elifushane elithi "Qala ukudweba".
2. Bhala `L` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Akudingeki uchofoze umugqa womyalo kuqala: ukubhala noma kuphi kuya lapho.
3. Isicelo sishintsha sibe `Khetha iphoyinti lokuqala:`. Bhala `0,0` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
4. Bhala `@4000,0` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Umugqa uvela, uqhubeka ngakwesokudla. I-`@` isho "kusukela ephoyintini lokugcina".
5. Bhala `@0,3000` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
6. Bhala `@-4000,0` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
7. Bhala `C` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. I-`C` yinketho ethi Vala (`C`): umugqa wokugcina ubuyela ephoyintini lokuqala futhi umyalo uyaphela.
8. Chofoza {{ui:Konke}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Ukubuka}} ethulusini. Unxantathu ugcwalisa isikrini.

Kufanele ubone imigqa emine eyakha unxantathu, nomlando ongaphezulu womugqa womyalo uludla lokho obhale kuwo.

9. Bhala `C` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uqale umyalo wendilinga.
10. Bhala `2000,1500` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uthole isikhungo.
11. Bhala `500` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uthole irediyasi.

Indilinga ivela phakathi kwe-unxantathu.

12. Cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Ifayela}}.

Isiphequluli silanda `Drawing1.dwg` ku-folda yakho yokulanda ejwayelekile, nomlando ubika ukuthi ukopishe njenge-DWG. Lelo fayela livuleka futhi ku-CadCraft, nakwezinye izinhlelo eziningi ze-CAD. (Uma ukhethe i-DXF ku-{{ui:Londoloza njenge}} ngaphambili, kulesi siphequluli, umdwebo omusha uyalondolozwa njenge-`Drawing1.dxf`.)

:::tip
Cindezela {{key:Enter}} emugqeni womyalo ongenalutho ukuze uphinde umyalo wokugcina. Cindezela {{key:Space}} esikhundleni se-{{key:Enter}} uma uthanda: ngaphandle kombhalo, yenza okufanayo.
:::

## Vula umdwebo osuvele ukhona

1. Cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Vula}}.
2. Khetha ifayela `.dwg` noma `.dxf` kusikhethi-fayela.

Ungabuye uhudule ifayela le-DWG noma le-DXF usuka ku-{{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=isiphathi-fayela sakho}} uliyeke endaweni yomdwebo. Ifayela lifundwa ekhasini; alishiyi ikhompyutha yakho.

Bona [Amafayela: okuvulwayo, okulondolozwayo](topic:files) ngokugcinwa nokuguqulwa.

## Ukulondoloza: okulindeleke

Ukulondoloza kusiphequluli kusebenza ngendlela ehlukile kunakuhlelo lwedeskithophu:

- {{ui:Londoloza}} njalo **ilanda ikhophi entsha**. Ayibhali phezu kwefayela ovule. Uma ifayela linegama elifanayo sele lisefolda yakho yokulanda, isiphequluli ngokuvamile sengeza inombolo, njengo `Drawing1 (1).dwg`.
- {{ui:Londoloza}} isebenzisa ifomethi yomdwebo: i-DWG uma uvule i-DWG, i-DXF uma uvule i-DXF. Ukukhetha, cindezela {{key:Mod+Shift+S}} noma chofoza {{ui:Londoloza njenge}}, bese ukhetha {{ui:DWG}} (nenguqulo yayo), {{ui:DXF R12}} noma {{ui:DXF 2000}}. Bona [Ukulondoloza](topic:files#saving).
- Ifayela ovule alikaze lishintshwe.
- Ukuvula elinye ifayela, noma {{ui:Okusha}}, kulibeka ethebhini layo phezu komdwebo, ngakho akukho okuvulekile okuvalwayo. Uma uvala ithebhu yomdwebo noma ushiye ikhasi onezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, i-CadCraft iyabuza kuqala: {{ui:Londoloza ikhophi kuqala}}, lahla, noma {{ui:Khansela}}. Ukuvala ithebhu yesiphequluli kukhombisa umbuzo womsakazo wesiphequluli "Shiya isayithi?" esikhundleni salokho.

Ukulondoloza kulayini (ifu) akukatholakali okwamanje. Lapho amafayela akho ahamba khona nokuthi kungani ehlala eyimfihlo kuchazwa ku-[Amafayela akho nobumfihlo](common:your-files).

## Okulandelayo

Cindezela {{key:F1}} noma chofoza {{ui:Usizo}} ebhalweni eliphezulu ukuze uvule lolu siza ecaleni komdwebo noma nini. Lusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhumeka.

- [Isikrini se-CadCraft](topic:workspace) sibiza yonke ibha nepaneli.
- [Dweba ngokuxhumanisa](topic:draw-with-coordinates) yiwona mhlahlandlela wokuqala omuhle kakhulu.
- [Ikhibhodi nemouse](topic:keyboard) ibala zonke izinkinobho.
- Bhala `HELP` emugqeni womyalo ukuze uthole uhlu lwayo yonke imiyalo, noma funda [Isalathisi semiyalo](topic:commands).
