## Okuzokwenziwa

Itafula nesihlalo esisodwa, sikopishwe kuzungeze itafula, sijikelezwe, sibhobozwe futhi sishintshwe usayizi. Ngaleso sikhathi usebenzisa {{cmd:MOVE}}, {{cmd:COPY}}, {{cmd:ROTATE}}, {{cmd:MIRROR}}, {{cmd:SCALE}} kanye {{cmd:UNDO}}.

## Dweba izingxenye

1. Thayipha `REC` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, thayipha `0,0` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese uthayipha `@1800,900` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Lona yitafula.
2. Thayipha `REC` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, thayipha `300,-700` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese uthayipha `@450,450` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Lesi yisihlalo, ngaphansi kwetafula.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Konke}}.

## Hambisa: iphoyinti lesisekelo, bese iphoyinti lesibili

Yonke imisebenzi yokuhambisa, yokukopisha, yokujikeleza nokushintsha usayizi iqala ngephoyinti **lesisekelo**: iphoyinti elithwalwayo, noma elijikelezwayo.

1. Chofoza isihlalo ukuze usikhethe.
2. Thayipha `M` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Umlando uthi `1 found`.
3. Ku-`Specify base point`, bamba iphoyinti lokuphezulu kwesobunxele lesihlalo (Iphoyinti lokuphela) bese uchofoza.
4. Ku-`Specify second point`, thayipha `@0,200` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Isihlalo siphakanyiswa ngo-200, sisondele etafuleni.

:::tip
Thayipha `D` ku-`Specify base point` ukuze unikeze ukuhamba kwesikhala esikhundleni salokho: i-CadCraft bese ibuza `Specify displacement`, wena uthayipha ukuthi kude kangakanani ngokuthwala nangokuphakamisa, njengo `0,200` (akudingeki `@`).
:::

## Kopisha, izikhathi eziningi ongazifuna

1. Chofoza isihlalo.
2. Thayipha `CO` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Bamba iphoyinti lokuphansi kwesobunxele lesihlalo bese uchofoza njengephoyinti lesisekelo.
4. Thayipha `@600,0` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ikhophi ivela ngu-600 ngakwesokudla.
5. Thayipha futhi `@600,0` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ikhophi yesibili ivela ngu-1200 ngakwesokudla kwangempela.
6. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda umyalo.

{{cmd:COPY}} iqhubeka kuze kube yilapho ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ikhophi ngayinye ibekwa ngobude obusuka ephoyinteni lesisekelo kuya kuleliphoyinti onikeza. Iphoyinti `@` libalwa kusukela ephoyinteni lokugcina owalifakile, okuyilona iphoyinti lale khophi emva kwekhophi yokuqala, ngakho `@600,0` kabili inika amakhophi ku-600 nango-1200. Thayipha `U` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uthathe ikhophi yokugcina ngaphandle kokushiya umyalo.

Kufanele ubone izihlalo ezintathu zilandelana ngaphansi kwetafula.

:::tip
{{cmd:COPY}} ilungele amakhophi angaphakathi komdwebo owodwa, ibekwe ngephoyinti lesisekelo. Ukuthwala izinto kumdwebo noma ithebhu enye, noma ukuzinamathisela kamuva, sebenzisa ibhodi lokopisha: {{key:Mod+C}}, bese {{key:Mod+V}}. {{key:Mod+D}} yenza ikhophi eyodwa ehamba nesikhombi. Bona [Kopisha, namathisela nephinda](topic:copy-paste).
:::

## Bhoboza umugqa ohlangothini olunye

1. Hudula ifreyimu yokukhetha (kusuka kwesokudla kuya kwesobunxele) phezu kwezihlalo ezintathu, ungathintanga itafula.
2. Thayipha `MI` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Ku-`Specify first point of mirror line`, bamba i-Midpoint yohlangothi lwesobunxele letafula bese uchofoza.
4. Ku-`Specify second point of mirror line`, bamba i-Midpoint yohlangothi lwesokudla letafula bese uchofoza.
5. Ku-`Erase source objects? [Yes/No] <N>`, cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uzigcine.

Kufanele ubone izihlalo ezintathu ngaphansi kwetafula nezihlalo ezintathu ezibhobozwe ngaphezulu kwalo. Umbhalo kukhethwa okubhobozwe uhlala ufundeka: ubekwa ubhobozwe kodwa awubhalwa ngemuva.

## Jikeleza isihlalo

1. Chofoza isihlalo esingakwesokudla kakhulu ngaphansi kwetafula.
2. Thayipha `RO` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Bamba iphoyinti lokuphansi kwesokudla lesihlalo bese uchofoza njengephoyinti lesisekelo.
4. Thayipha `90` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Isihlalo siyajikeleza ngamadigri angu-90 ngokuphambene nesikhathi sewashi, ngokuzungeza iphoyinti lesisekelo. I-engeli enegethivu ijikeleza ngesikhathi sewashi. Esikhundleni sokuthayipha, ungachofoza iphoyinti lesibili: i-engeli ilinganiswa kusuka ephoyinteni lesisekelo kuya kulelo phoyinti.

## Skala itafula

1. Khetha itafula.
2. Thayipha `SC` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Bamba iphoyinti lokuphansi kwesobunxele letafula bese uchofoza njengephoyinti lesisekelo.
4. Thayipha `1.2` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Itafula liba likhulu ngo-20%, likhula kude nephoyinti lesisekelo. Ifekthri engaphansi kuka-1 yenza kube ncane.

:::note
Uma uchofoza iphoyinti esikhundleni sokuthayipha ifekthri yokuskala, ifekthri ingubude obusuka ephoyinteni lesisekelo kuya kulelo phoyinti. Ukuchofoza iyunithi yomdwebo eyodwa kude kunikeza ifekthri engu-1. Ukuthayipha inombolo kuvame ukuba yilokho ofuna kakhulu.
:::

## Hlehlisa nophinda wenze

- {{key:Mod+Z}}, inkinobho ethi {{ui:Hlehlisa}}, noma `U` bese {{key:Enter}} kuhlehlisa umyalo wokugcina. Umyalo ophelele uyisinyathelo esisodwa: ukuhlehlisa {{cmd:COPY}} eyenze amakhophi amahlanu kususa wonke amahlanu.
- {{key:Mod+Y}}, {{key:Mod+Shift+Z}}, inkinobho ethi {{ui:Phinda wenze}}, noma `REDO` ibuyisela lokho.
- Umlando utshela ukuthi yini ehlehliswe ("Ihlehliswe COPY").

I-CadCraft igcina izinyathelo ezingu-500 zokugcina. Izilungiselelo zezingqimba (vula, qandisa, khiya, umbala) ziyizilungiselelo, hhayi ukuhlelwa, ngakho azihlehliswa.

## Elula igumbi libe banzi

{{cmd:STRETCH}} (`S`, noma {{ui:Stretch}}) ihambisa kuphela lokho okuphakathi kwefreyimu yokuhlangana: amakhona neziphetho zemigqa ezingaphakathi kwayo ayahamba, konke okunye kuhlala kunjalo, ngakho igumbi libanzi esikhundleni sokuhamba.

1. Thayipha `S` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Hudula ifreyimu kusuka kwesokudla kuya kwesobunxele phezu kodonga lwesokudla, ukuze imboze amakhona odonga neziphetho zanoma yini ehlangene nalo.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}}.
4. Chofoza iphoyinti lesisekelo, bese uthayipha `@600,0` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Igumbi libanzi nge-600; imigqa eyayiphela kodonga isaphela kulo. I-arc enesiphetho esisodwa ngaphakathi igcina ukuphakama kwayo, izindilinga nombhalo kuyahamba uma isikhungo sazo noma iphoyinti lokufaka lingaphakathi, nesilinganiso sishintsha ubude baso. Izinto ezikhethwa ngayinye, ngaphandle kwefreyimu, zihamba nje.

Ungakhetha futhi kuqala: hudula ifreyimu yokuhlangana (noma i-lasso yokuhlangana) phezu kodonga ngaphandle komyalo osebenzayo, bese uthayipha `S`. STRETCH ithatha lokho okukhethiwe ilelule okubanjwe yifreyimu zayo, njengoba ubuhudulile esikhundleni sayo.

## I-array: imigqa namakholomu, noma ngokuzungeza isikhungo

{{cmd:ARRAY}} (`AR`, noma {{ui:Array}}) ikopisha okukhethiwe ngegridi noma ngesiyingi.

1. Khetha isihlalo bese uthayipha `AR`, cindezela {{key:Enter}}. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze ukhethe Yamakhona (R).
2. Kuvela ukubuka kwamakholomu angu-4 nemigqa engu-3. Thayipha `COU`, cindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese uthayipha `5` wamakholomu no-`2` wemigqa.
3. Thayipha `S`, cindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese uthayipha `600` phakathi kwamakholomu no-`-900` phakathi kwemigqa (okubi kwehla phansi).
4. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukwenza amakhophi.

Ukuze uthole indilinga yezihlalo ezizungeze itafula, khetha `PO` (Polar) esikhundleni, chofoza isikhungo setafula, bese usetha `I` (izinto), `F` (i-engeli yokugcwalisa: 360 ukuze kuzungeze konke, 180 uhhafu) no-`ROT` (uma amakhophi ejikeleza ukuze abheke isikhungo). {{cmd:ARRAYRECT}} ne-{{cmd:ARRAYPOLAR}} zidlula lo mbuzo.

## Xhuma izingxenye zibe yi-polyline eyodwa

{{cmd:JOIN}} (`J`, noma {{ui:Modify}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Join}}): khetha imigqa, ama-arc, nama-polyline avulekile lapho iziphetho zihlangana khona, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ziba yi-polyline eyodwa, evaliwe uma ihamba yonke indilinga, ongayi-offset ngesinyathelo esisodwa. Imigqa esemgqeni owodwa iba umugqa owodwa nakuba kukhona izikhala phakathi kwayo; ama-arc esiyingini esisodwa aba i-arc eyodwa, noma indilinga.

## Faniswa izimfanelo

{{cmd:MATCHPROP}} (`MA`, noma {{ui:Modify}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Match properties}}): chofoza into onayo ukubukeka kwayo, bese ukhetha izinto ozoshintsha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ziyathatha ingqimba nombala wayo; umbhalo uthatha ubude bawo, isilinganiso sithatha usayizi waso, i-hatch ithatha iphethini yayo. Thayipha `S` (Settings) embuzweni wesibili ukucima noma yimuphi walezi zinto.

## Hlanganisa izinto

**Ngamaphoyinti.** {{cmd:ALIGN}} (`AL`, noma {{ui:Modify}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Align}}): khetha izinto bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese uchofoza iphoyinti kuzo (umthombo) nalapho kufanele zifike khona (indawo okuya kuyo).

- Ipheya eyodwa iyazihambisa. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ku-`Specify second source point` ukuze umise lapho.
- Amaphheya amabili aphinde ajikeleze, ngakho umugqa phakathi kwamaphoyinti omthombo wamabili uhlala ulandelana nomugqa phakathi kwamaphoyinti okuya kuwo amabili. I-CadCraft bese ibuza `Scale objects based on alignment points?`: `Y` ziyenza zibe zikhulu noma zibe ncane nazo ngokufanayo.
- Ipheya yesithathu iyazibhoboza uma amaphoyinti amabili alo esemaceleni ahlukene omugqa.

**Imiphetho nesikhala.** {{ui:Modify}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Align and distribute}} (noma `ALIGNPANEL`) ivula iphaneli encane phezu komdwebo. Khetha izinto, bese:

- khetha okuzohlanganiswa nakho ku-{{ui:Align to}} (wonke amakhethwa, into yokuqala noma yokugcina oyikhethile, noma iphoyinti ozochofoza) bese uchofoza {{ui:Left}}, {{ui:Center}}, {{ui:Right}}, {{ui:Top}}, {{ui:Middle}} noma {{ui:Bottom}};
- noma khetha {{ui:Equal gaps}} noma {{ui:Equal centres}} bese uchofoza {{ui:Across}} noma {{ui:Up and down}}: izinto ezimbili zangaphandle ziyahlala, ezinye zibekwa zilinganiswe phakathi kwazo (izinto ezintathu nangaphezulu).

Ukuchofoza ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, nokukhethwa kuhlala, ngakho ungazama enye indlela. Into ngayinye ibalwa ngebhokisi layo lemingcele, ku-UCS uma isethiwe. Emugqeni womyalo okufanayo ngu-{{cmd:ALIGNTO}} no-{{cmd:DISTRIBUTE}}.

## Susa nehlukanise

- {{cmd:ERASE}} (`E`, noma {{key:Delete}}) isusa izinto ezikhethiwe.
- {{cmd:EXPLODE}} (`X`) ihlukanisa i-polyline ibe imigqa ne-arc, ireferensi yebhlokhi ibe izinto zayo, nesilinganiso ibe imigqa, izinhloko zemicibisholo nombhalo. Izilinganiso ezihlukaniswe azisasilinganisi zodwa.

## Izinkinga ezivamile

- **Ukuthayipha ibanga ngaphandle kwe-`@` ephoyinteni lesibili.** `600,0` iphoyinti eliphelele 600,0, hhayi "600 ngakwesokudla". Sebenzisa `@600,0`, noma `600` eyodwa nesikhombi siqondise ngendlela efanele.
- **Ukujikeleza kuhambe ngendlela engalungile.** Ama-engeli anegethivu ajikeleza ngokuphambene nesikhathi sewashi.
- **Ukuhlehlisa kususe okungaphezu kwalokho okulindelekile.** Ukuhlehlisa kumodwa kuhlehlisa umyalo wokugcina wonke, kuhlanganise namakhophi wonke awenzile.
- **Umyalo awucelanga ukukhetha izinto.** Izinto zase zikhethiwe, ngakho uzisebenzisile. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} kuqala ukuze uqale ungakukhethanga lutho.
