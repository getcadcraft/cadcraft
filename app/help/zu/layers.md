## Okuzokwenza

Izingqimba ezintathu, Walls, Doors kanye ne-Notes, ngayinye inombala wayo, nezinto ezidwetshwe kuzo nezihanjiswa phakathi kwazo.

## Iphaneli yezingqimba

Izingqimba zihlala kuphaneli yangakwesobunxele, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izingqimba}}. Umugqa ngamunye ubonisa, kusukela kwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla:

- isichomo: sigcwele kungqimba **lwamanje**, lapho izinto ezintsha zingena khona;
- isampula yombala;
- igama lengqimba;
- izinkinobho ezine: vula/vala (iso), qandisa (iqandu), khiya (ukhiye) nokushicilela (iphrinta).

Yonke imidwebo inengqimba ebizwa `0`. Ukubhala `LA` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} kukuyisa kuphaneli.

## Dala izingqimba

1. Chofoza {{ui:Ungqimba olusha}} ngaphezulu kohlu. Kuvula ibhokisi legama.
2. Bhala `Walls` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} (noma chofoza {{ui:Engeza}}).

Umlayezo wesimo uthi "Added layer Walls and made it current." Ingqimba entsha ithola umbala 7 (omnyama kwitimu elikhanyayo, omhlophe kweli elimnyama).

3. Chofoza {{ui:Ungqimba olusha}} futhi, bhala `Doors` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Ungqimba olusha}} futhi, bhala `Notes` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Manje `Notes` ngeyamanje.

## Nikeza ingqimba ngayinye umbala

1. Chofoza isampula yombala emugqeni we-`Walls`. Isikhethi sombala sesistimu siyavula.
2. Khetha umbala bese uqinisekisa.

Isampula nakho konke okusesikhathini `Walls` okuyinkomba {{ui:Ngongqimba}} kuyashintsha.

:::note
Umbala okhethiwe ofana nomunye wemibala engu-255 ejwayelekile ye-CAD ugcinwa njengale nombolo. Noma yimuphi omunye umbala ugcinwa ngokuphelele ku-CadCraft, kodwa uyalondolozwa njengombala ojwayelekile osondele kakhulu kufayela le-DWG noma le-DXF.
:::

## Dweba kungqimba

1. Chofoza igama **Walls** ohlwini. Isichomo sayo siyagcwala futhi ibha yesimo ithi "Current layer: Walls".
2. Dweba unxantathu (`REC`). Uyongena ku-Walls.
3. Chofoza **Doors**, bese udweba umnsalo (`A`). Uyongena ku-Doors.

Ungakhetha futhi ungqimba lwamanje ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}} ngakwesokudla (kungakhethwa lutho), ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ungqimba}}.

## Hambisa izinto kungqimba omunye

1. Khetha izinto.
2. Ku-panel yesokudla, khetha ungqimba omusha ku-{{ui:Ungqimba}}.

Ziyahamba ziye kuleyo ngqimba futhi zithatha umbala wayo uma umbala wazo uyi-{{ui:Ngongqimba}}.

## Vala, qandisa, khiya nushicilele

| Inkinobho | Okwenzayo |
|---|---|
| Vula/vala (iso) | Ifihla izinto zengqimba. Azikwazi ukubonakala, ukukhethwa noma ukubanjwa. |
| Qandisa (iqandu) | Umphumela ofanayo nokuvala ku-CadCraft. Igcinwa ihlukile ngoba ezinye izinhlelo ziyiphatha ngokwahlukile, futhi isethingi iyalondolozwa. |
| Khiya (ukhiye) | Izinto zihlala zibonakala futhi ungazibamba, kodwa azikwazi ukukhethwa noma ukuguqulwa. Kuhle ngemuva ofuna ukulandelela phezu kwayo. |
| Shicilela (iphrinta) | Uma icindezelwe, ingqimba ibonakala esikrinini kodwa **ishiywa ekushicilelweni** nasemaPDFeni. Yisebenzise imigqa yokwakha namanothi akho. |

Hlanganisa inkinobho ukuze ubone okuzokwenza ("Turn off", "Freeze", "Lock", "Do not plot this layer").

Awukwazi ukuvala ingqimba yamanje: ibha yesimo ithi "That is the current layer. Make another layer current first." Ukuchofoza igama lengqimba ukuze iyenze yamanje nayo iyayivula futhi iyancibilika.

Ushintsho ngalunye lwengqimba, kuphaneli noma ngomyalo, ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyabuyisela.

## Sebenza ngezingqimba ngokukhetha izinto

Akudingeki wazi igama lengqimba: khetha into kuyo. Bhala umyalo, noma vula {{ui:Amathuluzi}} phezulu kuphaneli ye-{{ui:Izingqimba}}.

| Umyalo | Okwenzayo |
|---|---|
| {{cmd:LAYISO}} | Khetha izinto, cindezela {{key:Enter}}: izingqimba zazo kuphela zihlala zivulekile, zonke ezinye zivalwa. Uma ingqimba yamanje ivaliwe, enye yezingqimba ezihlukanisiwe iba yamanje. |
| {{cmd:LAYUNISO}} | Ivula kabusha izingqimba u-{{cmd:LAYISO}} wokugcina wazivala, futhi ibuyisela ingqimba yamanje eyashintshwa. |
| {{cmd:LAYOFF}} | Chofoza izinto ngokulandelana: ingqimba yento ngayinye iyavalwa. Bhala `U` ukubuyisela okokugcina, {{key:Enter}} ukuphela. |
| {{cmd:LAYFRZ}} | Okufanayo, kuqandisa ingqimba ngayinye. |
| {{cmd:LAYMCUR}} | Chofoza into: ingqimba yayo iba yamanje (futhi iyavuleka futhi iyancibilika). Uma into eyodwa sele ikhethiwe, leyo ndlela iyasebenziseka. |
| {{cmd:LAYON}} | Ivula zonke izingqimba. |
| {{cmd:LAYTHW}} | Incibilikisa zonke izingqimba. |

Ingqimba yamanje ayivaliwe noma iqandiswe ngale ndlela: umlando uyasho lokho, futhi ungenza enye ingqimba ibe yamanje nge-{{cmd:LAYMCUR}} kuqala. Ukukhetha isinkomba sebhulokhi kusebenzisa ingqimba yaso yona.

## Qamba kabusha, hlanganisa nesula izingqimba

- {{cmd:RENAME}}: bhala `LA`, igama elidala nelisha. Yonke into engqimbeni iyalandela. (`B` iqamba kabusha ibhulokhi ngendlela efanayo, nazo zonke izinkomba zalo.)
- {{cmd:LAYMRG}}: chofoza into kwingqimba ngayinye ukuze uhlanganise (noma bhala `N` ugcine igama), cindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese ukhetha ingqimba yokuya kuyo bese uphendula `Y`. Izinto ziyahamba ziye kuyo — emodeleni, emashidini nasebhulokhini — bese izingqimba ezihlanganisiwe ziyanyamalala.
- {{cmd:LAYDEL}}: khetha into engqimbeni (noma `N` negama layo) bese uphendula `Y`. Ingqimba iyasuka **kanye nazo zonke izinto ezisemaphethelweni**, noma kuphi. Ingqimba 0, i-DEFPOINTS neyamanje azikwazi ukususwa.

Yilelo ngalunye isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Zonke zintathu zisekhasini le-{{ui:Amathuluzi}} phezulu kuphaneli ye-{{ui:Izingqimba}}.

1. Chofoza umugqa wodonga, bese ubhala `LAYISO` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Kuphela i-Walls ihlala ivuliwe.
2. Dweba noma shintsha okudingayo ku-Walls.
3. Bhala `LAYUNISO` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Doors ne-Notes ziyabuya.

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Imigqa emisha yangena kungqimba engalungile.** Hlola ukuthi yiliphi isichomo esigcwele ngaphambi kokudweba. Ingqimba entsha iba yamanje ngokushesha nje uma uyengeza.
- **Into ayikhethwa.** Ingqimba yayo ikhiyiwe, ivaliwe noma iqandisiwe.
- **Ingqimba isashicilelwa kolunye uhlelo.** I-DXF R12 ayikwazi ukugcina "do not plot". I-CadCraft igcina leso sethingi yayo uma umdwebo unephepha, kodwa ezinye izinhlelo zizoshicilela leyo ngqimba.
- **Amagama ezingqimba ngamakhaphithali ngemva kokulondoloza.** I-DXF R12 igcina amagama ngamakhaphithali, ngakho i-`Walls` ibuya njenge-`WALLS`, namabhulangwe aguqukele ku-`_`. I-DWG elondoloziwe igcina amagama njengoba uwabhala.
- **Ukuqamba kabusha, ukuhlanganisa noma ukususa ingqimba.** Sebenzisa {{cmd:RENAME}}, {{cmd:LAYMRG}} noma {{cmd:LAYDEL}} (futhi ku-{{ui:Amathuluzi}}): bheka ngezansi.

## Hlanganisa izingqimba

Gcina uhlu lufushane ngokubeka izingqimba ezikwamafolda, njengo-Existing, Proposed ne-Services. Ifolda ngayinye iyavaleka, futhi izinkinobho zayo zivula noma zivale zonke izingqimba zayo, ziziqandise noma zikhiye ndawonye. Ingqimba yamanje ayivaliwe ngaso sikhathi.

Ku-{{ui:Amathuluzi}} ephaneli yezingqimba, khetha {{ui:Engeza amaqembu alungiselelwe}} ngeqoqo elenzelwe imisebenzi emincane yokuhlala, {{ui:Hlanganisa ngegama}} ukuhlanganisa izingqimba ezinesiqalo esifanayo (`E-Walls`, `E-Doors`), noma {{ui:Iqembu elisha}}. Hudula ingqimba uyilethe efolda, noma sebenzisa uhlu olungaphezulu kwezingqimba ukuze uthole ingqimba yamanje. Bhala ku-{{ui:Thola ungqimba}} ukuze unciphise uhlu. I-`LAYGROUP` (`LG`) yenza okufanayo emugqeni womyalo.

Ukuze uqambe kabusha iqembu, chofoza kabili igama lalo (noma likhethe bese ucindezela {{key:F2}}), bhala igama elisha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}; {{key:Esc}} igcina elidala, futhi {{cmd:UNDO}} iyabuyisela. I-{{ui:Iqembu elisha}} itholakala ku-{{ui:Amathuluzi}} ephaneli yezingqimba.

Izingqimba ngokwazo azishintshi, ngakho ezinye izinhlelo ze-CAD zizibona njengohlu olujwayelekile. I-CadCraft igcina amaqembu efayeleni.
