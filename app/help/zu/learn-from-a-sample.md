## Okuzofunda

Indlela isethi eqedile eyakhiwa ngayo: isikhala semodeli, amashidi nezibuko zokubuka ngesikali, ibhulokhi yesihloko nezici, izilinganiso ngamafiti nama-intshi nangamamilimitha, izingqimba. Izampuli zingezeCadCraft ngokwayo futhi zikhululekile ukusetshenziswa kanoma yini (CC0).

## Vula isampuli

1. Epaneli yangakwesobunxele, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Umdwebo}}, chofoza {{ui:Vula isampuli}} (noma bhala {{cmd:SAMPLES}}; ezitayelweni zeribhoni itholakala kumenyu ye-{{ui:Ifayela}}).
2. Chofoza {{ui:Vula}} eceleni kwe-Indlu yesampuli. Ivula ngethebhu yayo, ngakho umsebenzi wakho uhlala uvuliwe.

## Hamba ungqongqoze imodeli

1. Chofoza {{ui:Konke}} (noma bhala `ZOOM`, bese `E`): ipulani yoyeni, ukubukeka kwangaphambili, isigaba nemininingwane yodonga.
2. Ku-{{ui:Izingqimba}}, chofoza ilihlo eliseduze kwe-`A-ANNO-DIMS` ukuze ufihle izilinganiso, bese uchofoza futhi ukuze uzibonise.
3. Chofoza umnyango: i-Izimfanelo ibonisa inkomba yebhulokhi ye-`DOOR-32`. Iminyango namawindi ngamabhulokhi, abekwe ejikisiwe futhi ephenduliwe.

## Amashidi

1. Chofoza {{ui:A-101 FLOOR PLAN}} ngaphansi komdwebo: ishidi le-ARCH D linesibuko sokubuka esisodwa ngo-1/4" = 1'-0".
2. Chofoza kabili ngaphakathi kwesibuko sokubuka ukuze usebenze imodeli ngaso; chofoza kabili ngaphandle ukuze ubuyele.
3. {{ui:A-501 DETAILS}} ibonisa imininingwane yodonga ngo-1 1/2" = 1'-0": imodeli efanayo, isikali esihlukile.

## Ibhulokhi yesihloko

1. Kunoma iliphi ishidi, chofoza kabili ibhulokhi yesihloko: {{ui:Hlela izici}} ibala i-PROJECT, i-TITLE, i-SCALE, i-DATE, i-DRAWN ne-SHEET.
2. Shintsha i-DRAWN ibe izinhlamvu zamagama akho bese uchofoza {{ui:KULUNGILE}}.
3. Ukuze ushintshe ibhulokhi yesihloko uqobo kuwo wonke amashidi, yikhethe bese ukhetha {{ui:Amabhulokhi}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Hlela endaweni yayo}}. Bona [Izici](topic:attributes).

## I-as-built

Vula {{ui:Isampuli ye-as-built}} ngendlela efanayo. Amachashaza okugwayi ku-`SCAN-SLICE` ayisiqephu sokuskena; izindonga ku-`AB-WALL` zahlahlelwa phezu kwawo. Udonga oluseMpumalanga lungaphandle kokuqonda ngama-35 mm: izilinganiso ziyasho lokho kabili, ngamamilimitha (`AB-DIMS-MM`) nangamafiti nama-intshi (`AB-DIMS-FTIN`, izilinganiso ezifanayo ngesikali sokulinganisa). Imijikelo yokuqaphela ebomvu ku-`REV-1` ikhomba lokho ukuhlola kuthole.
