## Izindlela ezimbili zokuletha ikhasi le-PDF

- **Ukuhlanganisa** ({{cmd:PDFATTACH}}) kugcina i-PDF njengesithombe ngaphansi komdwebo wakho. Uyilandela, uyibamba ngemigqa yayo, uyinciphisa ukukhanya, uyihlaba, futhi iphrintwa kanye neshidi. Umdwebo ugcina igama lefayela kuphela.
- **Ukungenisa** ({{cmd:PDFIMPORT}}) kuguqula imigqa, amajika, izigcwalisi nombhalo wekhasi kube yizinto ezijwayelekile zomdwebo, ezikwizingqimba ezibizwa ngegama le-PDF. Ungazihlela, futhi i-PDF ayidingeki ngemva kwalokho.

Ukuvula i-PDF nge-{{ui:Vula}} kubuza ukuthi yiyiphi kwezimbili ofuna.

## Hlanganisa ikhasi

1. Bhala `PDFATTACH` (noma `PDFATT`) bese ukhetha i-PDF. Uma inamakhasi amaningi, bhala inombolo yekhasi.
2. Chofoza indawo yokufaka (ikhona yekhasi engakwesobunxele ngezansi).
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuthola usayizi wangempela wekhasi, noma bhala isikali: iyunithi yomdwebo ngephoyinti ngalinye le-PDF (iphoyinti liyi-1/72 yeyintshi, ngakho u-0.3528 emdwebeni wamamilimitha).
4. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze ungaphenduki, noma bhala i-engeli.

Ikhasi iyinto njengesethenjwa lebhulokhi: yihambise, uyijikeleze, ushintshe usayizi, uyikopishe, uyisuse, futhi uyibeke esendlalelweni. Yicima isendlalelo sayo ize inyamalale. Sondeza bese ikhasi liyadwetshwa kabusha lacace.

## Landela

Ngo-{{ui:Bamba}} kuvulekile, ikhesa libamba emigqeni ye-PDF uqobo: izinhlangothi, amaphakathi nezinqamuko (nokuthi {{ui:Eseduze}} uma leso sibambi sivulekile). Umbhalo obhalwe njengezinhlamvu ekhasini awubanjwa.

## Lungisa

- `PDFADJUST`: **Nciphisa ukukhanya** (0 kuya ku-80 ephesenti), **Ukuqhathanisa** (0 kuya ku-100), **Okumnyama kuphela**, kanye **Vula/Vala** ukuze ufihle ngaphandle kokuyisusa.
- `PDFCLIP`: bonisa kuphela unxande noma ipholigoni yekhasi; **Susa** isusa ukuhlaba.
- `PDFLAYERS`: i-PDF ingaba nezingqimba zayo. Umyalo uzidweba; bhala inombolo ukuze uvule noma ucime leso sendlalelo kulesi sisekelo. Izingqimba efayela elizicimile ziqala zicimile.

## Shicilela futhi gcina

PLOT iphrinta ikhasi njengemigqa, izigcwalisi nombhalo (ngakho lihlala licacile futhi lisesingeka ngokusesha). Izithombe ngaphakathi kwe-PDF ziyabonakala esikrinini kodwa azikaphrintwa okwamanje.

I-DWG noma i-DXF 2000 igcina izingaphansi njengesixhumanisi: igama lefayela, ikhasi, lapho lilele khona, isikali, ukuphendula, ukuhlaba, ukunciphisa nokuqhathanisa. I-PDF uqobo ayisemdwebeni. Vula kabusha umdwebo, bhala `PDF` bese ukhetha ifayela futhi; kuze kube yilapho lokho kwenziwa, amakhasi abonakala njengezinxande, futhi athatha usayizi wawo wangempela ngemva kwalokho. I-DXF endala (R12) ayinazo izingaphansi: igcina uhlaka negama kuphela. Akukahloleki ukuthi ezinye izinhlelo zifunda kanjani ukuhlaba nesikali esigciniwe.

## Ngenisa ikhasi

1. Bhala `PDFIMPORT` (noma `PDFIM`) bese ukhetha i-PDF nekhasi.
2. Chofoza lapho ikhona yekhasi engakwesobunxele ngezansi izofika khona.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuthola usayizi wangempela, bhala isikali, noma khetha **Amaphoyinti amabili**: ikhasi lidwetshwa ngosayizi wangempela, bese uchofoza amaphoyinti amabili ebanga oliwaziyo bese ubhala ibanga langempela; konke kulinganiswa ukuze kuhambisane.

Imigqa iba yimigqa namapholi-layini, izindilinga namajika ayaqashelwa, izimo ezigcwele ziba yimigqa yokufiphaza eqinile, futhi umbhalo uba ngumbhalo esendlalelweni ephela nge-`-TEXT`. Kuyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa. Izithombe ku-PDF azingeniswa: hlanganisa ikhasi esikhundleni salokho. Iskani (ikhasi elingesithombe kuphela) alinamigqa yokungenisa.

Ama-PDF afundwa njengamafayela angathembekile: awukho amascript asebenzayo, futhi ikhasi elikhulu kakhulu liyanqunywa, kuyatshelwa lokho.
