Imiyalo ye-CadCraft ifinyelela ngendlela efanayo, futhi umugqa womyalo uhlala ukhona nama-alias awo (`L`, `C`, `TR` …). **Isitayela semenyu** sinquma kuphela ukuthi izinkinobho zihlala kuphi esikrinini, zibizwa ngani khona nokuthi yiziphi okhiye ezizisebenzisayo. Khetha esinye ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:CadCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}; ushintsho luyasebenza ngokushesha, futhi lesi siphequluli siyayikhumbula.

## Izitayela ezintathu

| Isitayela | Okutholayo |
|---|---|
| {{ui:CadCraft}} | Ibha yamathuluzi yayo: {{ui:Ifayela}}, {{ui:Dweba}}, {{ui:Guqula}}, {{ui:Chaza}}, {{ui:Amapharamitha}}, {{ui:Amabhulokhi}}, {{ui:Amashidi}}, Hlela no-{{ui:Ukubuka}}, ngayinye nemenyu {{ui:Okuningi}}. Isiqalo. |
| {{ui:Ukudweba kwakudala}} | Iribhoni elihlelwe njengendlela izinhlelo zokudweba ze-2D zakudala ezihlela ngayo: imenyu {{ui:Ifayela}} namankinobho asheshayo, bese amathebhu {{ui:Ikhaya}} (Dweba, Guqula, Chaza, Izingqimba, Ibhulokhi, Izakhiwo, Izinsiza, Ibhodi lokukopisha), {{ui:Faka}}, {{ui:Chaza}}, {{ui:Amapharamitha}}, {{ui:Ukubuka}}, {{ui:Phatha}}, {{ui:Okuphumayo}} no-{{ui:3D}}. Thayipha imiyalo njengakuqala. Inkinobho ephakathi ishelela; ukuchofoza okwesokudla kuyi-{{key:Enter}}, nokudonsa ngenkinobho yesokudla akwenzi lutho. |
| {{ui:Iribhoni}} | Iribhoni lamathebhu emisebenzi elula: {{ui:Dweba}}, {{ui:Guqula}}, {{ui:Chaza}}, {{ui:Amapharamitha}}, {{ui:Amabhulokhi}}, {{ui:Amashidi}}, {{ui:Ukubuka}}, {{ui:3D}} no-{{ui:Amathuluzi}}, lapho kukhona imenyu {{ui:Ifayela}} namankinobho asheshayo ngakwesokudla. Zonke izitayela zigcina okhiye bakwa-CadCraft: {{key:Mod+Z}}, {{key:Mod+Y}}, {{key:Mod+S}}, {{key:Mod+Shift+S}}, {{key:Mod+O}}, {{key:Mod+P}}, {{key:Mod+C}}, {{key:Mod+X}}, {{key:Mod+V}}, {{key:Mod+D}}, {{key:Mod+A}}, kanye {{key:F3}}, {{key:F7}}, {{key:F8}}, {{key:F10}}, {{key:F11}}, {{key:F12}} zamasizo okudweba. Amagama awasoze abe okhiye: i-CadCraft iwathayipha emugqeni womyalo.

Iribhoni liphinde libambe amathuluzi ephaneli Yezingqimba namasizo okudweba ebha yesimo. Ithebhu ye-{{ui:3D}} igcwala ngemva kokuvulwa ithebhu ye-3D ngaphansi komdwebo; ithuluzi le-3D elikhethwe ethebhini le-Model livula kuqala ithebhu ye-3D, nethuluzi le-2D elikhethwe ethebhini le-3D libuyela kuqala ethebhini le-Model.

:::tip
Ukhiye ongowe-layout ye-CadCraft kodwa isitayela sakho sawunikeza omunye umyalo awenzi lutho, futhi ibha yesimo ikhomba lapho umyalo wawo uye khona ("Ku-My keys, Ortho ngu-Ctrl+Alt+O"). {{key:Delete}}, {{key:Esc}}, {{key:Enter}}, {{key:Tab}}, {{key:Space}} nemicibisho ihlala ibe nencazelo yayo. Uma umyalo usebenza, noma kuthayiphwa amagama emugqeni womyalo, okhiye bawo bangowomugqa womyalo.
:::

## Yenze eyakho

1. Khetha isitayela esiseduze nalokho ofuna, bese uchofoza {{ui:Lungisa…}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Okhiye nemenyu}}, thola umyalo (ibhokisi lokusesha liyasiza). Chofoza {{ui:Engeza ukhiye}} bese ucindezela ukhiye. Chofoza ukhiye ukuze uwususe.
3. Ne-ribhoni, {{ui:Lapho ihlala khona}} ihambisa umyalo ethebhini nasephaneli elihlukile, emenyuni {{ui:Ifayela}}, noma ku-{{ui:Okhiye kuphela}}.
4. Ku-{{ui:Mouse}}, setha isondo nezinkinobho ezishelela umdwebo.
5. Nika igama bese uchofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}.

Ukulondoloza isitayela esakhelwe ngaphakathi kwenza ikhophi yakho ("Ukudweba kwami kwakudala"); eyakhelwe ngaphakathi ayishintshi lutho. Ukhiye onikezwe imiyalo emibili ubonakala ngombala obomvu, futhi kufanele alungiswe ngaphambi kokuthi ulondoloze.

## Okhiye abathayipha umyalo

{{cmd:SHORTCUTS}} (Ukubuka › Okuningi › {{ui:Izinqamuleli zekhibhodi…}}) inikeza ukhiye umugqa womyalo ophelele, njengo-{{key:Ctrl+Alt+L}} we-`LINE` noma {{key:F9}} we-`ZOOM E`. Lezi zokhiye zibhekene nesitayela esisebenzayo: ngokuqala kwalolu hlobo, okhiye owawenze kuguqulelo endala uthathelwa ku-"My CadCraft keys", kanye, futhi basebenza njengakuqala. Ukuthumela isitayela kuwathatha nawo.

## Imouse

- **Isondo lemouse**: {{ui:Ukuskrola phezulu kusondeza ngaphakathi}} (isiqalo) noma {{ui:Ukuskrola phezulu kusondeza ngaphandle}}, nokuthi kusheshe kangakanani. Ukuhlikihla kwepheyadi kusondeza njalo ngendlela yokunyakaza kweminwe yakho.
- **Shelelisa umdwebo nge**: inkinobho ephakathi, inkinobho yesokudla, noma zombili. Ukuchofoza okwesokudla ngaphandle kokudonsa kuyi-{{key:Enter}} njalo. Inkinobho yakwesobunxele ikhetha futhi idwebe ku-CadCraft njalo, ngakho ukuyikhetha akushintshi lutho lapha.
- **Donsa kumabhuyu e-3D**: ithebhu ye-3D igcina izinkinobho zayo kuzo zonke izitayela (kwesobunxele kuzungeza, ephakathi nakwesokudla kushelela).

## Thatha nawe

{{ui:Thumela}} igcina isitayela esisebenzayo njengefayela elincane le-`.ccinput.json`; {{ui:Ngenisa…}} lingeza elinye, ukusuka kwenye ikhompyutha noma kumngane wakho. Ifayela elingeniswe lingakhomba kuphela imiyalo ye-CadCraft; okunye kuko kuyashiywa futhi kubalwe kubha yesimo. {{ui:Susa lesi sitayela}} isusa eyakho.

:::note
Lezi zilungiselelo zihlala kulesi siphequluli. {{ui:Sebenzisa leli sitayela kuzo zonke izinhlelo}} ngaphansi kwamakhadi wezitayela yenza ukuthi zonke izinhlelo zivule esitayelweni esihambisanayo (ibha yayo yamathuluzi, iribhoni noma ukudweba kwakudala), nezinguquko ozenzile kwenhlelo ngayinye. I-CadCraft ayinayo isitayela sebha yemenyu: uma ibha yemenyu yenye inhlelo ikhethelwa zonke izinhlelo, i-CadCraft igcina ibha yayo yamathuluzi.
:::
