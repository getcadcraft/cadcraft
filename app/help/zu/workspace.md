Isikrini sinezingxenye eziyisikhombisa. Kusukela phezulu kuya phansi: ibha ye-app, ithuluzi, indawo yomdwebo nepaneli ngakwesokunxele nakwesokudla, amathebhu angaphansi komdwebo, umugqa womyalo nebha yesimo.

## Ibha ye-app

Ibha encane engaphezulu ngqo ingeyewebhusayithi, hhayi yomdwebo. Ibonisa uphawu lwe-CadCraft, igama le-app, inguqulo oyisebenzisayo, ixhumanisi lekhasi le-app, inkinobho ye-{{ui:Usizo}} (noma {{key:F1}}), inkinobho ye-{{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}, inkinobho ye-{{ui:Isikrini esigcwele}} nezinkinobho ezimbili zesitayela, {{ui:Iphepha}} ne-{{ui:Imodeli}}.

Inkinobho ye-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} (igiya) iqukethe izilungiselelo zakho ze-CadCraft nezo zonke ama-app ayabelana ngazo: bona [Izilungiselelo ze-CadCraft](topic:settings).

{{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} igcina ikhophi ye-CadCraft, kubandakanya isifundi se-DWG, kulesi siphequluli ukuze ivuleke ngaphandle kokuxhumeka. Ilebula layo likhombisa isimo: "Ilondoloza N kwangu-M", "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi", "Akukwazanga ukulondoloza" noma "Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi akutholakali". Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

:::note
Izinkinobho ze-{{ui:Iphepha}} ne-{{ui:Imodeli}} ebhalweni eliphezulu zishintsha imibala yesikrini kuphela: i-{{ui:Iphepha}} kuyitimu eyanyene, i-{{ui:Imodeli}} eyimnyama. Azinalo ngqo nethebhu ethi {{ui:Imodeli}} ngaphansi komdwebo, ethi iguqule phakathi komdwebo namashidi.
:::

Inkinobho ye-{{ui:Isikrini esigcwele}}, noma {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (inkinobho ye-Control kunoma yikuphi kukhompyutha, kubandakanya i-Mac), iguqula phakathi kwemodi ezintathu zesikrini: ejwayelekile, esigcwele, nesigcwele namabha efihliwe. Uma amabha efihliwe, hambisa isikhombi emphethweni ongaphezulu wesikrini ukuze uwabuyisele.

Ifasitela elikhudlwana libuyisela amagama.

## Ipaneli yangakwesobunxele

Ipaneli yangakwesobunxele inezigaba ezintathu.

- **{{ui:Izingqimba}}.** Yonke ingqimba emdwebeni, nencelisa egcwele kwelamanje. Chofoza igama ukuze ulenze libe ingqimba yamanje. Umugqa ngamunye unesampula yombala (chofoza ukuze ukhethe umbala) namaqhosa amane: vula/vala (ilihlo), misa (isiqhwagi), khiya (ukhiyo) nokudweba (isiphrinta). {{ui:Ungqimba olusha}} ivula ibhokisi lokwengeza enye. Bona [Hlela umdwebo ngezingqimba](topic:layers).
- **{{ui:Amabhulokhi}}.** Amabhulokhi achazwe emdwebeni, ukuthi asetshenziswe kangaki ngamunye, nenkinobho ye-{{ui:Faka}} ngayinye. Bona [Yenza bese ufaka amabhulokhi](topic:blocks).
- **{{ui:Umdwebo}}.** Iminingwane yefayela elivuliwe: igama (ne-"(kushintshiwe)" lapho kukhona izinguquko ezingalondolozwanga), ifomethi, usayizi, amayunithi, inani lezinto, izingqimba, amabhulokhi namashidi. Ngemva kokuvula ifayela, ibhokisi elithi "Mayelana nalele fayela" lichaza noma yini i-CadCraft engakwazanga ukuyibonisa noma ebidinga ukuguqulwa.

## Indawo yomdwebo

Maphakathi nesikrini kukhona umdwebo. Isikhombi siyinkomba ye-crosshair. Lapho i-CadCraft ifuna iphoyinti, ukhamba olumncane nelebula ("Isiphetho", "Maphakathi") lubonisa lapho ukubamba into kuzofika khona. Lapho ifuna izinto, i-crosshair inebhokisi lokukhetha futhi izinto ngaphansi kwalo zikhanya.

Lapho umdwebo ungenalutho, ikhadi elithi "Qala ukudweba" libonisa imiyalo embalwa yokuqala. Liyanyamalala uma usebenzisa umyalo noma uvula ifayela. Ungalahla ifayela le-DWG noma le-DXF noma yiphi indawo kule ndawo ukuze liyivule.

## Amathebhu angaphansi komdwebo

Ngaphansi komdwebo kukhona amathebhu: {{ui:Imodeli}} kuqala, bese {{ui:3D}}, bese ithebhu eyodwa ngeshidi ngalinye (isakhiwo), bese inkinobho ye-{{ui:+}}.

- {{ui:Imodeli}} ingumdwebo uqobo, ngosayizi ogcwele, ngamayunithi omdwebo.
- {{ui:3D}} ibonisa umdwebo ulele phansi nezinto eziqinile ezenziwe kuwo. Lapho ibonakala, ithuluzi linequbu le-{{ui:3D}} esikhundleni samaqembu omdwebo. Bona [Imodeli ye-3D](topic:3d-solids).
- Ithebhu ngayinye yeshidi iyiphepha elinebhulokhi yesihloko nezibuko zokubuka emodelini. Okudwetshwa khona kusezingeni lephepha (amamilimitha noma ama-intshi). Chofoza kabili ngaphakathi kwesibuko sokubuka ukuze usebenze imodeli ngaso (bona [Sebenza imodeli ngesibuko sokubuka](topic:sheets-and-viewports#work-on-the-model-through-a-viewport)). Igama elide leshidi linqunywa ethebhini; hlanganisa isikhombi ukuze uthole igama lonke. Uma kukhona amashidi amaningi, {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:CadCraft}} › {{ui:Amathebhu eshidi}} › {{ui:Izinombolo zeshidi kuphela}} ibonisa inombolo yeshidi ngayinye (A-101) kuphela ethebhini layo.
- {{ui:+}} yenza ishidi elisha linomngcele, ibhulokhi yesihloko nesibuko sokubuka esisodwa. Lifana nomyalo we-{{cmd:LAYOUT}}.
- Chofoza kabili ithebhu yeshidi ukuze uyiqamabele kabusha. Uma ithebhu igxilile, i-{{key:Left}} ne-{{key:Right}} zihambisa phakathi kwamathebhu.

Ithebhu ngayinye igcina ukusondeza nokushelelisa kwayo. Bona [Lungiselela ishidi](topic:sheets-and-viewports).

## Ipaneli yangakwesokudla: Izimfanelo

Ipaneli yangakwesokudla iyashintsha ngokuthi yini ekhethiwe.

- **Akukho okukhethiwe, ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}}:** ihedi ifunda {{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}}. Lapha usetha i-{{ui:Ungqimba}}, {{ui:Umbala}} ne-{{ui:Ukuphakama kombhalo}} kwezinto ezintsha, {{ui:Amayunithi}} omdwebo, nosayizi wezilinganiso ezintsha ({{ui:Ukuphakama kombhalo}}, {{ui:Usayizi womcibisholo}}, {{ui:Izingxenyana}}, {{ui:Isikali sonke}}).
- **Akukho okukhethiwe, eshidini:** ihedi ifunda "Ishidi:" negama leshidi. Ungaqamba kabusha leshidi, ushintshe {{ui:Iphepha}} ne-{{ui:Ukuma}} kwalo, {{ui:Dweba leli shidi}} noma {{ui:Susa leli shidi}}. Izilungiselelo zezinto ezintsha zilandela ngezansi.
- **Into eyodwa ekhethiwe:** uhlobo lwayo njengehedi (Umugqa, Indilinga, Umbhalo, Isilinganiso, Isibuko sokubuka …), bese ingqimba yayo, umbala nejiyomethri yayo. Bhala inani elisha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze ulishintshe.
- **Izinto eziningi ezikhethiwe:** zingaki, kanye {{ui:Ungqimba}} ne-{{ui:Umbala}} abavamile. Ukushintsha lokhu noma lokho kushintsha zonke. "Ziyahluka" kusho ukuthi ziyahluka.

Bona [Hlela ngamagripu nezimfanelo](topic:grips-and-properties).

Ngaphansi kwayo, ipaneli ye-{{ui:Amapharamitha}} ivula uma ukhetha {{ui:Amapharamitha}} noma ubhala {{cmd:PARAMETERS}}: yonke ipharamitha nokubopha kwezilinganiso ethebhulini elilodwa. Bona [Sebenzisa amapharamitha nezibalo](topic:parameters).

## Umugqa womyalo

Ngaphansi kukhona umugqa womyalo: umlando wakho wokwenza, nomyalo ngaphansi kwawo.

- Ilebula lomyalo lifunda `Umyalo:` lapho kungasebenzi lutho. Lapho umyalo usebenza, ibonisa umbuzo, izinketho zawo phakathi kwamabhulaketi asquare nokuzenzakalelayo phakathi kwamabhulaketi angu-angle, njengalapho kuthi `Khetha irediyasi yendilinga [Idayamitha] <500>:`.
- Njengoba ubhala igama lomyalo, iseluleko esingakwesokudla siphakamisa imiyalo efanayo. Cindezela {{key:Tab}} ukuze wamukele eyokuqala. "Akukho lomyalo" kusho ukuthi akukho okufanayo.
- {{key:Up}} ne-{{key:Down}} zibuyisa imigqa obhale ngaphambili.
- Umlando ugcina imigqa engu-400 yokugcina. Imilayezo evela kumiyalo, izilinganiso ezivela ku-{{cmd:DIST}} ne-{{cmd:LIST}}, nemibiko yokulondoloza nokudweba ivela lapha.

## Ibha yesimo

Umugqa osezansi kakhulu yibha yesimo.

- **Izixhumanisi** ngakwesobunxele: i-X ne-Y yesikhombi. Umyalo unephoyinti lesisekelo, ibonisa nebanga nengosi kusuka kulo, njengalapho kuthi `Δ 1200 < 90°`.
- **Imilayezo** phakathi, njengaleyo ethi "Ortho ivuliwe" noma "Ingqimba yamanje: Walls". Iyanyamalala ngemva kwemizuzwana embalwa.
- **{{ui:Igridi}}**, **{{ui:Ortho}}**, **{{ui:Polar}}**, **{{ui:Bamba}}**, **{{ui:Landelela}}**, **{{ui:Dyn}}** ne-**{{ui:LWT}}** ngakwesokudla. Ngayinye iyinkinobho yokuguqula; inkinobho ecindeziwe ivuliwe. {{ui:LWT}} ibonisa ubukhulu bemigqa esikrinini (bona [Ubukhulu bemigqa nezinhlobo zemigqa](topic:units-papers-scales#line-weights-and-linetypes)). Ukhamba oluncane eduze kwe-{{ui:Bamba}} livula {{ui:Ukubamba izinto}}, lapho ukhetha khona ukubamba okusebenzayo. Bona [Bamba amaphoyinti ezintweni](topic:object-snaps).

## Ingxoxo yokushicilela ku-PDF

{{ui:Dweba}} (noma {{key:Mod+P}}) ivula ingxoxo ye-{{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}}, izilungiselelo ngakwesobunxele nokubukwa okuphilayo ngakwesokudla. Bona [Shicilela ku-PDF](topic:plot-pdf).

## Usizo

{{key:F1}} noma inkinobho ye-{{ui:Usizo}} ivula lolu siza njengephaneli ecaleni; umdwebo usasebenziseka ecaleni kwalo. Lusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhumeka. {{key:F1}} futhi, noma {{key:Esc}} ngaphakathi kwephaneli, iyayivala. Bona [Ukusebenzisa lolu siza](common:help).
