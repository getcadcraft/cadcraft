## Okuzokwenza

Tshela i-CadCraft ukuthi umdwebo omusha kufanele uqale ngani, ukuze ofisini kungadingeki muntu ukuthi asebenzise i-UNITS futhi ashintshe izinto ezifanayo njalo. Lesi yi-add-on: sivaliwe kuze kube yilapho usivula, futhi inqubomgomo ongayisethi ishiya izilungiselelo zeCadCraft ngendlela yazo.

:::note
Lezi zizinqubomgomo zomdwebo **omusha** nezishidi **ezintsha**. Umdwebo osuqalile ngaphambili awushintshwa, futhi nomdwebo ovulwayo awushintshwa.
:::

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula Izilungiselelo, uye ku-{{ui:Okwengeziwe}} bese uvula {{ui:Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela}}.
2. Ku-Izilungiselelo, vula ithebhu ethi **CadCraft** bese ukhetha {{ui:Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela}} kuhlu oluseceleni kwesobunxele.
3. Setha izinqubomgomo ozifunayo. Ngayinye iqala ku-**CadCraft's own** (yeCadCraft). Inqubomgomo enebhokisi ethi **Metric** ne-**Imperial** ithatha inani elilodwa ngohlobo ngalunye lomdwebo; gcwalisa ezidingekayo kuphela.
4. Akukho inkinobho yokugcina: ushintsho ngalunye luyagcinwa njengoba wenza.
5. Qala umdwebo omusha. Unayo amayunithi, ifomethi yobude, ukunemba kanye nokuphakama kombhalo owasethe, nesishidi sokuqala esisha siphepheni osikhethile.

## Izinqubomgomo

- **Amayunithi omdwebo omusha**: lutho, amamillimitha, amasentimitha, amamitha, amayintshi noma amafiti.
- **Ifomethi yobude** ne-**Ukunemba kobude**: lokho umyalo we-UNITS ukusetha, womdwebo omusha.
- **Ukuphakama kombhalo omusha**, ngamayunithi omdwebo.
- **Iphepha lesishidi elisha**: iphepha elisetshenziswa yi-{{ui:New sheet}}.
- **Usayizi wezichazi ephepheni** (Izilungiselelo › Jikelele › Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela): ukuphakama okufunwa yi-ofisi ngamamillimitha njengoba kuphrintwa. I-CadCraft isebenzisa leyo yamanothi namalebuli: {{cmd:ANNOSCALE}} isetha ukuphakama komhlaba omusha kulokho ekukalweni kwencazelo. Bona [Amayunithi, amaphepha nezikali](topic:units-papers-scales#annotate-by-scale).

Umdwebo ngamayintshi noma amafiti usebenzisa amanani e-Imperial; noma yiluphi olunye udwebo usebenzisa amanani e-Metric. Umdwebo ongenayo amayunithi usebenzisa uhlelo lwamayunithi olusethwe ku-Izilungiselelo › Jikelele › Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela, noma i-Metric uma lungakasethwa.

## Yabelana ngazo ngeofisi

1. Ku-Izilungiselelo, vula ithebhu ethi **Jikelele** bese ukhetha {{ui:Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela}}.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza kwifayela}}. Uthola ifayela elincane elige ngo `.ccdefaults.json`.
3. Kwenye ikhompyutha, vula i-add-on, vula indawo efanayo bese uchofoza {{ui:Layisha kusuka kwifayela}}. Izinqubomgomo zefayela zithatha indawo yezalapho kwikhompyutha.

Ifayela elilodwa libamba izinqubomgomo zazo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft, ngakho ifayela elifanayo lisebenza ne-BimCraft.

## Okufanele wazi

- Akukho nqubomgomo yesikali sesishidi: umbono wesishidi esisha ulingana nomdwebo, futhi usetha isikali sawo esishidini.
- Akukho nqubomgomo yesitayela sezilinganiso: umdwebo omusha awunazo izitayela zezilinganiso ezinamagama. Setha izilinganiso nge-DIMSTYLE, noma qala kumdwebo onawo.
- Ifayela liyi-JSON evulekile, elula. Inqubomgomo efayeleni elibhalayo engaziwa yile nguqulo, iyashiywa ibe ikhonjwe emyalezweni; okunye kuyalayisheka.
- Izinqubomgomo zigcinwa kulesi sibhrawuza, hhayi emdwebweni. Vala i-add-on bese konke kubuyela ku-CadCraft njengoba kunjalo.
