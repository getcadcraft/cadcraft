Bhala igama elifushane (i-alias) bese ucindezela Enter, noma uchofoze ithuluzi. Esc iyakhansela. I-Enter emugqeni ongenalutho iphinda umyalo wokugcina.

**Phrinta:** [cheat-sheet.pdf](https://getcadcraft.com/assets/apps/cadcraft/current/cheat-sheet.pdf) (ikhasi elilodwa le-A4; ephepheni le-Letter phrinta nge-"fit to page").

## Dweba

| Bhala | Umyalo | Ukwenzani |
|---|---|---|
| `L` | {{cmd:LINE}} | Dweba imigqa eqondile |
| `PL` | {{cmd:PLINE}} | Dweba ucingo lomugqa nomnsalo oxhunyiwe |
| `C` | {{cmd:CIRCLE}} | Dweba indilinga (isikhungo nerediyasi) |
| `A` | {{cmd:ARC}} | Dweba umnsalo |
| `REC` | {{cmd:RECTANG}} | Dweba unxantathu ngamakhona amabili |
| `POL` | {{cmd:POLYGON}} | Dweba ipholigoni elinganayo |
| `EL` | {{cmd:ELLIPSE}} | Dweba ielipsi |
| `DT` | {{cmd:TEXT}} | Bhala umugqa owodwa wombhalo |
| `T` | {{cmd:MTEXT}} | Bhala umbhalo ophindela emugqeni olandelayo (isigaba) |
| `H` | {{cmd:HATCH}} | Gcwalisa indawo ngephethini |

## Izinkinobho

Ctrl (Cmd ku-Mac): Z hlehlisa · Y phinda wenze · S londoloza · P shicilela.  F3 ukubamba izinto · F8 ortho · F10 polar.  Space noma Enter iphinda umyalo wokugcina · isondo lisondeza · ukudonsa isondo kushelelisa
