## Okuzokwenza

Ishidi le-A3 elinebhulokhi lesihloko, lonke uhlelo esibukweni sokubuka esisodwa ku-1:100, nesibuko sokubuka sesibili esibonisa ikhona elilodwa ku-1:20.

## Imodeli namashidi

Wakha isakhiwo ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}}, ngobukhulu obugcwele: udonga olu-6 m lunguyunithi ezingu-6000 ubude ngamamilimitha. **Ishidi** (layout) liyiphepha, lilinganiswa ngamamilimitha noma ngamainshi. **Isibuko sokubuka** yiwindi elisikwe kuleshidi elibonisa imodeli ngesikali.

## Ngaphambi kokuqala

1. Vula noma udwebe uhlelo ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}}.
2. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze kungakhethwa lutho.
3. Ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}}, setha {{ui:Amayunithi}} kuyunithi odwebe ngayo, njengo {{ui:Amamilimitha}}.

Amayunithi abalulekile: isikali sesibuko sokubuka njenge-1:100 sisho ukuthi i-millimeter eyodwa ephepheni ikhombisa amamilimitha angu-100 empeleni, ngakho i-CadCraft kudingeka ukwazi ukuthi iyunithi eyodwa yomdwebo iyini. Umdwebo ongashintshiwe njenge-{{ui:Akunayunithi}} uthathwa njengosezingeni lephepha (amamilimitha ephepheni le-ISO, amainshi ephepheni le-US).

## Yenza ishidi

1. Chofoza inkinobho {{ui:+}} ngakwesokudla kwamathebhu angaphansi komdwebo, noma bhala `LAYOUT` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Ishidi elisha}}.

Ithebhu elisha, `Layout1`, liyavula. Kufanele ubone ishidi etafuleni elimnyama kancane: umkhawulo, ibhulokhi lesihloko ekhoneni elingaphansi kwesokudla nesibuko sokubuka esisodwa phezu kwalo esibonisa lonke imodeli ngesikali esikhulu esijwayelekile esilingana. Umlando uqamba iphepha: ISO A3 emidwebeni yemetrikhi nengenayunithi, Tabloid (ANSI B) emidwebeni ngamainshi noma amafidi.

2. Chofoza {{ui:Konke}} ukuze ulinganise ishidi esikrinini.

## Khetha iphepha

1. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze kungakhethwa lutho. Ikhanda lephaneli yesokudla lifunda "Ishidi: Layout1".
2. Ku-{{ui:Igama}}, bhala `A-101 Plan` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ithebhu iyaqanjwa kabusha.
3. Ku-{{ui:Iphepha}}, khetha usayizi.
4. Ku-{{ui:Ukuma}}, khetha {{ui:Okuthe tshe}} noma {{ui:Okumile}}.

:::warning
Ukushintsha iphepha akuhambisi noma akulinganisi okusevele kuleshidi. Emva kokushintsha, hlola ukuthi umkhawulo, ibhulokhi lesihloko nesibuko sokubuka usalingana, bese uwahambisa noma ulinganise ngezibambo. Ukushintsha phakathi kwamaphepha amamilimitha namainshi kugcina izinombolo, ngakho konke kushintsha usayizi: isilawuli sesimo siyakuxwayisa.
:::

## Setha isikali sesibuko sokubuka

1. Chofoza uhlaka lwesibuko sokubuka (umphetho waso). Ikhanda lephaneli lifunda "Isibuko sokubuka".
2. Ku-{{ui:Isikali}}, khetha `1:100`.

Imodeli ngaphakathi kwesibuko sokubuka idwetshwa kabusha ku-1:100, igxile lapho ibisiphi khona. Uhlu lunikeza izikali zemetrikhi ukusuka ku-10:1 kuya ku-1:10000; imidwebo ngamainshi noma ngamafidi nayo ithola izikali zokwakha (1/4" = 1'-0" nezinye ezinjalo) nezikali zobunjiniyela (1" = 20' nezinye ezinjalo). {{ui:Lwanela imodeli yonke}} isetha isikali esithile, esingajwayelekile esilingana konke.

3. Uma uhlelo lungasagxili, shintsha {{ui:X}} ne-{{ui:Y}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Isikhungo sombono (imodeli)}} kuphaneli yesokudla: yiphoyinti lemodeli elibonakala phakathi kwesibuko sokubuka.

## Bonisa ingxenye yemodeli esibukweni sokubuka

1. Isibuko sokubuka sikhethiwe, chofoza {{ui:Khetha iwindi kwimodeli…}} kuphaneli yesokudla (noma bhala `VPVIEW` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}).
2. I-CadCraft ishintshela ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}}. Chofoza ikhona elilodwa lendawo ofuna ukuyibonisa.
3. Chofoza ikhona eliphambene.

Ubuyela kuleshidi, futhi isibuko sokubuka sibonisa leyo ndawo ngesikali esikhulu esijwayelekile esilingana; umlando uyasho ukuthi yiliphi. {{ui:Bonisa imodeli yonke}} ibuyisela uhlelo lonke ngesikali esijwayelekile.

## Engeza isibuko sokubuka sesibili sembonini

1. Yenza indawo ishidini: khetha isibuko sokubuka sokuqala bese uhudula isibambo sekhona ukuze silincane.
2. Bhala `MV` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Isibuko sokubuka}} eqenjini lamashidi.
3. Chofoza ikhona elilodwa lesibuko sokubuka esisha ishidini.
4. Chofoza ikhona eliphambene.

Isibuko sokubuka esisha sibonisa imodeli yonke. Umlando uyasho isikali sayo.

5. Chofoza uhlaka lwesibuko sokubuka esisha.
6. Chofoza {{ui:Khetha iwindi kwimodeli…}} bese ukhetha indawo encane ezungeze ikhona elilodwa lohlelo.
7. Ku-{{ui:Isikali}}, khetha `1:20`.

Izinhlaka zezibuko sokubuka zisongqimbeni `Viewports`, osethwe ukungashicilelwa, ngakho izinhlaka zibonakala esikrinini kodwa hhayi ku-PDF.

## Gcwalisa ibhulokhi lesihloko

Ibhulokhi lesihloko yenziwe ngemigqa nombhalo ojwayelekile esongqimbeni `Title block`. Iqala ngegama lomdwebo, isikali sesibuko sokubuka sokuqala, usuku lwanamuhla nenombolo yeshidi.

1. Chofoza kabili umbhalo wesihloko omkhulu. Ibhokisi lokuhlela livula phezu kwawo.
2. Bhala isihloko seshidi.
3. Chofoza {{ui:KULUNGILE}}, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}}.

Yenza okufanayo kwezinye izinkambu. Sebenzisa {{cmd:TEXT}} ukubhala eseleni elingenalutho elithi "DRAWN BY". Umbhalo we-SCALE awuzibuyekezi uma ushintsha isikali sesibuko sokubuka: uhlele ngesandla.

## Dweba ishidini

Konke odwebayo ethebhini leshidi kusephepheni, ngamayunithi ephepha: umbhalo ophakamele u-3.5 uphakama ngu-3.5 mm ishidi le-ISO. Amanothi, imicibisholo yenyakatho nemikhawulo yeshidi kufanele kube lapha.

Ukuze ushintshe uhlelo uqobo, hamba ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}}, noma usebenze ngesibuko sokubuka (isigaba esilandelayo); zonke izibuko sokubuka zibonisa lenguquko.

## Sebenza emodelini ngesibuko sokubuka

1. Chofoza kabili phakathi kwesibuko sokubuka, kude nohlaka lwaso. Noma bhala `MS` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: uma kunesibuko sokubuka esisodwa ishidi, ingena ngqo; uma sikhona esiningi, chofoza kuleyo ofuna.

Uhlaka lwedwetshwa lunzima kakhudlwana, umlando uyasho ukuthi usengaphakathi, nethebhu yeshidi ihlala ikhethiwe. Konke odwebayo noma oshintshayo manje kumodeli, ngamayunithi emodeli, njengakwithebhu le-{{ui:Imodeli}}: umugqa odwetshwe ngaphakathi kwesibuko sokubuka ku-1:100 uyi-millimeter yangempela. Izingqimba ezimisiwe kulesi sibuko sokubuka azikwazi ukukhethwa lapha.

2. Phendulela ivili noma hudula ngenkinobho ephakathi ukusondeza nokuhambisa okubonisa isibuko sokubuka. Ishidi lihlala lapho lisekhona.
3. Chofoza kabili noma ngaphandle kohlaka, noma bhala `PS` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Ubuyele ekishidini. Isibuko sokubuka sigcina umbono ohamba nawo; {{key:Mod+Z}} ibuyisela umbono wakuqala ngesinyathelo esisodwa. Ukuchofoza noma yiluphi uthebhu ngaphansi komdwebo nakho kuphuma esibukweni sokubuka.

:::note
Isibuko sokubuka esikhiyiwe ukubonisa ({{ui:Ukubonisa kuvaliwe}} kuIzakhiwo) sigcina umbono waso: ngaphakathi kwaso, ukuhamba nokusondeza kuhambisa ishidi lonke. Isibuko sokubuka esivaliwe akunakungeniswa.
:::

## Fakela umbono igama

Isihloko sombono ngaphansi kwesibuko sokubuka singabonisa igama lombono wesibuko sokubuka futhi silandele lona:

1. Nikeza umbono igama: khetha uhlaka lwesibuko sokubuka bese ugcwalisa {{ui:Igama lombukwa}} ku-Izakhiwo — noma londoloza umbono ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}} nge-{{cmd:NAMEDVIEW}} bese, isibuko sokubuka sikhethiwe, bhala `VPVIEW`, `V` negama lombono: isibuko sokubuka sibonisa lowo mbono futhi sithatha igama lawo.
2. Bhala `VIEWLABEL` (`VL`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese uchofoza lapho ilebula liya khona ({{key:Enter}}ililethe ngaphansi kwekhona elingaphansi kwesobunxele lesibuko sokubuka).

Uma igama lombono lishintsha, ilebula liyashintsha nalo, ngesinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esifanayo.

## Misa ungqimba kwesibuko sokubuka esisodwa

Ukuze ufihle ungqimba kwesibuko sokubuka esisodwa kodwa hhayi kwezinye (izinto zasendlini embonini, nje):

1. Khetha uhlaka lwesibuko sokubuka, noma usebenze ngaphakathi kwaso (chofoza kabili).

Iphaneli {{ui:Izingqimba}} ithola enye inkinobho emugqeni ngamunye, nomugqa ophezu kohlu uyakusho lokho.

2. Chofoza leyo nkinobho emugqeni wongqimba.

Ungqimba unyamalala kulesi sibuko sokubuka kuphela. Chofoza futhi ukuze uyikhulule; {{key:Mod+Z}} iyahlehlisa noma yiluphi. Ngezingqimba noma izibuko eziningi ngesikhathi esisodwa, bhala `VPLAYER`: [Misa (F)] noma [Khulula (T)], amagama ezingqimba (`A-*` ikhetha zonke ezingqimba eziqala nge-A-), bese [Okwamanje (C)], [Konke (A)] noma [Khetha (S)]. `VPLAYER` `V` (Vpvisdflt) ikhetha izingqimba izibuko sokubuka ezintsha eziqala zimisiwe kuzo.

## Amanye amashidi

Ngalinye {{ui:+}} lengeza ishidi elilandelayo (`Layout2`, `Layout3` …) ngenombolo elandelayo yeshidi. Ukuze usule ishidi elivuliwe, cindezela {{key:Esc}} bese uchofoza {{ui:Susa leli shidi}} kuphaneli yesokudla. Lisuswa ngaphandle kokubuza; {{key:Mod+Z}} ililetha ngakho.

Shicilela ishidi nge-{{ui:Phrinta leli shidi}} noma {{key:Mod+P}}; bona [Shicilela ku-PDF](topic:plot-pdf).

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Isibuko sokubuka asibonisi lutho.** Imodeli iyize, noma umbono ugxilise ngaphandle. Khetha uhlaka bese uchofoza {{ui:Bonisa imodeli yonke}}.
- **Uhlu lwezikali lubonisa izilinganiso kuphela, akukho zikali zamafidi namainshi.** Setha {{ui:Amayunithi}} ku-{{ui:Amainshi}} noma ku-{{ui:Amafidi}} ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}}.
- **I-1:100 ibonakala kakhulu noma incane kakhulu izikhathi eziyishumi.** Amayunithi omdwebo akalungile. Setha {{ui:Amayunithi}} kukho okuyiyunithi eyodwa empeleni.
- **Ukuchofoza uhlelo ngaphakathi kwesibuko sokubuka akukhethi lutho.** Kuleshidi ukhetha izinto zeshidi uqobo. Chofoza kabili phakathi kwesibuko sokubuka kuqala ukuze usebenze ohlelweni ngaso.
- **Ukuchofoza ngaphandle kwesibuko sokubuka akwenzi lutho.** Useseceleni kwesibuko sokubuka: chofoza kabili ngaphandle kwaso, noma bhala `PS`, ukuze usebenze kuleshidi futhi.
- **"Viewports go on a layout. Open a layout tab (or make one with LAYOUT) first."** {{cmd:MVIEW}} isebenza kuphela ethebhini leshidi.
