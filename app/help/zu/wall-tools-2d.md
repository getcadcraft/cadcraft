Lezi zinsiza zisebenza kodonga lwe-2D olusuka ku-`WALL` (bheka "Dweba udonga, izinmnyango namawindi nge-2D"). Zisebenzisa imithetho efanayo nezinsiza zodonga ku-BimCraft, ngakho udonga lwenza ngendlela efanayo kuzo zombili.

## Lapho zikhona khona izinsiza

- Kuthuluzi, iqembu le-{{ui:Udonga}} linezinkinobho ezithi {{ui:Udonga}}, {{ui:Unxantathu}}, {{ui:Hlukanisa}} ne-{{ui:Okuphambanisiwe}}. Imenyu yalo ye-{{ui:Okuningi}} inazo zonke ezinye izinsiza zodonga, ngayinye nesithonjana sayo.
- Ezitayelweni zemenyu yeribhoni, ithebhu ye-{{ui:Udonga}} inamaphaneli {{ui:Dweba udonga}}, {{ui:Sika}}, {{ui:Setha futhi uguqule}}, {{ui:Bamba}} ne-{{ui:Hlanganisa}}.
- Esakhiweni, imigqa yezinsiza nebhokisi lezinsiza zikhombisa iqembu elifanayo le-{{ui:Udonga}}.
- Ungabhala noma yimuphi umyalo obizwe ngezansi.

## Okuhlanganiswe kugcinwe

Lapho ukuphela komdonga kufika kolunye udonga, i-CadCraft igcina lokho kuhlanganiswa kodwa kokubili odongeni. Ayisasebenzisi ukusebenza ngokuhlanganiswa ngokuya ngokuthi amaphethelo afike kuphi. Ngakho:

- Odonga abahlangene nodonga oluhanjiswayo balandela bese bahlala behlanganisiwe. Amaphetheli abo ashelela emigqeni yawo, ngakho akukho okuphenduka.
- Donga esinye isiphetho sodonga ngesibambi sawo bese udonga olulandela khona lugcina nalo isiqondiso salo. Isiphetho sawo sishelela emgqeni wawo ukuze sihlangane nodonga oludonselwe, ngakho isiphetho esidonselwe sifika emgqeni lowo, hhayi ngaso sonke isikhathi ngaphansi kwe-cursor. Ukubuka kwandulela kukhombisa lapho. Udonga olungena kodonga oludonselwe lulandela emgqeni walo. Ukudonsa isiphetho ngqo ngodonga kushintsha ubude balo, futhi odonga abahlangene nalo bayahamba nalo.
- I-`ROTATE` kodonga oluhlangene isebenza ngendlela efanayo: amaphetheli abomakhelwane bayo ashelela emigqeni yawo kodonga oluphendukile.
- Ukuhambisa ikhona, khetha bobabili odonga abahlangana khona bese udonsa isibambi kwikhona. Bobabili odonga bahlala ekhoneni futhi bobabili bayaphenduka. Odonga abasemuva bahlala njengoba babekunjalo.
- Bamba {{key:Alt}} lapho uchofoza indawo entsha yesibambi ukuze uphule ukuhlanganiswa ngenhloso: udonga luya lapho ulubeke khona futhi akukho okulandelayo.
- Uma ukudonsa kuzophendula udonga oluhlangene ngaphansi komphetho waso omude, kuyenqatshwa futhi akukho okushintshayo.
- Udonga ohudulwe ngu-{{ui:Vimbela ukuhlanganiswa}} (`D` ku-`WALL`), noma ovalwe ku-Properties ({{ui:Vimbela ukuhlanganiswa ekuqaleni}}, {{ui:Vimbela ukuhlanganiswa ekugcineni}}), uhlala ukhululekile kulowo mphetho, nakuba uthinta enye udonga, uze uvumele futhi.
- I-`WALLDISJOIN` ({{ui:Vimbela ukuhlanganiswa}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Udonga}}) yenza okufanayo ngokuchofoza: chofoza udonga oseduze nesiphetho ukuze uvimbele ukuhlanganiswa kuleso siphetho, bese uchofoza futhi ukuze uvumele.
- Idrowing elidala alinayo imihlanganiswa egciniwe okwamanje. Ushintsho lokuqala owenzayo luyayitholela kanye, kusuka ekumeni kodonga ngaphambi koshintsho, bese luyayigcina. I-`WALLJOINS` yenza lokhu ngesikhathi esisodwa.
- Amafayela e-DWG ne-DXF aphatha imihlanganiswa ngaphakathi kodonga ngalunye. I-CadCraft iyayifunda njengoba igciniwe. Uhlelo olungagcini idatha eyengeziwe lubonisa odonga njengamabhulokhi ajwayelekile futhi lulahle imihlanganiswa, nokuhlanganiswa kodonga abahanjiswe kolunye uhlelo kulahlwa uma kushintshwa noma yiluphi udonga.

## Isitayela sokuhlanganisa ikhona

Ikhona lapho odonga ababili bahlangana ngokuphelela kuqondiswa njenge-mitre, ngaphandle uma ukhetha esinye isitayela.

- `WALLJOINSTYLE` ({{ui:Isitayela sokuhlanganisa}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Udonga}}): chofoza ikhona. Ukuchofoza ngakunye kuya esitayelweni esilandelayo: {{ui:Mitre}}, {{ui:Butt}}, {{ui:Square off}}, bese {{ui:Mitre}} futhi. Bhala `T` ukuze ushintshe ukuthi yiyiphi kodwa ababili odonga owedlula ekhoneni owachofoza ekugcineni. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuphela. Ukusebenza kwesimiyalo kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
- {{ui:Butt}}: udonga olodwa lweqa lufinyelele ubuso bangaphandle bodonga lolunye, nolunye lumile ngokuchukumisa ngakolunye uhlangothi. {{ui:Square off}}: kufana, kodwa udonga oluhamba luphela lunqamula ngokuqondile kuwo. Ekhoneni elingqondile zombili ziyafana.
- Ku-Properties kodonga: {{ui:Isitayela sokuhlanganisa ekuqaleni}}, {{ui:Isitayela sokuhlanganisa ekugcineni}}, nakuthi {{ui:Ihamba kuqala}}, {{ui:Ihamba ekugcineni}}.
- Isitayela sigcinwa kodonga bobabili futhi sihamba kumafayela e-DWG ne-DXF. Ikhophi lodonga liqala ngaphandle kwaso.
- Ukuhlanganisa kwe-T akunasitayela sokukhetha: udonga oluthinte luhamba luyodlula. Ikhona lapho odonga abathathu noma ngaphezulu bahlangana lihlala lingu-mitre. Kanjalo nekhona elibukhali kakhulu (ngaphansi cishe kuka-30 degrees) nodonga ababili emugqeni owodwa.

## Dweba izimo zodonga

Ngayinye yenza odonga ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, namakhona ahlanganisiwe. Ku-`WALL`, `O` isetha ukuthambekela kokubekwa (udonga luhamba kangakanani ngakwesobunxele kokuchofoza kwakho) kanti `D` ivala ukuhlanganiswa kodonga obadwetsha ngokulandelayo.

- `WALLRECT` (`WR`): amakhona amabili aphambene, njenge-`RECTANG`.
- `WALLROT`: uhlangothi lokuqala, bese kuthi uhlangothi lolunye lukhuphuke kangakanani, ukuze kube unxantathu ophendukele noma yiyiphi ingosi.
- `WALLPOLY` (`WP`): inani lezinhlangothi (3 kuya ku-64), isiphakathi, `I` ye-inscribed noma `C` ye-circumscribed, bese iradiyasi, njenge-`POLYGON`: chofoza, noma uyibhale (iradiyasi ebhaliwe ihamba ngokuya ku-pointer, noma ngokuya ku-X uma i-pointer isasesiphakathini).
- `WALLARC` (`WARC`): isiphakathi, isiqalo, isiphetho. `D` iphendula umnsalo ngendlela ehlukile.
- `WALLTAN`: chofoza eduze kwesiphetho sodonga, bese isiphetho somnsalo, ohamba ngaleso sodonga.
- `WALLPICK`: chofoza umugqa noma iphola-layini bese uthi ingabe ufuna nemigqa ehlangene nawo.
- `WALLTRACE`: chofoza ngaphakathi endaweni evaliwe yemigqa nodonga ukuze ubeke odonga bayo obancane kakhulu.

Izindlela zodonga azigobile, ngakho umnsalo udwetshwa njengodonga abafushane abangu-15 degrees ngamunye. Bahlangana njengamakhona ajwayelekile.

## Sika odonga

- `WALLSPLIT` (`WS`): chofoza udonga, bese iphoyinti. `C` isika lapho enye udonga ihlangana nalo, `G` ishiya igebe phakathi kwezingxenye, `F` yenza iziphetho ezisikiwe zikhululekile esikhundleni sokuhlanganisiwe. Ingxenye yokuqala igcina indawo kadonga emdwebeni, nezinmnyango nezimpandla ziyahamba nengxenye yazo. Ukusika ngemnyango noma iwindi kuyenqatshwa.
- `WALLSPLITGAP` ({{ui:Hlukanisa nesikhala}}): ukuhlukaniswa okufanayo, kodwa ibuza igebe kuqala.
- `WALLDELSEG`: chofoza udonga, bese amaphoyinti amabili endawo ofuna ukuyisusa.
- `WALLREJOIN`: chofoza izingxenye ezimbili zodonga oyedwa ukuze zibe udonga oyedwa futhi.

## Hambisa odonga ngebanga

- `WALLDIM` ({{ui:Hlela ngesilinganiso}}): chofoza isilinganiso esiqondile noma esihambisanayo esisuka kodonga kuye kolunye, bese ubhala inani elisha. Udonga osemgqeni ophelile luhambe luqonde ngokuzimela, udonga osemgqeni wokuqala luhlale lapho, nodonga abahlangene nalo bayalandela. Iphoyinti elikude lesilinganiso lihamba nodonga walo, ngakho isilinganiso sibonisa inani obhale.
- `WALLOFFSET` (`WO`): chofoza udonga, bese uhlangothi lokuwuhambisela kulo (`D` ibhala ibanga, `C` yenza ikhophi esikhundleni).
- `WALLSTRETCH`: iwindi lapho kuzungezwe iziphetho zodonga, bese iphoyinti lesisekelo nephoyinti lesibili.
- Ku-Properties, {{ui:Ubude}} obhaliwe ugcina ukuqala bese uthatha udonga ohlangene nesiphetho esikude nawo, kanti {{ui:Ukujula}} omusha ukhula kusuka {{ui:Umugqa wendawo}} owukhethile (ubuso bokhohlo, ubuso bokudla noma phakathi).

## Ilinganiso phakathi kwemigqa emaphakathi

Umugqa wephakathi lodonga awudwetshwa, kodwa ukubamba into kuwuthola. Uma ukubamba into kusebenza (ngo-{{key:F3}}), hambisa i-pointer phezu kwesiphakathi sobukhulu bodonga: uphawu luthi `Wall center: endpoint` noma `Wall center: midpoint`, futhi umugqa wephakathi ubonakala njengomugqa ochashaziwe ngenkathi uphawu lusemugqeni. Uma {{ui:Perpendicular}} noma {{ui:Nearest}} kuvuliwe ohlwini lokubamba izinto, lezo nazo zisebenza emugqeni wephakathi.

1. Qala `DIMLINEAR` (noma `DIMALIGNED`).
2. Chofoza udonga wokuqala lapho uphawu lufunda `Wall center: midpoint` noma `Wall center: endpoint`.
3. Chofoza udonga wesibili ngendlela efanayo, bese uchofoza lapho umugqa wesilinganiso ufika khona.
4. Ukuze ushintse ibanga, qala `WALLDIM`, chofoza isilinganiso bese ubhala inani elisha. Udonga wesibili uyahamba.

Ukubamba emugqeni wephakathi kusebenza kuyo yonke imiyalo ebuza iphoyinti.

## Khiya odonga

Ukukhiya kuhlala nodonga efayeleni. Uma uhambisa udonga oyedwa okhiyiwe, obunye buyalandela. Uma ushintsho olulodwa luhambisa odonga bobabili ngendlela ehlukile, luyenqatshwa futhi akukho okushintshayo.

- `WALLHOLD`: khiya udonga kodonga lolunye. `A` iwuhambisa kumgqa wodonga lolunye kuqala, `K` igcina ibanga alinalo.
- `WALLHOLDDIM`: khiya ibanga phakathi kodonga ababili abahambisanayo.
- `WALLGRID`: khiya udonga emgqeni wegridi (umugqa oseqenjini legridi negama lawo emBhalweni ngesinye sesiphetho), ngokuwuhamba noma nesiphetho sibe kuwo. Udonga okhiywe ngomugqa ugcina ibanga alinalo kuwo. Hambisa umugqa wegridi nge-`GRIDMOVE` bese udonga uyalandela uphelele, nodonga abahlangene nawo. Udonga oluhamba emgqeni wegridi luhamba lonke nawo ngisho nangaphandle kokukhiya, nodonga oluphela kuwo kuphela luyelulwa ngaleso siphetho.
- `WALLHOLDS` ihlu ukukhiya. `WALLUNHOLD` isusa ukukhiya kodonga.
- Ukukhiya ngakunye kubonisa ikhiye elincane esakhiweni: eceleni kwesiphakathi sodonga okhiyiwe, noma eceleni kwesiphetho esikhiywe emgqeni wegridi. Chofoza ikhiye ukuze ususe lowo mkhiya.

## Lokho okungakakho

Ukukhiya kusebenzisa imigqa emaphakathi kodonga. Odonga lapha abanawo amaqembu, ngakho akukho ukuqala kwezingqimba ekuhlanganeni, futhi odonga abagobi (bheka amagobolondo ngenhla). Lezi zinsiza azikasetshenziswa ngabantu abaningi ekhasini elikhulu, futhi azihlolwanga kolunye uhlelo lwe-CAD.
