## Okusebenzayo

I-DWG eyenziwe ngamabhulokhi aguquguqukayo ivulwa nawo esebenza kulokhu:

- **Amagama awo angempela.** Ibhulokhi eguquguqukayo ibhalwe ohlwini lwepaneli ye-{{ui:Blocks}} ngegama lalo ("Receptacle", "Sink"), hhayi njengebhulokhi engenagama `*U`.
- **Isimo ayalondolozwa kuso.** Inkomba ngayinye ikhombisa kahle lokho okukhombisile enye inhlelo: isimo sokubonakala esimiselwe kuso, elulwe noma eflipwe njengoba alondolozwa.
- **Ukushintsha isimo sokubonakala.** Khetha inkomba eyodwa (noma ezimbalwa zebhulokhi efanayo), vula {{ui:Properties}}, bese ku-isigaba {{ui:Custom}} ukhetha esinye isimo oluhlwini lwe-{{ui:Visibility}}. Inkomba ikhombisa leso simo masinyane, nezici zayo: ezifihliwe yisimo ziyanyamalala, ezibonisiwe yisona ziyavela namanani azo. Inkomba igcina wonke amanani, ngakho akukho okushiyeka kungenalutho; {{ui:Properties}} ibhala ezifihliwe ngokuthi "hidden". {{key:Mod+Z}} ibuyisela isimo sakudala esinyathelweni esisodwa.
- **Ama-grip.** Ibhulokhi eguquguqukayo ekhethiwe ibonisa ama-grip ayo lapho amapharamitha ayo akhona: unxantathu omncane wesimo sokubonakala (chofoza wona ukuze uthole uhlu lwezimo), imicibisholo emagumbini ebanga, umcibisholo ophindwe kabili we-flip. Ukusebenza ngesimo sokubonakala kuphela kwenzeka khona manje; ezinye zithi kubha yesimo.
- **Amanye amanani, afundeka kuphela.** Ngaphansi koluhlu lokubonakala, {{ui:Custom}} futhi ibonisa amabanga webhulokhi, amakona okuhlanganisa, izimo ze-flip namaphoyinti njengoba idrowing iwabambe.
- **Ukuhlela izimo zokubonakala.** Vula ibhulokhi nge-{{cmd:BEDIT}}: umhleli ubonisa isimo esisodwa, esibizwa ngegama kwibha ngaphezulu kwedrowing (khetha esinye lapho). Thayipha {{cmd:BVSTATE}} ukushintsha, ukungeza (ukufihla konke, ukubonisa konke, noma ukugcina okubonakalayo), ukuqamba kabusha noma ukususa isimo; {{cmd:BVHIDE}} ifihla izinto ezikhethiwe esimweni esibonakalayo; {{cmd:BVSHOW}} ibonisa ezifihliwe zinyanyekile ukuze ukhethe ezikufanele ukubuyisa. Gcina ibhulokhi: izinkomba ezisethelwe isimo esiqanjwe kabusha ziyasigcina ngegama lakhe elisha, nezisethelwe isimo esisusiwe zibonisa isimo sokuqala.
- **Kuhlala kuguquguquka.** Lapho silondolozwa njenge-DWG enguqulweni idrowing yafika ngayo, amabhulokhi ahlala eguquguquka, nesimo osikhethile: enye inhlelo iwavula njengamabhulokhi afanayo aguquguqukayo. Izimo zokubonakala owazihlelile nazo ziyalondolozwa: amagama awo amasha, izimo owazengezile nokuthi ngayinye ibonisa ini.

## Okungafundeki kuphela noma akukakufiki

| Akukafiki | Okwamanje |
|---|---|
| Ukushintsha amabanga, amakona noma ama-flip (izenzo zokwelula, zokuhambisa, zokuflipha) | Akhombisa afunda kuphela ku-{{ui:Custom}}; hlela ohlelweni olwenza ibhulokhi. |
| Lookup, XY, ukuzungeza namapharamitha wesiqondo | Ayikafundwa: inkomba ibonakala njengoba yalondolozwa. |
| Ukudonsa ama-grip amabanga, ama-flip namaphoyinti | Akhomba lapho amapharamitha akhona; i-grip yokubonakala ne-{{ui:Visibility}} oluhlwini lwe-Properties ziguqula izimo. |
| Ukwenza amabhulokhi aguquguqukayo (ukungeza amapharamitha, noma izimo zokubonakala ebhulokhini elingenazo) | Amabhulokhi owenza ku-CadCraft ayizinto ezilula; {{cmd:BPARAMETER}} ikutshela lokho. |
| Ukungeza noma ukususa izinto zebhulokhi elinezimo zokubonakala kumhleli webhulokhi | Ibhulokhi liyalondolozwa njengebhulokhi elilula (umhleli uyakutshela lapho evala). |
| Ukuwagcina eguquguqukayo kuguqulo ehlukile ye-DWG noma ku-DXF | Alondolozwa njengamabhulokhi alula akhombisa isimo osikhethile, nokulondoloza kuyakutshela lokho. |

:::note
Ukuhamba noma ukushintsha izinto ku-{{cmd:BEDIT}} kugcina ibhulokhi elinezimo zokubonakala eguquguquka. Ibhulokhi elingenazo izimo, noma elengezwe noma lasuswa izinto zalo kumhleli, liba ibhulokhi elilula.
:::
