## Okusebenzayo

Ifayela le-DWG elenziwe ngamabhulokhi aguquguqukayo livula nawo lisebenza kulokhu:

- **Amagama awo wangempela.** Ibhulokhi eguquguqukayo ibhalwa kuphaneli ye-{{ui:Amabhulokhi}} ngegama layo ("Receptacle", "Sink"), hhayi njengebhulokhi `*U` elingenagama.
- **Isimo esagcinwa kuso.** Isinkomba ngasinye sibonisa okufanayo nalokho okwakubonisa uhlelo lolunye: isimo sokubonakala esasethwe kuso, eselulwe noma esiphenduliwe njengoba sagcinwa.
- **Ukushintsha isimo sokubonakala.** Khetha isinkomba esisodwa (noma amabhulokhi amaningana afanayo), vula {{ui:Izakhiwo}}, bese ukhetha esinye isimo ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Ukubonakala}} esigabeni se-{{ui:Okwezifiso}}. Isinkomba sibonisa leso simo masinyane, nezici zaso: ezifihlwa yisimo ziyanyamalala, ezibonakala ngaso ziyavela nezinani zazo. Isinkomba sigcina zonke izinani, ngakho akukho okuphuma kungenalutho; i-{{ui:Izakhiwo}} ihlu ezifihliwe ilebula ngokuthi "hidden". {{key:Mod+Z}} ibuyisela isimo sakuqala esinyathelweni esisodwa.
- **Izibambi.** Ibhulokhi eguquguqukayo elikhethiwe libonisa izibambi zalo lapho kukhona amapharamitha alo: unxantathu omncane wesimo sokubonakala (uchofoze wona ukuze uthole uhlu lwezimo), izimpandla ezigxiyeni lobude, impandla ephindwe kabili yokuphenduliwe. Okusebenza manje yisibambi sokubonakala kuphela; ezinye zisho lokho kububha besimo.
- **Amanye amanani, afundeka kuphela.** Ngaphansi kohlu lokubonakala, i-{{ui:Okwezifiso}} futhi ibonisa amabanga ebhulokhi, izingosi zokuhlelwa, izimo zokuphenduliwa namaphoyinti njengoba umdwebo uwagcina.
- **Ukuhlela izimo zokubonakala.** Vula ibhulokhi nge-{{cmd:BEDIT}}: umhleli ubonisa isimo esisodwa, esichazwe ebhari ngaphezulu komdwebo (khetha esinye lapho). Bhala {{cmd:BVSTATE}} ukushintsha, engeza (ifihla yonke, ibonise yonke noma igcine lokho okubonakalayo), qamba kabusha noma susa isimo; i-{{cmd:BVHIDE}} ifihla izinto ezikhethiwe esimweni esibonakalayo; i-{{cmd:BVSHOW}} ibonisa ezifihliwe zifiphele ukuze ukhethe ezizobuyisela. Londoloza ibhulokhi: izinkomba ezisethwe esimweni esiqanjwe kabusha ziyasigcina ngegama elisha, futhi ezisethwe esimweni esisusiwe zibonisa isimo sokuqala.
- **Kugcina ukuguquguquka.** Olondolozwe njenge-DWG ngoguqulo lomdwebo weza ngalo, amabhulokhi ahlala eguquguquka, nesimo okukhethile: uhlelo lolunye luwavula njengamabhulokhi afanayo aguquguqukayo. Izimo zokubonakala owazihlela nazo ziyalondolozwa: amagama azo amasha, izimo owengeze nokuthi yilowo nalowo ubonisa ini.

## Okufundeka kuphela, noma okungakakho

| Akukakho | Esikhundleni, njengamanje |
|---|---|
| Ukushintsha amabanga, izingosi noma ukuphenduliwa (ukwelula, ukuhambisa, izenzo zokuphenduliwa) | Abonisa kuphela ku-{{ui:Okwezifiso}}; hlela ohlelweni olwenza ibhulokhi. |
| Ukubheka, i-XY, ukuzungezisa namapharamitha e-polar | Akukafundwa: isinkomba sibonisa njengoba sagcinwa. |
| Ukudonsa izibambi zebanga, zokuphenduliwa namaphoyinti | Zibonisa lapho amapharamitha akhona; isibambi sokubonakala nohlu lwe-{{ui:Ukubonakala}} ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}} ashintsha izimo. |
| Ukwenza amabhulokhi aguquguqukayo ohlobo lolunye uhlelo (ukwelula, ukuphenduliwa, ukubheka) | Amabhulokhi owenza ku-CadCraft ayizinto ezijwayelekile; i-{{cmd:BPARAMETER}} iyasho lokho. Amapharamitha akho namapharamitha amaningi okubonakala atholakala kumphathi wamapharamitha (bheka "Amapharamitha akho nezimo zokubonakala" ngezansi). |
| Ukungeza noma ukususa izinto zebhulokhi enezimo zokubonakala kumhleli webhulokhi | Ibhulokhi-ke ilondolozwa njengebhulokhi elijwayelekile (umhleli uyasho lokho uma uvala). |
| Ukubagcina beguquguqukayo koguqulo lwe-DWG olunye noma ku-DXF | Balondolozwa njengamabhulokhi ajwayelekile abonisa isimo okukhethile, nokulondoloza kuyasho lokho. |

:::note
Ukuhambisa noma ukushintsha izinto ku-{{cmd:BEDIT}} kugcina ibhulokhi elinezimo zokubonakala lisaguquguquka. Ibhulokhi elingenazo izimo, noma elinezinto ezingezwe noma ezisulwe kumhleli, liba ibhulokhi elijwayelekile.
:::

## Amapharamitha akho nezimo zokubonakala

Ibhulokhi owenza ku-CadCraft lingaba namapharamitha alo **namapharamitha amaningi** okubonakala, ngayinye inezimo zayo. Bhala {{cmd:BPARAMS}} (noma `BPM`) ukuvula **umphathi wamapharamitha** webhulokhi oliguqulayo nge-{{cmd:BEDIT}}, webhulokhi lesinkomba esikhethiwe, noma ibhulokhi oyiqamba.

- **Amapharamitha**: igama, uhlobo (inombolo, ubude, ingosi, inani, yebo/cha), nenani noma ifomula phezu kwamanye amapharamitha (`w * 2`, `if(n > 3, 1, 0)`). I-{{ui:Ifika ku}} ibonisa umphumela. Ifomula engasebenzi, noma inani elingafanele uhlobo lalo (inani le-1.5), iyenqatshwa futhi umphathi uyasho ngani; akukho okunye okushintshayo.
- **Amapharamitha okubonakala**: i-{{ui:Engeza ipharamitha yokubonakala}} yenza eyodwa enesimo sokuqala. Isimo ngasinye singaqanjwa kabusha, sisuswe (eyokugcina ihlala) noma sinikezwe inombolo ye-"ibonisa lapho". Ifomula ikhetha isimo kumapharamitha (isimo enombolo yaso ehambisana, noma kungenjalo isimo esendaweni efanele ohlwini); uma ingekho ifomula, isinkomba ngasinye sikhetha esasikho.
- **Ukubeka izinto esimweni**: kumhleli webhulokhi khetha izinto, bhala {{cmd:BPARAMS}} bese uchofoza {{ui:Beka izinto ezikhethiwe lapha}} esimweni. Izinto ezingadwetshwe yipharamitha yokubonakala zihlala zibonakala.
- **Lesi sinkomba**: uma uqala esinkombeni esikhethiwe, umphathi ubonisa futhi amanani nezimo zalesi sinkomba.

Ngaphandle komhleli webhulokhi ushintsho ngalunye lungesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Kumhleli webhulokhi ushintsho luyaba olumhleli: {{ui:Londoloza ibhulokhi}} iyagcina. `-BPARAMS` ibuza okufanayo komugqa womyalo.

**Efayelweni.** I-DWG ne-DXF zigcina amapharamitha, ukuthi yiziphi izinto ezisesimweni esithile, namanani esinkomba ngasinye, emarekhodini e-CadCraft; uvula kabusha ifayela ku-CadCraft bese konke kuyabuya. Olunye uhlelo lubona isinkomba ngasinye njengebhulokhi nazo zonke izinto zalo, ngoba i-DWG igcina okungenani ipharamitha eyodwa yokubonakala ngebhulokhi ngalinye lohlobo lwalo. Isinkomba olunye uhlelo olusihambisile, noma ibhulokhi olushintshe izinto zalo, kubuya ngaphandle kwezingxenye ezingasahambisani (akukho okuqagelwayo).
