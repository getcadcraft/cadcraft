## Okuzokufunda

Ungalayisha kanjani inqubo ye-LISP (ifayela le-`.lsp`) ukuze imiyalo yayo isebenze ku-CadCraft, ukuthi uyikhipha kanjani, nokuthi ungayenza kanjani ilayishwe njalo lapho i-CadCraft iqala.

## Layisha inqubo

1. Vula {{ui:Ukubuka}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Layisha uhlelo lokusebenza…}}, noma thayipha {{cmd:APPLOAD}} (noma `AP`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Layisha ifayela…}} bese ukhetha amafayela `.lsp` amaningana. Inqubo evula ingxoxo ivame ukuza nefayela `.dcl`: nalo likhethe.
3. Ingxoxo ichaza ukuthi yiliphi ifayela ngalinye elenza, isibonelo "Layishwe delblocks.lsp: umyalo owodwa, DELBLOCKS."
4. Chofoza {{ui:Vala}}, thayipha umyalo wenqubo bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Ungaphinda uphonse amafayela `.lsp` no-`.dcl` emdwebeni, noma engxoxweni ye-{{ui:Layisha izinhlelo}}: ayalayishwa ngokushesha.

Ifayela lifundwa lapha, kusiphequluli sakho. Akukho okulayishwa.

:::tip
Cindezela {{key:Enter}} komugqa womyalo ongenalutho ukuze uphinde umyalo wenqubo, njengawo wonke amanye imiyalo. Ama-alias emiyalo yakho nawo angafinyelela kuwo: bheka "Hlela ama-alias emiyalo".
:::

## Khipha inqubo

Ku-{{ui:Layisha izinhlelo}}, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izinhlelo ezilayishiwe}}, chofoza {{ui:Khipha}} ngasemuva kwefayela. Imiyalo ayenzile iyanyamalala size uyilayishe futhi.

## Layisha inqubo njalo lapho i-CadCraft iqala

1. Layisha inqubo.
2. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izinhlelo ezilayishiwe}}, chofoza {{ui:Engeza kwiSuthi Yokuqala}} ngasemuva kwayo.

Kusukela manje iyalayisha yodwa njalo lapho i-CadCraft iqala kulesi siphequluli. Ukuyeka lokho, chofoza {{ui:Khipha}} ngasemuva kwayo ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Isuthi Yokuqala}}.

I-{{ui:Isuthi Yokuqala}} igcina ikhophi yefayela ngalinye kusitoreji salesi siphequluli. Uma ushintsha inqubo, layisha ifayela elisha bese uyengeza futhi.

## Layisha acad.lsp no-acaddoc.lsp kusuka ofolda wokusekela (Studio)

Ku-CadCraft Studio, {{ui:Ifolda yokusekela}} engxoxweni ye-{{ui:Layisha izinhlelo}} ithatha ifolda kukhompyutha yakho. Chofoza {{ui:Khetha ifolda…}}. Lapho i-CadCraft iqala ifunda `acad.lsp` bese `acaddoc.lsp` kulowo folda (noma yimuphi uhlobo lwamagama), iyawalayisha, bese iyalayisha i-Startup Suite. `acaddoc.lsp` ifundwa futhi njalo lapho kuvulwa ithebhu yomdwebo omusha. Akukho okunye okufundwa kuwo folda, nefayela elidlula i-2 MB liyadlulwa, ngomlayezo.

Uma ifayela lichaza umsebenzi ogama lawo `S::STARTUP`, lisebenza kanye ngemva kokuthi amafayela alayishiwe (futhi ngemva kwe-Startup Suite). {{ui:Layisha manje}} yenza okufanayo ngesicelo; {{ui:Sula}} ikhohlwa ifolda.

Esiphequluliswini ikhasi alikwazi ukugcina ifolda, ngakho akukho folda yokusekela: sebenzisa i-Startup Suite engenhla. Amafayela `.fas` no-`.vlx` ahlanganisiwe nezinhlelo zomdabu aqhubeka enqatshelwa.

## Layisha ifayela futhi kusuka emlandweni

{{ui:Umlando}} uhlu lwamafayela alayishwe kulesi siphequluli, okusha kuqala. Chofoza {{ui:Layisha}} ngasemuva kwelilodwa ukuze liyelayishwe, ngaphandle kokuliyifuna kukhompyutha yakho.

## Amafayela i-CadCraft engeke ayilayishe

| Ifayela | Yini | Okufanele wenze |
|---|---|---|
| `.fas`, `.vlx` | i-LISP ehlanganisiwe noma evikelwe | layisha umthombo `.lsp` esikhundleni salo |
| `.arx`, `.crx`, `.dbx` | izinhlelo zomdabu ze-Windows | azikho izindlela zokuzisebenzisa ku-CadCraft |
| `.dvb` | iphrojekthi ye-VBA | azikho izindlela zokuzisebenzisa ku-CadCraft |
| `.craft`, `.lua`, `.py` | amascript e-CraftScript | zivule ku-Script workspace ye-PartCraft |

Ingxoxo ye-Layisha izinhlelo itshela ukuthi yiliphi kulezi ifayela ngalinye lapho ulikhetha.

## Misa inqubo

Cindezela {{key:Esc}}. Kwelinye lemimemo yenqubo, u-Esc uyiqeda ngendlela inqubo elindele ngayo (ukuhlanza kwayo kuyasebenza). Uma isebenza futhi ingabuzi lutho, u-Esc uyimisa ngokuphelele futhi ukutshele; izinqubo zakho ezilayishiwe zisalayishiwe.

:::note
Konke inqubo elukushintsha emdwebeni kuyisinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} esisodwa iyakubuyisela konke emuva.
:::
