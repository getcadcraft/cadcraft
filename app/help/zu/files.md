Umdwebo ngamunye owuvulayo (noma oqala nge-{{ui:Okusha}}) uthola ithebhu yawo phezu kwendawo yomdwebo; umdwebo omusha ongakaze uthintwe usetshenziswa esikhundleni sokungeza ithebhu. Chofoza ithebhu ukuze ushintshe, {{ui:×}} yayo ukuze uyivale (i-CadCraft iyakubuza kuqala uma kukhona izinguquko ezingalondolozwanga), noma hlela imidwebo evulekile ecaleni komunye nomunye: bona [Hlukanisa isikrini](topic:split-screen).

## Ukuvula umdwebo

- Cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}, chofoza {{ui:Vula}}, noma bhala `OPEN`, bese ukhetha ifayela.
- Noma hudula ifayela le-`.dwg` noma le-`.dxf` liyiselwe endaweni yomdwebo.

Ifayela lifundwa ngaphakathi kusiphequluli sakho. Alilayishelwa noma yiphi indawo. Umlando ubika ukuthi zingaki izinto nezingqimba ezifikile nokuthi kuthathe isikhathi esingakanani, futhi isigaba sangakwesokunxele esithi {{ui:Umdwebo}} sibonisa ifomethi, njengo "DWG 2018 (AC1032)".

Umfundi we-DWG uyingxenye ye-CadCraft futhi uyalanjwa nge-DWG yokuqala oyivulayo ngemva kokulayisha ikhasi (izinkulungwane ezimbalwa ze-KB), ngakho akukho okulindele ukulandwa okwahlukile. Ufunda ifayela ngasemuva, ngakho ikhasi lisaphendula lapho i-DWG enkulu ivulwa.

I-DXF ifundwa ngezingcezu ezingu-4 MB, hhayi njengebhulokhi elilodwa lombhalo, ngakho i-DXF edlula ku-512 MB nayo ivulwa. Okukhawulela ukuvulwa yimemori yekhompyutha yakho: lapho i-DXF ivulwa, lindele ukuthi isebenzise cishe amahlandla angu-16 kuya kwangu-20 ubukhulu befayela (i-DXF engu-100 MB enezinto ezicishe zibe ngu-690,000 yasebenzisa cishe ngu-1.5 GB esivivinyweni sethu).

### Okungenayo

- Imigqa, imisnsalo, imibuthano, ama-ellipse, amaphoyinti, iphola-layini (nezingxenye zomsnsalo), umbhalo nombhalo wemigqa eminingi, amasolidi, ama-hatch (amaphethini ajwayelekile adwetshwa njengemigqa yawo, amanye njengethoni elilodwa), izethenjwa zamabhulokhi namazici azo (amanani ongawahlela; bona [Izici zebhulokhi](topic:attributes)), izilinganiso, namashidi nezibuko zokubuka zawo.
- Ama-spline angena njenge-spline (idigri, amaphoyinti okulawula, izinkulumo, izisindo, amaphoyinti okufaneleka); bona [Ama-spline namaphola-layini](topic:splines).
- Ama-multileader anomugqa oyodwa womhola nombhalo angena njenge-multileader ye-CadCraft oyingahlela (bona {{cmd:MLEADER}}). Abanye (anebhulokhi, imigqa eminingi yomhola noma imigqa egobile) adwetshwa njengoba adwetshwa yolunye uhlelo futhi ahamba njengento eyodwa; umlando uthi zingaki.
- Izithombe (izinhlobo) zibonakala njengohlaka negama lefayela lesithombe kuze unikeze i-CadCraft ifayela ngo-{{cmd:IMAGE}}: bona [Izithombe emdwebweni](topic:pictures#show-the-pictures-a-dwg-uses). Ama-wipeout abonakala njengohlaka lwawo, nokungaphansi kwawo kuhlala kubonakala.
- Amatafula ku-DWG angena njengoba adwetshwa yolunye uhlelo.
- Izinhlangothi ze-3D emkhathini wemodeli zingena ku-{{ui:3D}} ithebhu, hhayi njengezinto ze-2D; kanjalo nalezo ezisebhulokhini, kanye ngesethenjwa ngasinye sebhulokhi kumodeli. Ebhulokhini nasemashidini zidwetshwa nazo zibe matasa, njengemiklamo. Bona [Imodeli ku-3D](topic:3d-solids).
- Izingqimba zigcina umbala wazo, izilungiselelo zazo zokuvuleka, zokumiswa, zokukhiywa nezokushicilelwa. I-DEFPOINTS ayisoze ishicilelwe.
- Izilinganiso ziba yizilinganiso zangempela ze-CadCraft ezihlelekayo lapho amaphoyinti azo efundeka khona. Kuqala zibonakala njengoba zadwetshwa yolunye uhlelo; uma ushintsha enye, i-CadCraft iyiphinda idwetshwe ngesitayela sayo.
- Ishidi ngalinye liba yithebhu yeshidi. Kolunye uhlelo, ishidi ngalinye linesibuko sokubuka sokuqala esifihliwe esiyiwona ishidi uqobo; i-CadCraft iyasishiya lowo.

### Okungakangeni okwamanje

Konke i-CadCraft engakwazi ukukudweba kubalwa, hhayi kuqagelwe. Ibhokisi ethi "About this file" kwisigaba sangakwesokunxele liyibala njenge "Akukabonakali:" elandelwa uhlobo ngalunye lwento nenani layo. Izimo ezivamile yimitapo ku-DXF, ama-mesh, namasolidi we-3D ngaphakathi kwamabhulokhi. Amasolidi we-3D, izifunda namabhodi emkhathini wemodeli avela ku-3D tab (bona [Imodeli ku-3D](topic:3d-solids#solids-from-other-programs)). Izibuko sokubuka ezipheqwe kufayela zibonakala zigoqiwe, njengoba zinjalo khona, futhi zigcina ukugoqeka kwazo uma ulondoloza; kanjalo ne-UCS yamanje, ama-UCS amagama nombono ogoqiwe.

Umbhalo udwetshwa ngefonti eyodwa esitayeleni se-Helvetica. Ilebula elilingana embayweni owodwa kufonti ewuncane yohlelo lwangempela lingaggoqeka libe yimigqa emibili ku-CadCraft.

## Ukulondoloza

Kukhona izindlela ezintathu zokulondoloza. Ngalinye lilanda ikhophi.

- **Londoloza**: cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}, chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}, noma bhala `SAVE` (futhi `QSAVE`). Ilondoloza ngefomethi yomdwebo: i-DWG uma uvule i-DWG, i-DXF uma uvule i-DXF. I-DWG igcina inguqulo ifike ngayo (ifayela le-2018 lilondolozwa njenge-2018). Umdwebo omusha usebenzisa ifomethi nenguqulo ye-DWG okhethe ekugcineni ku-{{ui:Londoloza njenge}} kulesi siphequluli; uma ungakaze ukhethe, i-DWG ngefomethi ye-AutoCAD 2000.
- **Londoloza njenge**: cindezela {{key:Mod+Shift+S}}, chofoza {{ui:Londoloza njenge}}, noma bhala `SAVEAS`. Ingxoxo ethi {{ui:Londoloza ikhophi njenge}} inikeza {{ui:DWG}}, uhlu {{ui:Inguqulo}} (AutoCAD 2018, 2013, 2010, 2007, 2004 noma 2000), {{ui:DXF R12}} kanye {{ui:DXF 2000}}, ngalinye linegama lefayela elizalinika, nefomethi nenguqulo yomdwebo wamanje sezikhethiwe. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}} ukuze ulande, noma {{ui:Khansela}} (noma {{key:Esc}}) ukuze ungalondolozi lutho; umlando uthi "Akulondoloziwe." Okukhethayo kuyahlala: {{key:Mod+S}} isisebenzisa kulo mdwebo kusukela khona, nemidwebo emisha kulesi siphequluli iqala nakho.
- **DXFOUT**: bhala `DXFOUT`. Njalo ilanda ikhophi ye-DXF R12, futhi i-{{key:Mod+S}} elandelayo isalondoloza ngefomethi yomdwebo wayo.

Isiphequluli silanda `<igama lomdwebo>.dwg` noma `<igama lomdwebo>.dxf`. Ukulondoloza ngakunye kuba **ukulanda okusha**; ifayela ovule alibhalwa phezu kwalo futhi alishintshwa. Umlando uyakuqinisa, njengokuthi "Londoloziwe njengekhophi Plan.dwg (DWG (AutoCAD 2018)). Ifayela lakho lokuqala Plan.dxf alishintshiwe." Ku-DXF uthi "(DXF R12, ivulwa kunoma yiluphi uhlelo lwe-CAD)". Imigqa ngaphansi kwayo ibala noma yini eyashintsha ngendlela.

Uma i-CadCraft isebenza kuhlelo lwedeskithophu, ukulondoloza kusebenza njengakunye kwezinye izinhlelo zedeskithophu: {{key:Mod+S}} ibhala umdwebo kabusha kulelo fayela ovule, ngefomethi nenguqulo yawo, futhi umlando uthi kuphi kulondolozwe. {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} nokukhipha kuboniswa uxoxo lokulondoloza lwesistimu, nelifayela le-`.dwg`, `.dxf` noma `.ccsheets` olichofoza kabili livulwa ku-CadCraft.

Ku-PDF, shicilela esikhundleni (bona ngezansi).

## Okugcinwa futhi okushintshwa yi-DWG

I-CadCraft ibhala i-DWG ngokwayo, ngaphakathi kusiphequluli sakho, ngalo lolo mtapo we-DWG eyifundayo. Khetha inguqulo ku-{{ui:Londoloza njenge}}: ifomethi ye-AutoCAD 2000 (inguqulo yefayela AC1015) ivulwa ezinhlelweni eziningi, ezindala nazo zihlanganisiwe; amafomethi amasha (2004, AC1018, kuze kube ngu-2018, AC1032) agcina futhi imibala yangempela. Akukho okulayishelwayo, futhi ukulondoloza i-DWG kusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.

Inguqulo ngayinye ihlolwa ngokufunda amafayela alondoloziwe ngabafundi be-DWG bezinhlelo ezintathu zolunye uhlelo nangokwe-CadCraft uqobo. I-DWG ye-CadCraft ayikaze ivulwe ku-AutoCAD ngokwayo kuze kube manje.

### Okugcinwa yi-DWG

- Imigqa, imisnsalo, imibuthano, ama-ellipse, amaphoyinti, namaphola-layini nezingxenye zawo zomsnsalo.
- Umbhalo womugqa owodwa nokuhlelwa kwawo nesici sobubanzi.
- Umbhalo wemigqa eminingi nombhalo ogoqiwe, njengombhalo wemigqa eminingi. Olunye uhlelo luyawugoqa futhi ngobubanzi obufanayo.
- Imibala yezinto: ngongqimba, ngebhulokhi, imibala engu-255 ejwayelekile, nemibala yangempela kwifomethi ye-2004 nakwezinye ezisanda.
- Izingqimba nombala wazo, uhlobo lomugqa, ubukhulu bomugqa nezilungiselelo zazo zokuvula, zokumisa, zokukhiya nezokushicilela; uhlobo lomugqa, ubukhulu bomugqa nesikali sohlobo lomugqa zezinto ngazo, amaphethini ohlobo lomugqa nesikali sohlobo lomugqa somdwebo.
- Amayunithi omdwebo nefomethi yobude (amadesimali, amafidi namainshi, amafrakshini…).
- Amabhulokhi nephoyinti lawo lesisekelo nezincazelo zezici, kuhlanganise ezingabonakali, ezingaguquki nezilungiselelwe ngaphambili.
- Izethenjwa zamabhulokhi nesikali sazo, ukujikeleza kwazo nenani lezici.
- Ukugcwaliswa okuqinile, nama-hatch ahlelwe ngamaphethini, isikali nengosi yawo (imigqa yephethini yawo, ukuze ezinye izinhlelo zidwebe imigqa efanayo ne-CadCraft).
- Izilinganiso, njengezilinganiso zangempela: ezinye izinhlelo ziyazilinganisa kabusha uma zelulwa. Ngayinye igcina izilungiselelo zayo (ukuphakama kombhalo, usayizi wenhloko yomcibisholo, amadesimali, ifomethi yobude) nombhalo wayo, futhi ithwala ikhophi edwetshiwe elikhona kuzo zonke izilinganiso ze-DWG.
- Amashidi (ama-layout): ishidi ngalinye nosayizi wephepha namayunithi alo, okudwetshwe kulo, nezibuko zalo zokubuka nombono nesikali sazo.
- Imibono enegama.
- Ama-multileader, njenge-multileader.
- Amagama engqimba namabhulokhi njengoba uwabhalile, ngamachibi amakhulu nancane. (I-DXF R12 iwabhala ngamachibi amakhulu.)

### Okushintshwa yi-DWG

Okulandelayo kuvela emlandweni emva kokulondoloza, uma kusebenza emdwebeni wakho.

| Ku-CadCraft | Ku-DWG elondoloziwe |
|---|---|
| Imibala yangempela (ekhethwe ngesikhethi-mbala), kwifomethi ye-2000 | Umbala ojwayelekile oseduze: ifomethi ye-2000 inemibala engu-255 kuphela. Londoloza njenge-2004 noma kamuva ukuze uwagcine. |
| Imibono enegama yeshidi | Imibono yesikhala sephepha: i-DWG ayisho ukuthi iliphi ishidi, ngakho lapho i-CadCraft ivula kabusha ifayela ziba ezeshidi lokuqala. |
| Umbhalo wemigqa eminingi nesici sobubanzi | Ububanzi bohlamvu bejwayelekile: ku-DWG isici sobubanzi sisebenza kumbhalo womugqa owodwa kuphela. |
| Amagama engqimba noma ebhulokhi anezinhlamvu i-DWG ingazivumeli | Ashintshwe: igama elivela emdwebweni oxhunyiwe njenge `Base\|Walls` liba `Base$0$Walls` (indlela okubhalwa ngayo ukubopha umdwebo oxhunyiwe), nezinye izinhlamvu zibe `_`. |

## Kungani i-R12, nokuthi yini eguqukayo

Uma ulondoloza njenge-DXF, i-CadCraft ibhala i-DXF R12, inguqulo zonke izinhlelo ze-CAD zingavula. Iwudala kunezici ezithile, ngakho izinto ezimbalwa zibhalwa ngendlela elula:

| Ku-CadCraft | Kufayela le-R12 elondoloziwe |
|---|---|
| Umbhalo wemigqa eminingi | Into yombhalo eyodwa ngomugqa ngamunye |
| Ama-ellipse | Amaphola-layini alandela i-ellipse |
| Ama-hatch (avela kumafayela avuliwe) | Imiphetho yawo kuphela |
| Imibala yangempela (ekhethwe ngesikhethi-mbala) | Umbala ojwayelekile oseduze |
| Ubukhulu bomugqa, nezikali zohlobo lomugqa zezinto ngazo | Akufakwanga: i-R12 ayinazo zombili (izinhlobo zemigqa ziyagcinwa) |
| Amagama engqimba namabhulokhi | Abhalwa ngamachibi amakhulu; izikhala nezinye izinhlamvu ziba `_`, namagama anqunywa abe ngezinhlamvu ezingu-31 ("Title block" iba `TITLE_BLOCK`) |
| Izingqimba ezimisiwe ukungashicilelwa | Ezinye izinhlelo zizozishicilela (i-CadCraft ikhumbula isilungiselelo uma umdwebo uneshidi) |
| Izilinganiso | Izilinganiso zangempela nebhulokhi lazo elidwetshiwe, ukuze ezinye izinhlelo zibonise zona njengoba uzibona |
| Ama-multileader | Imigqa, inhloko yomcibisholo nombhalo abadwetshayo |
| Imibono enegama | Iyagcinwa, namagama ngamachibi amakhulu; imibono yeshidi ibuya eshidini lokuqala |
| Amashidi amaningi | Ishidi lokuqala yisikhala sephepha esibonwa zonke izinhlelo; ezinye zigcinwa njengamabhulokhi anamagama `CCLAYOUT_1`, `CCLAYOUT_2` … i-CadCraft iwaguqula ibuyele emashidini uma ivula ifayela |

Umlando ubala konke lokhu okusebenze emdwebeni wakho.

:::tip
Ukuze uqhubeke nokusebenza emdwebeni izinsuku ezimbalwa, londoloza njenge-DXF bese uvula leyo DXF ngesikhathi esilandelayo. Konke i-CadCraft engakwazi ukudweba kuyasinda kuhambo olugcwele, kuhlanganise izilinganiso, amabhulokhi namashidi onke.
:::

## Imidwebo exhunyiwe (izithenjwa zangaphandle)

{{cmd:XATTACH}} (`XA`, noma {{ui:Amabhulokhi}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Xhuma umdwebo (xref)}}) ixhumanisa i-DWG noma i-DXF enye: khetha ifayela, bese uchofoza lapho lingena khona ({{key:Enter}} ilibeka ku-0,0). Ingena njengebhulokhi elilodwa elithatha igama lalo kusuka efayelini, ngesikali esifanele uma imidwebo emibili inamayunithi ahlukene. Izingqimba nobhulokhi bayo bagcina amagama abo negama lokuxhuma phambili — `Plan|Walls`, `Plan|Door` — ngakho abahlanganisi nezakho: vula futhi uvale izingqimba ezixhunyiwe, umise noma ukhiye njengezakho. Ukuxhuma kufundeka kuphela: shintsha omunye umdwebo bese uphinda uwulayisha. Lokho osetha kwizingqimba zokuxhuma (umbala, uhlobo lomugqa, ubukhulu bomugqa, vula/vala, misa, khiya, shicilela) kugcinwa uma uphinda ulayisha, lapho ulondoloza umdwebo njenge-DWG ngesikhathi ukuxhuma kusasekhona, nalapho omunye umdwebo exhuma lona.

{{cmd:XREF}} (`XR`): `?` ibala izixhumanisi (ezilayishiwe noma cha, iyiphi ifayela, zingaki izethenjwa); `A` iyangeza; `R` iphinda iyilayishe kufayela layo (khetha ifayela futhi: isiphequluli asikwazi ukufinyelela amafolda akho ngokwaso); `B` iyibopha (iba yizigaba ezijwayelekile namabhulokhi nezingqimba ezithi `Plan$0$Walls`); `D` iyikhipha (izethenjwa zayo nezingqimba zayo ziyasuswa). Ngayinye iyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

{{cmd:XCLIP}} (`XC`) iqamula izethenjwa zamabhulokhi nezixhumanisi: zikhethe, `N` ngomugqa omusha womngcele, bese `R` namakhona amabili noma `P` namaphoyinti okuyinxanxathela ({{key:Enter}} iyivala); `D` isusa ukuqamula. Okuphakathi kuphela kuyabonakala futhi kushicilelwe, futhi ukuqamula kuhamba nesethenjwa. I-DWG ne-DXF 2000 zigcina ukuqamula; i-DXF R12 igcina isethenjwa siphelele futhi isho lokho. Uma ubopha ixhumanisi elinezethenjwa ezinqanyuliwe, {{cmd:XREF}} `B` iyabuza: `A` ibopha konke, `C` igcina kuphela ingxenye eqanyuliwe yesethenjwa ngasinye (imigqa nemisnsalo inqunywa emngceleni; umbhalo namabhulokhi angaphakathi ahlala uma iphoyinti lawo lokufaka lingaphakathi).

Umdwebo ovulwayo ungaxhuma amafayela i-CadCraft ingawabonile: ubonisa ukuthi zingaki ezingalayishiwe. Sebenzisa {{cmd:XREF}}, `R` bese ukhetha ifayela ngalinye ukuze uwabone.

Uma ulondoloza, ixhumanisi elilayishiwe liyabotshelwa kufayela (ezinye izinhlelo zibona amabhulokhi nezingqimba ezijwayelekile ezinamagama `Plan$0$…`), futhi umlando uyakusho lokho. Ixhumanisi elingalayishiwe lihlala liyixhumanisi kufayela lalo, ku-DWG nasku-DXF.

## Vula ku-3D

{{ui:Ukubuka}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Vula ku-ModelCraft}} ({{cmd:OPENIN3D}}) ivula umdwebo kuhlelo lokumodela nge-3D ethebhini elisha, njenge-DWG, ngaphandle kokuwulayisha. Izinguquko ezenziwe khona zihlala khona; uzilondoloze khona. Into yemenyu ibonakala kuphela lapho lolo hlelo lungamukela khona umdwebo oshiywe.

## Ukushicilela i-PDF

Cindezela {{key:Mod+P}}, chofoza {{ui:Dweba}}, noma bhala `PLOT`. I-PDF yenziwa ngaphakathi kusiphequluli futhi ilandwe, noma ivulwe ethebhini elisha ukuze ukhiphe. Bona [{{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}}](topic:plot-pdf).

## Lapho amafayela aya khona

Konke ukulondoloza nokushicilela kuyokulanda, ngakho amafayela ayakhela noma yiluphi uhlangothi isiphequluli sakho sibeka khona okulandiwe: ngokuvamile efolda lokulanda. Ukuze ukhethe ifolda njalo, shintsha isilungiselelo sokulanda kusiphequluli sakho; [Iziphequluli nekhompyutha](common:browsers) ikhombisa lapho.

Akukho okuvulwayo noma okulondolozwayo okuthunyelwa kithi. Bona [Lapho amafayela akho aya khona](common:your-files).

## Amafayela wephrojekthi e-PLN ne-PLA

I-CadCraft ivula imidwebo ye-DWG ne-DXF, hhayi amafayela e-Archicad uqobo (`.pln`, `.pla`, `.tpl`, `.bpn`). Ukuvula elilodwa kubonisa ukuthi yiliphi. Ku-Archicad, londoloza imibono noma amashidi odinga njenge-DWG bese uwavula lapha, noma khipha imodeli njenge-IFC bese uyivula ku-BimCraft. Lapho i-BimCraft ingamukela ifayela, i-CadCraft inikeza {{ui:Vula ku-BimCraft}}.

## Uma ikhasi livala ngaphambi kokulondoloza

I-CadCraft igcina ikhophi yomdwebo ngamunye oyiguqulayo, kulesi siphequluli kuphela, kungemuva kwesikhashana uma uyeka ukuhlela. Uma ithebhu livala, isiphequluli siyama noma ikhompyutha iqalisa kabusha ngaphambi kokulondoloza, i-CadCraft iyakubuza **Buyisela umsebenzi wakho?** ngesikhathi esilandelayo iqala: {{ui:Buyisela}} ivula kabusha umdwebo, ungalondolozi, ethebhini lawo. Ukulondoloza umdwebo kususa ikhophi yawo. Bona [Buyisela umsebenzi wakho](common:recover).

## Ku-inthanethi nangaphandle kwe-inthanethi

Ukuvula, ukudweba, ukulondoloza (i-DWG ne-DXF) nokushicilela konke kwenzeka ekhompyutheni yakho, ngakho kusebenza ngaphandle koxhumo lapho i-CadCraft seyilayishiwe.

Ukuze usebenzise i-CadCraft ngaphandle koxhumo ngokuphelele, chofoza {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} kubha engaphezulu lapho usuxhumeke khona. Ilebula liyakhula ("Ilondoloza 40 kwangu-212") liphele ku "Okulungele ngaphandle". Ikhophi elondoloziwe ifaka isifundi se-DWG, cishe ngu-5 MB ngokuphelele, ngakho **amafayela e-DWG avula ngaphandle nawo** uma selondoloziwe. Chofoza inkinobho futhi ukuze uthole {{ui:Londoloza futhi (thola inguqulo entsha kakhulu)}} noma {{ui:Susa ikopi engaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}. "Akukwazanga ukulondoloza" kusho ukuthi amanye amafayela awazange alandwe: hlola uxhumo bese uchofoza ukuze uzame futhi. "Ukulondoloza ngaphandle akutholakali" kusho ukuthi lo msebenzi wesiphequluli awukwazi ukugcina ikhophi (amawindi azimele nezinye kompyutha ezilawulwayo).

Ngaphandle kwekhophi elondoloziwe, umfundi we-DWG uyalanjwa ngesikhathi sokuqala uvula i-DWG ngemva kokulayisha ikhasi, ngakho ukuvula i-DWG ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kungahluleka; amafayela e-DXF awadingi lutho lolu. Ungalayishi kabusha ikhasi ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ngaphandle uma i-CadCraft ilondoloziwe.

Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline) ngemininingwane.

:::offline
Usuphandle kwe-inthanethi manje. Imidwebo ilondoloza njenge-DWG noma i-DXF futhi ishicilelwa ngokujwayelekile, namafayela e-DXF avula. Amafayela e-DWG avula uma i-CadCraft ikhombisa "Okulungele ngaphandle", noma uma umfundi we-DWG seyilayishiwe kule thebhu.
:::

## Izithiyo emafayeleni

Izithiyo nezipharamitha (bona [Qinisa umdwebo](topic:constraints)) zilondolozwa kokubili i-DWG ne-DXF. Into ngayinye ethintekile ithwala inothi elincane le-CadCraft (idatha eyengeziwe), nebhulokhi elingasetshenziswa elithi `CADCRAFT_PARAMETRIC` libamba izithiyo nethebhula lamapharamitha. Ezinye izinhlelo zivula umdwebo ngokujwayelekile futhi zinganaki kokubili; zingasusa ibhulokhi elingasetshenziswa lapho zihlanza. Amanothi abhekisela ezintweni ngokhiye wazo, hhayi ngama-handle efayela, ngakho uhlelo oluguqula izinombolo zama-handle alubhidlizi. Uma enye inhlelo isuse noma ishintshe into ethintekile, i-CadCraft iyasusa izithiyo kuyo lapho ivula ifayela futhi isho ukuthi zingaki. Ifayela elingenazo livula ngaphandle kwezithiyo.

Isikethi esikhishwe kusimodeli sezingxenye (`.json`) nso siyavula: bona [Hambisa izikethi kanye nakusimodeli sezingxenye ezinye](topic:solidworks-sketches).

## Akutholakali okwamanje

- Ama-multileader njengezinto ze-multileader ku-DXF elondoloziwe (i-R12 ayinazo: alondolozwa njengalokho adwetshwa khona).
- Izithombe ku-DXF elondoloziwe (zilondolozwa njengezinhlaka zazo; i-DWG izigcina njengezithombe), nama-wipeout afihla okungaphansi kwawo.
- Ukulondoloza inguqulo entsha ye-DXF kunaleyo ka-2000.
- Ukulondoloza ku-cloud nokuvumelanisa phakathi kwamakhompyutha.
