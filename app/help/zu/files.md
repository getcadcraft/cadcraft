## Ngokubheka kokuqala

| Ifomethi | Vula | Londoloza | Amanothi |
|---|---|---|---|
| DXF (umbhalo, noma iyiphi inguqulo) | Yebo | Yebo, njenge-DXF R12 noma i-DXF 2000 | I-DXF ye-binary iyenqatshelwa: yilondoloze njenge-DXF yombhalo kuqala. |
| DWG | Yebo | Yebo, njenge-DWG (AutoCAD 2000, 2004, 2007, 2010, 2013 noma 2018) | Ifundwa futhi ibhalwe ithala le-DWG lakwa-CadCraft (amafayela e-AutoCAD R13 kuya ku-2018). I-DWG yakho ayikaze ishintshwe. Amafayela alonakele, noma izinguqulo umfundi angazisebenzisi, ayenqatshelwa ngomlayezo. |
| PDF | Iletha ikhasi: njenge-underlay noma njengezinto ([ama-underlay e-PDF](topic:pdf-underlay)) | Yebo, ngokudweba | I-PDF ye-vector yeshidi, noma yemodeli ngesikali. |

Umdwebo ngamunye ovula (noma oqala nge-{{ui:Okusha}}) unikezwa ithebhu yawo phezu kwendawo yomdwebo; umdwebo omusha ongakaze uthintwe usetshenziswa esikhundleni sokungeza ithebhu. Chofoza ithebhu ukuze ushintshe, u-{{ui:×}} ukuze uyivale (i-CadCraft iyabuza kuqala uma kukhona izinguquko ezingalondoloziwe), noma ubeke imidwebo evuliwe ecaleni komunye nomunye: bona [Hlukanisa isikrini](topic:split-screen).

## Ukuvula umdwebo

- Cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}, chofoza {{ui:Vula}}, noma ubhale `OPEN`, bese ukhetha ifayela.
- Noma hudula ifayela `.dwg` noma `.dxf` liye endaweni yomdwebo.

Ifayela lifundwa ngaphakathi kwesiphequluli sakho. Alilayishwa noma kuphi. Umlando ubika ukuthi zingaki izinto nezingqimba ezingenile nokuthi kuthathe isikhathi esingakanani, futhi isigaba se-{{ui:Umdwebo}} sepaneli yangakwesobunxele sibonisa ifomethi, njengalapho kuthi "DWG 2018 (AC1032)".

Isifundi se-DWG sikhona ngaphakathi kwe-CadCraft futhi silayishwa ngokuvula i-DWG yokuqala emva kokuthi ikhasi seliyilayishile (amakhulu ambalwa e-KB), ngakho akudingeki ukulinda ukulandwa okuhlukile. Lifunda ifayela ngasemuva, ngakho ikhasi liyaphendula kuqhubeke lapho i-DWG enkulu ivulwa.

### Okungenayo

- Imigqa, imisnsalo, izindilinga, amaielipsi, amaphoyinti, umbhalo nombhalo wemigqa eminingi, izinto eziqinile, imigqa yokufiphaza (amaphethini ajwayelekile adwetshwa njengemigqa yawo, ezinye njengethoni elilodwa), izinkomba zamabhulokhi nezici zazo (amanani ongawahlela; bona [Izici zebhulokhi](topic:attributes)), izilinganiso, namashidi nezibuko zawo zokubuka.
- Ama-spline angena njenge-spline (idigri, amaphoyinti okulawula, amanodi, izisindo, amaphoyinti okulingana); bona [Ama-spline namaphola-layini](topic:splines).
- Ama-multileader anomugqa oqondisayo owodwa nombhalo angena njengama-multileader we-CadCraft oyingahlela (bona {{cmd:MLEADER}}). Ezinye (enebhulokhi, imigqa eqondisayo eminingi noma imigqa egobile) zidwetshwa njengoba zidwetshwe yenye inhlelo futhi zinyakaza zibe yinye; umlando ubonisa ukuthi zingaki.
- Izithombe zibonakala njengohlaka negama lefayela lesithombe size usinike i-CadCraft ifayela ngo-{{cmd:IMAGE}}: bona [Izithombe emdwebweni](topic:pictures#show-the-pictures-a-dwg-uses). Ama-wipeout abonakala njengohlaka lwawo, futhi okungaphansi kwawo kuhlala kubonakala.
- Amatafula ku-DWG angena njengoba adwetshwe yenye inhlelo.
- Ubuso be-3D esikhaleni semodeli bungena ethebhini le-{{ui:3D}}, hhayi njengezinto ze-2D; kanjalo nalezo ezisebhulokhini, kanye ngenkomba yebhulokhi ngayinye emodelini. Ebhulokhini nasemshidini zidwetshwa nazo zibe mbonge, njengemigqa ephelelezayo. Bona [Imodeli ye-3D](topic:3d-solids).
- Izingqimba zigcina umbala wazo, izilungiselelo zokuvula, zokumisa, zokukhiya nezokudweba. I-DEFPOINTS ayikaze idwetshwe.
- Izilinganiso ziba yizilinganiso ze-CadCraft zangempela, ezihleleka lapho amaphoyinti azo efundeka khona. Zibonakala kuqala ngendlela efanana ngqo nalapho zidwetshwe ngayo yenye inhlelo; uma ushintsha enye, i-CadCraft iyidweba kabusha ngesitayela sayo.
- Isakhiwo ngasinye siba yithebhu yeshidi. Kwenye inhlelo, isakhiwo ngasinye sinesibuko sokubuka sokuqala esifihliwe esiyiso leshidi uqobo; i-CadCraft ayiyifaki leyo.

### Okungakangeni okwamanje

Lokho i-CadCraft engenakukudweba kubalwa, akukhethwa ngokuqagela. Ibhokisi elithi "Mayelana nalele fayela" epaneli yangakwesobunxele liyikhomba njenge-"Akukakabonakali okwamanje:" kulandelwe uhlobo ngalunye lwento nenani layo. Izibonelo ezivamile yizilinganiso ku-DXF, ama-mesh, nezinto eziqinile ze-3D ngaphakathi kwamabhulokhi. Izinto eziqinile ze-3D, izifunda nemizimba esikhaleni semodeli zibonakala ethebhini le-3D (bona [Imodeli ye-3D](topic:3d-solids#solids-from-other-programs)). Izibuko zokubuka ezigoqwe kufayela zibonakala zigoqiwe, njengalapho, futhi ziyagcina ukugoqeka kwazo uma ulondoloza; kanjalo ne-UCS yamanje, ama-UCS aqanjiwe nombono ogoqiwe.

Umbhalo udwetshwa ngofonti eyodwa esitayela se-Helvetica. Ilebula elilingana komugqa owodwa kofonti elincane yenhlelo yokuqala lingahlukana libe yimigqa emibili ku-CadCraft.

## Ukulondoloza

Kukhona izindlela ezintathu zokulondoloza. Ngayinye ilanda ikhophi.

- **Londoloza**: cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}, chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}, noma ubhale `SAVE` (futhi `QSAVE`). Ilondoloza ngefomethi yomdwebo: i-DWG uma uvule i-DWG, i-DXF uma uvule i-DXF. I-DWG igcina inguqulo ayifika ngayo (ifayela lika-2018 lilondolozwa njenge-2018). Umdwebo omusha usebenzisa ifomethi kanye nenguqulo ye-DWG okhethe ekugcineni ku-{{ui:Londoloza njenge}} kulesi siphequluli; uma ungakaze ukhethe, i-DWG yefomethi ye-AutoCAD 2000.
- **Londoloza njenge**: cindezela {{key:Mod+Shift+S}}, chofoza {{ui:Londoloza njenge}}, noma ubhale `SAVEAS`. Ingxoxo ethi {{ui:Londoloza ikhophi njenge}} inikeza {{ui:DWG}}, nohlu {{ui:Version}} (AutoCAD 2018, 2013, 2010, 2007, 2004 noma 2000), {{ui:DXF R12}} kanye {{ui:DXF 2000}}, ngalunye lunegama lefayela azolinika, kanye nefomethi nenguqulo yomdwebo yamanje sezikhethiwe. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}} ukuze ulande, noma {{ui:Khansela}} (noma {{key:Esc}}) ukuze ungalondolozi lutho; umlando-ke usho ukuthi "Akulondoloziwe." Okukhethayo kuyahlala: {{key:Mod+S}} kusebenzisa lokho kulo mdwebo kusukela manje, nemidwebo emisha kulesi siphequluli iqala ngakho.
- **DXFOUT**: bhala `DXFOUT`. Ilanda njalo ikhophi ye-DXF R12, futhi {{key:Mod+S}} elandelayo isalondoloza ngefomethi yomdwebo wawo.

Isiphequluli silanda `<igama lomdwebo>.dwg` noma `<igama lomdwebo>.dxf`. Ukulondoloza ngakunye **kulanda okusha**; ifayela ovule lona alibhalwa phezu kwalo noma lishintshwe. Umlando uqinisekisa lokho, njengalapho kuthi "Ikhophi elondolozwe njenge-Plan.dwg (DWG (AutoCAD 2018)). Ifayela lakho lokuqala le-Plan.dxf alishintshiwe." Kwe-DXF kuthi "(DXF R12, ivuleka kunoma iyiphi inhlelo ye-CAD)". Imigqa ngaphansi kwawo ibhala konke okushintshile ngesikhathi kulondolozwa.

Uma i-CadCraft isebenza ohlelweni lwedeskithophu, ukulondoloza kusebenza njengakwezinye izinhlelo zedeskithophu: {{key:Mod+S}} ibhala umdwebo kabusha kufayela owuvulile, ngefomethi nangenguqulo yawo, nomlando ubonisa lapho olondolozwe khona. {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} nokuphuma kwemidwebo kuvula ingxoxo yokulondoloza yesistimu, futhi ifayela `.dwg`, `.dxf` noma `.ccsheets` ocindezela kabili kulo livula ku-CadCraft.

Ye-PDF, dweba esikhundleni (bona ngezansi).

## Okugcinwa nokushintshwa yi-DWG

I-CadCraft ibhala i-DWG ngokwayo, ngaphakathi kwesiphequluli sakho, ngethala le-DWG efanayo eyifundayo. Khetha inguqulo ku-{{ui:Londoloza njenge}}: ifomethi ye-AutoCAD 2000 (inguqulo yefayela AC1015) ivuleka ezinhlelweni eziningi kakhulu, ezindala nazo zihlanganisiwe; amafomethi amasha (2004, AC1018, kuya ku-2018, AC1032) agcina futhi imibala yangempela. Akukho okulayishwayo, futhi ukulondoloza i-DWG kusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.

Inguqulo ngayinye iyahlolwa ngokufunda amafayela alondoloziwe ngabafundi be-DWG bezinhlelo ezintathu ezihlukile kanye ne-CadCraft ngokwayo. I-DWG ye-CadCraft ayikakavulwa ku-AutoCAD ngokwayo.

### Okugcinwa yi-DWG

- Imigqa, imisnsalo, izindilinga, amaielipsi, amaphoyinti, namaphola-layini nezingxenye zawo zomnsalo.
- Umbhalo womugqa owodwa nokulinganiswa kwawo nesilinganiso sobubanzi.
- Umbhalo wemigqa eminingi nowaphindeliwe, njengombhalo wemigqa eminingi. Enye inhlelo iphinda ibeke emigqeni kabusha ngobubanzi obufanayo.
- Imibala yezinto: ngongqimba, ngebhulokhi, imibala engu-255 ejwayelekile, nemibala yangempela kufomethi ye-2004 nangemva kwayo.
- Izingqimba nombala wazo, uhlobo lomugqa, ubukhulu bomugqa nezilungiselelo zazo zokuvula, zokumisa, zokukhiya nezokudweba; uhlobo lomugqa, ubukhulu bomugqa kanye nesikali sohlobo lomugqa zezinto ngazinye, amaphethini ohlobo lomugqa, nesikali sohlobo lomugqa somdwebo.
- Amayunithi omdwebo nefomethi yobude (ngamadesimali, amafiti nama-intshi, izingxenyana…).
- Amabhulokhi nephoyinti lawo lesisekelo nezincazelo zezici, kubandakanya ezingabonakali, ezingaguquki nezibekelwe kusengaphambili.
- Izinkomba zamabhulokhi nesikali sazo, ukujikeka kwazo nezinani lezici.
- Ukugcwaliswa okuqinile, nemigqa yokufiphaza enamaphethini ngephethini, isikali nengosi yayo (imigqa yephethini uqobo, ukuze ezinye izinhlelo zidwetshe imigqa efanayo nale-CadCraft).
- Izilinganiso, njengezilinganiso zangempela: ezinye izinhlelo ziyazilinganisa kabusha uma zeluliwe. Ngayinye igcina izilungiselelo zayo (ukuphakama kombhalo, usayizi womcibisholo, izingxenyana, ifomethi yobude) nombhalo wayo, futhi ithwala ikhophi edwetshiwe eyilelo kuzo zonke izilinganiso ze-DWG.
- Amashidi (izakhiwo): ishidi ngalinye nosayizi wephepha namayunithi alo, okudwetshwe kuwo, nezibuko zalo zokubuka nombono nesikali sazo.
- Imibono equanjiwe.
- Ama-multileader, njengama-multileader.
- Amagama ezingqimba namabhulokhi njengoba uwabhalile, ngamanani amakhulu namancane. (I-DXF R12 iwabhala ngamanani amakhulu.)

### Okushintshwa yi-DWG

Lokhu ngakunye kudwetshwe emlandweni ngemva kokulondoloza, uma kusebenza komdwebo wakho.

| Ku-CadCraft | Ku-DWG elondoloziwe |
|---|---|
| Imibala yangempela (ekhethwe ngesikhethi-mibala), kufomethi ye-2000 | Umbala ojwayelekile oseduze kakhulu: ifomethi ye-2000 inemibala engu-255 kuphela. Londoloza njenge-2004 noma kamuva ukuze uyigcine. |
| Imibono equanjiwe yeshidi | Imibono yephepha lesikhala: i-DWG ayisho ukuthi iyiphi ishidi, ngakho uma i-CadCraft ivula ifayela futhi, zibhekele ishidi lokuqala. |
| Umbhalo wemigqa eminingi nesilinganiso sobubanzi | Ububanzi bohlamvu obujwayelekile: ku-DWG isilinganiso sobubanzi sisebenza kumbhalo womugqa owodwa kuphela. |
| Amagama ezingqimba noma amabhulokhi anezinhlamvu ezingavunyelwe yi-DWG | Kushintshiwe: igama elivela emdwebweni oxhunyiwe njengalelo `Base\|Walls` liba `Base$0$Walls` (indlela ukubopha komdwebo oxhunyiwe okunikeza ngayo igama), nezinhlamvu ezinye ziba `_`. |

### Kungani R12, nokuguqulwayo

Lapho ulondoloza njenge-DXF, i-CadCraft ibhala i-DXF R12, inguqulo ezinhlelo zonke ze-CAD zingayivula. Idlule ezinye izici, ngakho izinto ezithile zibhalwa ngendlela elula:

| Ku-CadCraft | Kufayela le-R12 elondoloziwe |
|---|---|
| Umbhalo wemigqa eminingi | Into yombhalo eyodwa ngomugqa ngamunye |
| Ama-ellipse | Amaphola-layini alandela i-ellipse |
| Imigqa yokufiphaza (kumafayela avuliwe) | Imigqa yayo yangaphandle kuphela |
| Imibala yangempela (ekhethwe ngesikhethi-mibala) | Umbala ojwayelekile oseduze kakhulu |
| Ubukhulu bemigqa, nezikali zohlobo lomugqa zezinto | Kushiywe: i-R12 ayinazo zombili (uhlobo lomugqa luyagcinwa) |
| Amagama ezingqimba namabhulokhi | Abhalwa ngamanani amakhulu; izikhala nezinhlamvu ezinye ziba `_`, namagama anqunywa abe izinhlamvu ezingu-31 (`Title block` iba `TITLE_BLOCK`) |
| Izingqimba ezimiselwe ukungadwetshwa | Ezinye izinhlelo zizozidweba (i-CadCraft ikhumbula leso silungiselelo uma umdwebo uneshidi) |
| Izilinganiso | Izilinganiso zangempela nebhulokhi yazo edwetshiwe, ukuze ezinye izinhlelo zizibonise njengoba uzibona |
| Ama-multileader | Imigqa, ikhanda lomcibisholo nombhalo abawudwetshayo |
| Imibono equanjiwe | Iyagcinwa, ngamagama ngamanani amakhulu; imibono yeshidi ibuyela eshidini lokuqala |
| Amashidi amaningi | Ishidi lokuqala yisikhala sephepha esibonwa yizinhlelo zonke; amanye agcinwa njengamabhulokhi anegama elithi `CCLAYOUT_1`, `CCLAYOUT_2` … i-CadCraft ayiguqula ibe amashidi uma ivula ifayela |

Umlando udwebe konke lokhu okusebenza komdwebo wakho.

:::tip
Ukuze uqhubeke nokusebenza komdwebo phakathi kwezinsuku ezimbalwa, londoloza njenge-DXF bese uvula leyo DXF ngesikhathi esilandelayo. Konke i-CadCraft engakwazi ukudweba kuyasinda kulo mjikelezo wokulondoloza nokuvula kabusha, kubandakanya izilinganiso, amabhulokhi namashidi onke.
:::

## Imidwebo exhunyiwe (izinkomba zangaphandle)

{{cmd:XATTACH}} (`XA`, noma {{ui:Amabhulokhi}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Xhuma umdwebo (xref)}}) ixhuma enye i-DWG noma i-DXF: khetha ifayela, bese uchofoza lapho lingena khona ({{key:Enter}} iyilayisha ku-0,0). Ingena njengebhulokhi elilodwa elithatha igama lefayela, ngesikali esifanele uma imidwebo emibili inamayunithi ahlukile. Izingqimba namabhulokhi ayo agcina amagama awo ngegama lokuxhuma elingaphambili — `Plan|Walls`, `Plan|Door` — ngakho angaxubani nawakho: vula nevale izingqimba ezixhunyiwe, mise noma ukhiye njengezakho. Ukuxhuma kufundeka kuphela: shintsha omunye umdwebo bese uyalayisha kabusha. Konke osethe ezingqimbeni zokuxhuma (umbala, uhlobo lomugqa, ubukhulu bomugqa, ukuvula/ukuvala, ukumisa, ukukhiya, ukudweba) kuyagcinwa lapho uyilayisha kabusha, lapho ulondoloza umdwebo njenge-DWG kusengakaxhunyiwe, nalapho komunye umdwebo uxhuma lona.

{{cmd:XREF}} (`XR`): `?` ibala izixhumanisi (ezilayishiwe noma ezingalayishiwe, iyiphi ifayela, izinkomba ezingaki); Xhuma (`A`) ixhuma; Layisha kabusha (`R`) ilayisha enye kusuka efayelweni layo (khetha ifayela futhi: isiphequluli asikwazi ukufinyelela amafolda akho ngokwaso); `B` bopha owodwa (uba yizinto zamabhulokhi nezingqimba ezivamile ezinegama elithi `Plan$0$Walls`); Khipha (`D`) yenye (izinkomba zayo nezingqimba zayo ziyanyamalala). Isenzo ngasinye siyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

{{cmd:XCLIP}} (`XC`) isika izinkomba zamabhulokhi nezixhumanisi: zikhethe, bese ukhetha Entsha (`N`) ukuze uthole umngcele omusha, bese Unxantathu (`R`) namakhona amabili noma Ipholigoni (`P`) namaphoyinti epholigoni ({{key:Enter}} iwuvala); Susa (`D`) isusa i-clip. Okungaphakathi kuphela kwebonakala futhi kudwetshwe, futhi i-clip ihamba nenkomba. I-DWG ne-DXF 2000 ziyagcina ama-clip; i-DXF R12 igcina inkomba yonke futhi ithi lokho. Uma ubopha ixhumanisi elinezinkomba ezisikiwe, {{cmd:XREF}} `B` iyabuza: Bopha konke (`A`) ibopha konke, Gcina (`C`) igcina ingxenye esikiwe yenkomba ngayinye kuphela (imigqa nemisnsalo isikwa emngceleni; umbhalo namabhulokhi angaphakathi ahlala uma indawo yokufaka yawo isemngceleni).

Umdwebo ovula ungaxhuma amafayela i-CadCraft ingawaboni: ubonisa ukuthi zingaki ezingalayishiwe. Sebenzisa {{cmd:XREF}}, bese ukhetha `R` ukhethe ifayela ngalinye ukuze uwabone.

Lapho ulondoloza, ixhumanisi elilayishiwe **luyabophwa** lingene ngaphakathi kwefayela (ezinye izinhlelo zibona amabhulokhi nezingqimba ezivamile ezinegama elithi `Plan$0$…`), futhi umlando uyakusho. Ixhumanisi elingalayishiwe lihlala liyixhumanisi lefayela lalo, ku-DWG nasku-DXF.

## Vula ku-3D

{{ui:Ukubuka}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Vula ku-ModelCraft}} ({{cmd:OPENIN3D}}) livula umdwebo ohlelweni lokumodela nge-3D ethebhini elisha, njenge-DWG, ngaphandle kokuwulayisha. Izinguquko ezenziwe lapho zihlala lapho; zilondoloze lapho. Into yemenyu ibonakala kuphela lapho uhlelo lolo lungawamukela umdwebo onikezwayo.

## Ukushicilela i-PDF

Cindezela {{key:Mod+P}}, chofoza {{ui:Dweba}}, noma ubhale `PLOT`. I-PDF yenziwa ngaphakathi kwesiphequluli futhi ilandwa, noma ivulwe ethebhini elisha ukuze uphrinte. Bona [Shicilela ku-PDF](topic:plot-pdf).

## Lapho amafayela akhona

Ukulondoloza nokudweba ngakunye kuwukulanda, ngakho amafayela afika lapho isiphequluli sakho sibeka khona okulandiwe: ngokuvamile ifolda yeZilandiwe. Ukuze ukhethe ifolda njalo, shintsha isilungiselelo sokulanda kwisiphequluli sakho; [Iziphequluli namakhompyutha](common:browsers) ibonisa lapho.

Akukho okuvulayo noma okulondolozayo okuthunyelwa kithi. Bona [Lapho amafayela akho ahamba khona](common:your-files).

## Amafayela wephrojekthi e-PLN ne-PLA

I-CadCraft ivula imidwebo ye-DWG ne-DXF, hhayi amafayela e-Archicad siqu (`.pln`, `.pla`, `.tpl`, `.bpn`). Ukuvula elilodwa kukutshela ukuthi yini. Ku-Archicad, londoloza imibono noma amashidi odinga njenge-DWG bese uyivula lapha, noma uhlinzeke imodeli njenge-IFC bese uyivula ku-BimCraft. Lapho i-BimCraft ingalamukela ifayela, i-CadCraft inikeza {{ui:Vula ku-BimCraft}}.

## Uma ikhasi livala ngaphambi kokulondoloza

I-CadCraft igcina ikhophi lomdwebo ngamunye oyiguqulayo, kulesi siphequluli kuphela, ngemva kwesikhashana uma usuyeka ukuhlela. Uma ithebhu ivala, isiphequluli siyama noma ikhompyutha iqalwa kabusha ngaphambi kokulondoloza, i-CadCraft ibuza **Buyisela umsebenzi wakho?** uma iqala ngesikhathi esilandelayo: {{ui:Buyisela}} ivula umdwebo futhi, ungalondolozwanga, ethebhini layo. Ukulondoloza umdwebo kususa ikhophi yawo. Bona [Buyisela umsebenzi wakho](common:recover).

## Ku-inthanethi nangaphandle kwayo

Ukuvula, ukudweba, ukulondoloza (i-DWG ne-DXF) nokushicilela kwenzeka kukhompyutha yakho, ngakho kusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhumeka uma i-CadCraft isiyilayishile.

Ukuze usebenzise i-CadCraft ngaphandle kokuxhumeka ngokuphelele, chofoza {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} ebhalweni eliphezulu lapho usuxhunyiwe. Ilebula liyabalwa ("Ilondoloza 40 kwangu-212") liphele ku-"Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi". Ikhophi elilondoloziwe lifaka isifundi se-DWG, cishe ama-MB angu-5 ngokuphelele, ngakho **namafayela e-DWG avulwa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi** uma selilondoloziwe. Chofoza inkinobho futhi ukuze uthole {{ui:Londoloza futhi (thola inguqulo entsha kakhulu)}} noma {{ui:Susa ikopi engaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}. "Akukwazanga ukulondoloza" kusho ukuthi amanye amafayela ahlehlanga: hlola uxhumo bese uchofoza ukuzama futhi. "Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi akutholakali" kusho ukuthi le windi yesiphequluli ayikwazi ukugcina ikhophi (amawindi azimele namanye amakhompyutha alawulwayo).

Ngaphandle kwekhophi elilondoloziwe, isifundi se-DWG silandwa ngokuvula i-DWG yokuqala emva kokuthi ikhasi liyilayishile, ngakho ukuvula i-DWG ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kungahluleka; amafayela e-DXF akudingi sona. Ungayilayishi kabusha ikhasi ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ngaphandle uma i-CadCraft ilondolozwe.

Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline) ngemininingwane.

:::offline
Awusaxhunyiwe manje. Imidwebo ilondolozwa njenge-DWG noma i-DXF futhi idwetshwa ngokujwayelekile, futhi amafayela e-DXF ayavuleka. Amafayela e-DWG avuleka uma i-CadCraft ibonisa "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi", noma uma isifundi se-DWG sesivele silayishiwe kule thebhu.
:::

## Imibandela emafayeleni

Imibandela nama-parameter (bona [Bopha umdwebo](topic:constraints)) ilondolozwa kokubili i-DWG ne-DXF. Into ngayinye ebophiwe ithwala inothi elincane le-CadCraft (idatha engeziwe), ibhulokhi engasetshenziswa egama elithi `CADCRAFT_PARAMETRIC` ibamba imibandela nethebula lama-parameter. Ezinye izinhlelo zivula umdwebo ngokujwayelekile futhi zinganaki zombili; zingasusa ibhulokhi engasetshenziswa lapho zihlanza. Amanothi abhekisela ezintweni ngokhiye wazo, hhayi ngama-handle efayela, ngakho uhlelo oluqala kabusha ama-handle alubhubhisi. Uma enye inhlelo isusile noma yashintsha into ebophiwe, i-CadCraft ikhipha imibandela kuyo lapho ivula ifayela futhi ithi zingaki. Ifayela elingenayo livulwa ngaphandle kwemibandela.

Ishidi elithunyelwe kusuka kumodeli yezingxenye (`.json`) nalo liyavuleka: bona [Hambisa amashidi ukuya nokusuka kwezinye izimodeli zezingxenye](topic:solidworks-sketches).

## Akukatholakali okwamanje

- Ama-multileader njengezinto ze-multileader kufayela elilondoloziwe le-DXF (i-R12 ayinalutho: ilondolozwa njengalokho ezidweba khona).
- Izithombe kufayela elilondoloziwe le-DXF (zilondolozwa njengohlaka lwazo; i-DWG igcina njengezithombe), nama-wipeout afihla okungaphansi kwawo.
- Ukulondoloza inguqulo ye-DXF esha kunowe-2000.
- Ukulondoloza kwifu nokuvumelanisa phakathi kwamakhompyutha.

Bona [Akakakhiwa okwamanje](topic:not-built-yet) ngohlu olugcwele.
