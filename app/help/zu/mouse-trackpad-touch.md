I-CadCraft yenzelwe imbewu enamavili. Konke nakho kusebenza ngepedi, ngezinkambiso ezimbalwa ozofunda. Izikrini zokuthinta zisebenza ekukhetheni, nemikhawulo.

## Imbewu enamavili

- **Zoom:** gingqa amavili. Umdwebo uzoom ngokuzungeza isikhombi, ngakho khomba lokho ofuna ukukubona.
- **Hamba:** bamba amavili phansi bese uhudula, noma hudula ngenkinobho yesokudla.
- **Zoom kukho konke:** chofoza kabili amavili, noma chofoza {{ui:Extents}}.
- **Buyela embonweni wangaphambilini:** chofoza {{ui:Previous}} eqenjini lokubuka, noma thayipha `Z`, {{key:Enter}}, `P`, {{key:Enter}}. Uchungechunge lwezinyathelo zamavili lubalwa njengombono owodwa, ngakho i-{{ui:Previous}} eyodwa iyawuhlehlisa. Londoloza imibono ofuna ukuyibuyela nge-{{cmd:NAMEDVIEW}} (`NV`).
- **Enter:** chofoza ngesokudla.

## Ipedi

:::mac
- **Hamba:** hambisa iminwe emibili kupedi, nakuyiphi indlela: umdwebo uyalandela iminwe yakho. Ungaphinda uhudule ngokuchofoza ngeminwe emibili ubambe (ukuhudula ngenkinobho yesokudla).
- **Zoom:** cindezela ngeminwe emibili. Ukuzoom kulandela isikhombi.
- **Chofoza ngesokudla (Enter):** chofoza ngeminwe emibili. Vula "Secondary click" ku-**System Settings**, bese **Trackpad**, uma kungenzi lutho. I-Control-click ingabalwa njengokuchofoza okujwayelekile ku-CadCraft esikhundleni sokuchofoza ngesokudla, ngakho sebenzisa ukuchofoza ngeminwe emibili.
- **Zoom endaweni:** chofoza {{ui:Window}} eqenjini lokubuka bese uchofoza amakhona amabili.
:::

:::windows linux
- **Hamba:** hambisa iminwe emibili kwi-touchpad, nakuyiphi indlela: umdwebo uyalandela iminwe yakho. Ungaphinda uhudule ngenkinobho yesokudla ebanjiwe.
- **Zoom:** cindezela ngeminwe emibili. Ukuzoom kulandela isikhombi.
- **Chofoza ngesokudla (Enter):** chofoza ngeminwe emibili, noma chofoza ikhona eliphansi lesokudla le-touchpad, kuya ngezilungiselelo zakho ze-touchpad.
- **Zoom endaweni:** chofoza {{ui:Window}} eqenjini lokubuka bese uchofoza amakhona amabili.
:::

Ukubamba {{key:Space}} nokuhudula **akuhambisi** ku-CadCraft: {{key:Space}} ingu-Enter.

Imbewu enamavili isazoom, inyathelo elilodwa ngesikhathi. I-touchpad ethumela ukuskrola ngezinyathelo eziphelele, njengevili, izoom ngokuskrola ngeminwe emibili esikhundleni sokuhamba: phuma ngaphandle, khomba indawo ofuna, bese uzoom ngena futhi. Ithebhu ye-3D igcina izinyathelo zayo zepedi (bona [Model in 3D](topic:3d-solids#look-around)).

## Izikrini zokuthinta nama-thebulethi

I-CadCraft ifunda umunwe njengokuchofoza imbewu:

- Ukuthinta kukhetha iphoyinti noma into, noma kukhetha.
- Ukuhudula umunwe endaweni engenalutho kudweba i-lasso (ukuhudula kuqala ngakwesokudla kuyiwindi, kuqala ngakwesobunxele kuyinhlangano; bona [Lasso selection](topic:lasso-selection)).
- Ku-{{cmd:TRIM}} ne-{{cmd:EXTEND}}, ukuhudula umunwe ngezingxenye kuzo zonke ziyazinquma noma zelule.
- Ukuhudula isibambi sento ekhethiwe kuhambisa lesi sibambi (izibambi nebhokisi lokukhetha zikhulu ngomunwe).
- **Ukucindezela** ngeminwe emibili kuzoom eduze kwendawo phakathi kwazo; hambisa iminwe emibili ndawonye ukuze **uhambe**. Umunwe wesibili uyasusa lokho okuqalwe ngumunwe wokuqala, ngakho ukucindezela akusoze kushiye ukuthinta.
- **Ukubamba isikhathi eside** (bamba ungasunduli cishe ingxenye yesibili) kuyi-chofo ngesokudla: {{key:Enter}}. Iqeda umyalo, iqeda ukukhetha, noma iphinda umyalo wokugcina.

Ngaphansi komugqa womyalo kuvela **umugqa wamakhiye**: {{ui:Esc}}, Hlehlisa, Phinda wenze, izinhlamvu ukhibhodi yethebulethi ezifihla (`@` `,` `<` `'` `"` `-`), {{ui:Snap}}, {{ui:Ortho}}, inkinobho yekhibhodi, kanye ne-{{ui:Enter}} enkulu. Thinta inkinobho yekhibhodi (noma umugqa womyalo) ukuze uthayiphe umyalo noma inombolo.

Akukho ukuhamba kokuncika, ngakho izimaki zokubamba zivela lapho umunwe wakho ukhona kuphela.

:::touch
Usebenzisa isikrini sokuthinta. Ngokwedlule ukubuka okusheshayo, ikhibhodi exhunywe nembewu noma ipedi kwenza i-CadCraft ilula kakhulu ukuyisebenzisa.
:::

## Amabhrawuza

I-CadCraft isebenza ngendlela efanayo ku-Chrome, Edge, Firefox ne-Safari, ku-Mac, Windows nakuLinux. Umehluko mncane:

| Isihloko | Okufanele ukwazi |
|---|---|
| Ukuvula i-DWG | Imidwebo emikhulu ithatha imizuzwana embalwa ukufundwa kuwo wonke amabhrawuza; umugqa wesimo ubonisa "Reading …" ngalesi sikhathi |
| Ukulanda | Ibhrawuza ngalinye linquma lapho okulandiwe khona nokuthi ibuze kuqala yini; bona [Browsers and computers](common:browsers) |
| {{ui:Open to print}} | Ivula i-PDF ethebhini elisha. Uma ibhrawuza livimbela ama-pop-up, i-CadCraft ilanda i-PDF esikhundleni salokho |
| F7 | I-Firefox ingase ikunikeze ukuphequlula kwe-caret; khetha No |
| Ukushiya ngokuguqulwe okungakalondolozwa | Wonke amabhrawuza abonisa umbuzo wawo "Leave site?", engakwazi ukuwushintsha i-CadCraft |

:::safari
Ku-Safari, i-PDF evulwe nge-{{ui:Open to print}} ingase ibonakale ethebhini ingaphrintwa: sebenzisa **Ifayela**, bese **Phrinta**.
:::

:::firefox
I-Firefox ingase ibuze ukuthi yenzeni ngokulanda ngakunye. Khetha ukusindisa ifayela, noma shintsha lokhu kanye kuzilungiselelo ze-Firefox ngaphansi kwe-Ukulanda.
:::
