## Okuzofunda

Izindlela ezimbili ezisheshayo zokushintsha into ngaphandle komyalo: hudula **izibambisi** zayo, noma thayipha amanani amasha ephaneli ye-**Izakhiwo** ngakwesokudla.

## Izibambisi

Uma ukhetha izinto (kuze kufike kwezingu-200 ngesikhathi) kungekho myalo oqhubekayo, amasikwele amancane avela kuzo. Lezi yizibambisi:

| Into | Izibambisi |
|---|---|
| Umugqa | Ezimbili iziphetho, nephakathi (lihambisa umugqa wonke) |
| Indilinga | Isentha (liyahambisa) nezindawo ezine esigqumeni (zishintsha irediyasi) |
| Umnsalo | Isentha, neziphetho zombili (zishintsha lapho uqala khona nalapho uphela khona) |
| Iphola-layini | Zonke izikhombisi |
| Umbhalo, iphoyinti, isethenjwa sebhulokhi | Indawo yokufaka |
| Ukuma kwendilinga | Isentha |
| Isilinganiso | Amaphuzu ayo achaza kanye nendawo yomugqa wesilinganiso |
| Iwindi lokubuka | Isentha (liyahambisa) namakhona amane (ashintsha usayizi) |

### Hambisa isibambisi

1. Dweba umugqa bese uchofoza kuwo. Izibambisi ezintathu ziyavela.
2. Chofoza isibambisi esisesiphethweni esisodwa. Ibha yesimo ithi "Chofoza lapho lesi sibambisi siya khona (Esc ukukhansela). Ukubamba kanye ne-Ortho kuyasebenza."
3. Hambisa i-crosshair. Kuyavela ukubuka kuqala.
4. Chofoza indawo entsha, noma ubambe iphuzu kwenye into bese uchofoza.

Isiphetho siyahamba, nengxenye esele ihlala indawo yayo. Ungacindezela isibambisi uphinde uhudule, bese uyasikhulula lapho kufanele khona.

:::note
Awukwazi ukuthayipha i-coordinate yesibambisi. Ukuze uthole indawo ethile, bamba ephuzini, noma uthayiphe inani ku-Izakhiwo.
:::

Cindezela {{key:Esc}} noma chofoza ngesokudla ukukhansela ukuhamba kwesibambisi.

## Izakhiwo

Iphaneli ekudla ibonisa izakhiwo zento ekhethiwe.

1. Chofoza umugqa. Ihloko yephaneli ithi "Umugqa".
2. Chofoza inkambu {{ui:Length}}, thayipha `2500` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Umugqa manje ube ngu-2500 ngobude ngqo, ugcina iphuzu lawo lokuqala nengosi yawo. {{key:Enter}} noma ukuchofoza kwenye indawo kusebenzisa inani; {{key:Esc}} ibuyisela inani elidala. Yonke into eshintshayo iyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, nakuba izinto eziningi zishintsha ngesikhathi esisodwa.

Izinkambu zamukela izinkulumo (`=2*45`) kanye nobude onamayunithi (`3'6"`, `900mm`), iphuzu ngalinye linayo X, Y no-Z, futhi izinto eziningi zingashintshwa ndawonye. Konke lokhu kuchazwe ku-[Iphaneli Yezakhiwo](topic:properties-panel).

## Amaphutha avamile

- **Azikho izibambisi ezivela.** Umyalo uyasebenza (cindezela {{key:Esc}}), noma kukhethwe izinto ezingaphezu kuka-200, noma into isendaweni ekhiyiwe yongqimba.
- **Isibambisi sifike endaweni engalungile.** Ukubamba kuyibambile. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ngenkathi uchofoza ukuze uzidlulisele ukubamba.
- **Ukuthayipha ku-Izakhiwo akusebenzanga.** Cindezela {{key:Enter}} noma chofoza kwenye indawo ukuze usebenzise. Inani elingavunyelwe (ubude noma irediyasi eyi-zero noma engaphansi, umbhalo ongesona inani) luphendula inkambu ibe bomvu futhi lusho ukuthi kungani.
- **Izinhlamvu ezithayiphiwe zangena enkambini yezakhiwo.** Ngenkathi inkambu inekhesa, izinhlamvu zibhalwa lapho. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} noma {{key:Esc}}, bese uthayipha umyalo.
