Izinto eziqinile zethebhu ye-3D zingabhalwa njenge-USD.

- Thayipha {{cmd:USDOUT}} bese ukhetha `.usda` (umbhalo), `.usdc` (ye-binary) noma `.usdz` (ifayela elilodwa lephakheji), noma
- thayipha {{cmd:EXPORT3D}} bese ukhetha {{ui:USD}} ephakhejini le-`.usdz`.

Into eqinile ngayinye iba yinto eyodwa nezinhlangothi zayo njengemeshi nombala wayo njengempahla, ngamamitha nge-Z ephezulu. Into eqinile esetshenziswe kaningi ibhalwa kanye futhi ibekwe lapho kukhona ikhophi ngayinye.
