## Amayunithi omdwebo

Udweba ngosayizi ogcwele, ngamayunithi omdwebo. Okumelwe iyunithi eyodwa ngukuthi isethingi yomdwebo **Amayunithi**:

1. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze kungakhethwa lutho, kuthebhu le-{{ui:Imodeli}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}}, khetha {{ui:Amayunithi}}: {{ui:Akunayunithi}}, {{ui:Amamilimitha}}, {{ui:Amasentimitha}}, {{ui:Amamitha}}, {{ui:Amainshi}} noma {{ui:Amafidi}}.

Izilinganiso zingabhala ubude bazo ngamamilimitha, amasentimitha, amamitha noma amakhilomitha noma ngayiphi iyunithi lomdwebo: bheka [Izilinganiso ngamm, cm, m noma km](topic:dimension-settings#dimensions-in-mm-cm-m-or-km).

Ukushintsha amayunithi akushintshi usayizi wutho. Kushintsha indlela izikali ezifundwa ngayo emashidini nasekushicilelweni, nezikali ezitholakalayo. Umdwebo ovulwe kusuka ku-DWG noma ku-DXF ugcina amayunithi agcinwe efayeleni; isigaba se-{{ui:Umdwebo}} sephaneli yangakwesobunxele siwabonisa.

Umdwebo ongenawo amayunithi uthathwa njengalowo ongawephepha: amamilimitha ephepheni le-ISO, amaintshi ephepheni laseMelika.

## Izinyawo namaintshi, nezingxenye

Indlela ubude **obubhalwa ngayo futhi obuthayiphwa ngayo** kuyisethingi ehlukile, ifomethi yobude. Ayishintshi ijiyomethri, kuphela indlela izinombolo ezibonakala ngayo nokuthi yini ongayibhala.

1. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze kungakhethwa lutho, kuthebhu le-{{ui:Imodeli}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}}, khetha {{ui:Ifomethi yobude}}: {{ui:Amadesimali}} (`150.5`), {{ui:Ezokwakha}} (`12'-6 1/2"`), {{ui:Ubunjiniyela}} (`12'-6.5"`), {{ui:Amafrakshini}} (`150 1/2`) noma {{ui:Isayensi}} (`1.505E+02`).
3. Khetha {{ui:Ukunemba}}: izinhlamvu zamadesimali, noma ingxenye encane kakhulu (1/16 yukhetho lwejwayelekile lwezokwakha).

Noma bhala {{cmd:UNITS}} (`UN`) bese uphendula imibuzo yayo emibili.

Nge-{{ui:Ezokwakha}} noma {{ui:Ubunjiniyela}}, iyunithi eyodwa yomdwebo iyidimbane eyodwa, njengakwi-AutoCAD: setha futhi {{ui:Amayunithi}} ku-{{ui:Amainshi}}. Ungabhala ubude ngezinyawo namaintshi noma kuphi lapho kubuzwa khona ubude, nakumabhokisi e-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}:

| Obhalayo | Okusho |
|---|---|
| `12'6"` noma `12'-6"` | Izinyawo ezi-12 namaintshi ayi-6 (150) |
| `12'6-1/2"` | Izinyawo ezi-12 namaintshi ayi-6½. Sebenzisa uhlamvu lokuhlukanisa ngaphambi kwengxenye: i-{{key:Space}} iyi-{{key:Enter}} emugqeni womyalo. |
| `12'` | Izinyawo ezi-12 |
| `6"` noma `6` | Amaintshi ayi-6: inombolo ephelele iyamaintshi |
| `1/2`, `6-1/2` | Izingxenye zisebenza kunoma iyiphi ifomethi |

Amaphoyinti athatha ubude obufanayo: `@12'6",0` noma `@10'<90`. Izingosi zihlala ngamadigri.

Ifomethi ibonakala emlandweni ({{cmd:DIST}}, {{cmd:ID}}, {{cmd:LIST}}, {{cmd:AREA}}: izindawo njengamaintshi asquare nezinyawo ezisquare), ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}, kuzixhumanisi zebha yesimo, nasezilinganisweni **ezintsha**. Amashidi agcina amayunithi aphepheni awo (amamilimitha noma amaintshi) njengamadesimali.

:::note
Ifomethi yobude igcinwa emafayeleni e-DWG ne-DXF, futhi isilinganiso ngasinye sigcina esaso: umdwebo olondoloziwe uvuleka kabusha njengoba wawushiya.
:::

## Usayizi wamaphepha

Amashidi nokushicilelwa kwemodeli kusebenzisa lezi zayizi, kunoma iyiphi indlela:

| Iphepha | Usayizi | Iyunithi esidweni |
|---|---|---|
| ISO A4 | 297 × 210 mm | amamilimitha |
| ISO A3 | 420 × 297 mm | amamilimitha |
| ISO A2 | 594 × 420 mm | amamilimitha |
| ISO A1 | 841 × 594 mm | amamilimitha |
| ISO A0 | 1189 × 841 mm | amamilimitha |
| Letter | 11 × 8.5 in | amaintshi |
| Legal | 14 × 8.5 in | amaintshi |
| Tabloid / ANSI B | 17 × 11 in | amaintshi |
| ANSI C | 22 × 17 in | amaintshi |
| ANSI D | 34 × 22 in | amaintshi |
| ARCH C | 24 × 18 in | amaintshi |
| ARCH D | 36 × 24 in | amaintshi |
| ARCH E | 48 × 36 in | amaintshi |

Amashidi amasha aqala ku-ISO A3 emidwebeni ye-metric nelungenamayunithi, nasku-Tabloid emidwebeni yamaintshi noma yezinyawo. Ukushicilelwa kwemodeli kuqala ku-ISO A4 noma ku-Letter ngendlela efanayo, bese kukhumbula okokugcina okukhethile. Amashidi avela emafayeleni avuliwe agcina usayizi wawo, abonakale njenge "Custom" uma engenye yalezi.

## Izikali

Isikali `1:n`isho ukuthi iyunithi eyodwa ephepheni ibonisa n zeyunithi efanayo emodeleni, ngakho 1:100 ibonisa i-1 m yesakhiwo njengamamilimitha ayi-10 ephepheni. I-CadCraft iyakubala lokhu kusuka emayunithini omdwebo nasemayunithini ephepha.

**Izikali zemetric**, ezitholakala kuwo wonke umdwebo: 10:1, 5:1, 2:1, 1:1, 1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:20, 1:25, 1:50, 1:100, 1:200, 1:250, 1:500, 1:1000, 1:1250, 1:2000, 1:2500, 1:5000, 1:10000.

**Izikali zezokwakha**, ezitholakala uma amayunithi eyamaintshi noma ezinyawo: 3" = 1'-0", 1-1/2" = 1'-0", 1" = 1'-0", 3/4" = 1'-0", 1/2" = 1'-0", 3/8" = 1'-0", 1/4" = 1'-0", 3/16" = 1'-0", 1/8" = 1'-0", 3/32" = 1'-0", 1/16" = 1'-0".

**Izikali zobunjiniyela**, ezitholakala uma amayunithi eyamaintshi noma ezinyawo: 1" = 10', 1" = 20', 1" = 30', 1" = 40', 1" = 50', 1" = 100'.

**Izikali zakho.** Bhala `SCALELISTEDIT` (noma `SCALELIST`) ukuze wengeze isikali sakho, njengo-1:75 noma isikali sendawo sephepha elingu-2 : 300 lomdwebo. `Add` ibuza igama, bese amayunithi ephepha namayunithi omdwebo (igama elifana no `1:75` linikeza izinombolo zalo; cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uzithathe). I-`Edit`, i-`Delete`, i-`List` ne-`Reset` zisebenza kuhlu lofanayo; i-`Reset` ibuyela kuhlu lwejwayelekile. Izikali zakho zivela ngemva kwejwayelekile kuhlu lwesikali se-viewport phezu kwe-Properties nakuxoxo lwe-{{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}}, i-viewport ngalesi sikali iyafundeka ngegama layo, futhi zigcinwa emdwebeni (DWG ne-DXF 2000) njengohlu lwesikali lomdwebo. Umdwebo ovulwe kolunye uhlelo uletha uhlu lwawo; izikali ezifana nezijwayelekile azikhonjwa kabili.

Uma i-viewport ilungiswa nge-{{ui:Bonisa imodeli yonke}} noma {{ui:Khetha iwindi kwimodeli…}}, i-CadCraft ikhetha isikali esijwayelekile esikhulu esilingana. I-{{ui:Lwanela imodeli yonke}} isetha isikali esinembile, esifundeka njengesilinganiso esilula.

## Usayizi ephepheni nasemodeleni

Usayizi wombhalo nezilinganiso useyiyunithi yendawo odweba kuyo.

| Ufuna ephepheni | Udwetshwe esidweni | Udwetshwe emodeleni ku-1:50 (umdwebo wamamilimitha) | Udwetshwe emodeleni ku-1/4" = 1'-0" (umdwebo wamaintshi) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Umbhalo we-2.5 mm | 2.5 | 125 | — |
| Umbhalo we-3/32" | 0.09375 | — | 4.5 |

Kwezilinganiso, gcina usayizi njengosayizi wephepha bese usetha isikali sejwayelekile ({{cmd:DIMSCALE}}) sibe yisikali sokushicilela: 50 ye-1:50, 48 ye-1/4" = 1'-0". Bheka [Setha usayizi wezilinganiso](topic:dimension-settings).

## Chaza ngesikali

Dweba imodeli ngosayizi ogcwele bese uvumela i-CadCraft ikwenzele izibalo ezingenhla.

1. Khetha umbhalo noma izilinganiso okufanele zigcine usayizi wazo ephepheni. Ku-Properties khetha {{ui:Okuchaza}}. I-{{ui:Usayizi ephepheni}} bese ifunda, isibonelo, `2.5 mm at 1:50`.
2. Bhala {{cmd:ANNOSCALE}} nesikali umdwebo ozoshicilelwa ngaso, njengo `1:100`.

Yonke imibhalo nezilinganiso ezichazayo ziyalungiswa ukuze zishicilelwe ngosayizi ofanayo ngesikali esisha: umbhalo we-2.5 mm ungu-125 ubude emdwebeni wamamilimitha ku-1:50 futhi ungu-250 ku-1:100. Ububanzi bekhefu buyashintsha nawo; umbhalo wesilinganiso, izimpandla namagebe ashintsha ndawonye (isikali sawo jikelele). Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, nezinto ezingezona ezichazayo azishintshwa ngaso sonke.

Isethingi igcinwa ku-DWG ne-DXF: ezinye izinhlelo zifunda ithegi elichazayo, ne-CadCraft futhi igcina isikali ngasinye sento esilungiselelwe kuso.

Uma ihhovisi lakho lisetha amabanga ephepha ku-Default Policies add-on (Izilungiselelo › Jikelele › Default Policies › Annotation sizes on paper), i-{{cmd:ANNOSCALE}} iphinde isethe ukuphakama umbhalo omusha oqala ngakho kulelo hhovisi lamanothi ngalesi sikali. Bheka [Default Policies](topic:default-policies).

### Into eyodwa ezikalini eziningi

Ishidi ngokuvamile libonisa uhlelo lofanayo ku-1:100 nengxenye yalo ku-1:50. Nika amanothi nezilinganiso zombili izikali bese i-viewport ngayinye iwabonisa ngosayizi wephepha wawo:

1. Khetha umbhalo nezilinganiso ezichazayo bese ubhala `OBJECTSCALE`.
2. Khetha `Add` bese ubhala isikali, isibonelo `1:50`. (`Current scale` yengeza isikali sokuchaza somdwebo; `List` ibonisa okugcinwa yinto ngayinye; `Delete` isusa eyodwa.)

I-viewport esikalini sayo okusohlwini lwento iyibonisa ngosayizi walesi sikali: umbhalo we-2.5 mm udwetshwa ungu-250 ubude nge-viewport ka-1:100 kanti ungu-125 nge-viewport ka-1:50, esikrinini nasekushicilelweni. Into ihlala lapho ikhona; usayizi wayo kuphela oyahluka. Ku-viewport esikalini esingekho ohlwini, into ibonisa usayizi wayo wamanje, kuze ubhale `ANNOALLVISIBLE` bese ukhetha `OFF`: khona iyashiywa lapho. I-{{cmd:ANNOSCALE}} iyalungisa izinto ezidweba isikali esisha futhi ishiya ezinye njengazo.

Ohlu lugcinwa ku-DWG ne-DXF njengedatha yayo ye-CadCraft. Olunye uhlelo lubona ngalinye njengelichazayo, esikalini esisodwa lelo elilungiselelwe kulo. Iswishi ye-ANNOALLVISIBLE ayigcinwa efayeleni.

## Ubukhulu bomugqa nezinhlobo zemigqa

Ingqimba, noma into, ingaba **nobukhulu bomugqa** (0.05 kuya ku-2.11 mm) kanye **nohlobo lomugqa** (oluqhekeziwe, olufihliwe, olumaphakathi, olungabonakali, amachashaza…). Zisethe ephaneli yesokudla: lapho kungakhethwa lutho, i-{{ui:Uhlobo lomugqa}} ne-{{ui:Ubukhulu bomugqa}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}} yizona izinto ezintsha zizithola (i-{{ui:Ngongqimba}} ukulandela ingqimba yazo), kanti ezimbili ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ungqimba …}} ziyezingqimba yamanje. Lapho izinto zikhethiwe, lezi zimbili namakhodi e-{{ui:Isikali sohlobo lomugqa}} ziyashintsha lezo zinto. Imiyalo ngu-{{cmd:LINETYPE}} (`LT`), {{cmd:LWEIGHT}} (`LW`) ne-{{cmd:LTSCALE}} (`LTS`).

Izinhlobo zemigqa zibonakala esikrinini nasekushicilelweni. Amachashaza azo asezinhlamvwini zomdwebo: umdwebo wemetric usebenzisa usayizi we-ISO (DASHED: amachashaza ayi-12.7 mm), umdwebo wamaintshi usebenzisa wamaintshi (0.5"). Uma amachashaza ebonakala emancane noma amakhulu kakhulu, shintsha i-{{ui:Isikali sohlobo lomugqa}} (LTSCALE) yomdwebo: yonke ichashaza negebe liyaphindwa ngayo; isikali sento siphinda siphindwe futhi. Iphethini eyincane kakhulu ukuthi ingabonakali idwetshwa njengomugqa oqinile.

Ubukhulu bomugqa buphrintwa ekushicilelweni, ngamamilimitha ephepheni noma ngabe yisikali esinjani. Imigqa enobukhulu be-{{ui:Okuzenzakalelayo}} isebenzisa ububanzi bepeni okukhethwe kuxoxo lwe-{{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}} (0.13 kuya ku-0.70 mm; 0.25 mm ekuqaleni); ubukhulu be-0 buphrintwa njengomugqa omncane kakhulu. Esikrinini umugqa ngamunye ububanzi bepikseli eyodwa uze ucindezele i-{{ui:LWT}} kwibha yesimo (noma ubhale {{cmd:LWDISPLAY}}): khona ubukhulu obungaphezu kwe-0.25 mm budweba bubanzi (0.5 mm = amapikseli ama-2) emodeleni, nangobubanzi bayo beqiniso ephepheni esidweni. Ukukhetha kugcinwa kule browser.

## Izinga lehhovisi

Ihhovisi lingagcina ukuphakama kombhalo wephepha, izingqimba, izinkundla zesihloko nezifanekiso ekurekhodweni elilodwa, izinga lehhovisi. I-CadCraft iyalifunda ngalo mlandu: irekhodi lomdwebo ngokwalo (lisethwe nge-{{cmd:OFFICESTANDARD}} › `Import`, ligcinwa efayeleni), izinga elabelwana ngalo ithimba lakho, bese ama-Default Policies akho. Umbhalo nezilinganiso ezichazayo zithatha ukuphakama kwazo ephepheni esikalini sokuchaza kuleyo. Izinga elingenalutho alishintshi lutho. I-{{cmd:OFFICESTANDARD}} › `Layers` yengeza izingqimba zehhovisi ezingekho emdwebeni.
