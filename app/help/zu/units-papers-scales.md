## Amayunithi omdwebo

Udweba ngobukhulu obugcwele, ngamayunithi omdwebo. Okumelwa yiyunithi eyodwa yisethingi yomdwebo **Amayunithi**:

1. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze kungakhethwa lutho, ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}}, khetha {{ui:Amayunithi}}: {{ui:Akunayunithi}}, {{ui:Amamilimitha}}, {{ui:Amasentimitha}}, {{ui:Amamitha}}, {{ui:Amainshi}} noma {{ui:Amafidi}}.

Izilinganiso zingabhala ubude bazo ngamamilimitha, amasentimitha, amamitha noma amakhilomitha noma yiyiphi iyunithi yomdwebo: bona [Izilinganiso nge-mm, cm, m noma km](topic:dimension-settings#dimensions-in-mm-cm-m-or-km).

Ukushintsha amayunithi akulinganisi lutho. Kushintsha ukuthi izikali zifundwa kanjani emashidini nasemishicilelweni, nokuthi zikhethwa zikuphi izikali. Umdwebo ovulwe ku-DWG noma i-DXF ugcina amayunithi agcinwe kufayela; isigaba sangakwesokunxele esithi {{ui:Umdwebo}} awabonisa.

Umdwebo {{ui:Akunayunithi}} uthathwa njengoyunithi wephepha: amamilimitha ephepheni le-ISO, amainshi ephepheni le-US.

## Amafidi namainshi, namafrakshini

Ukuthi ubude **bubhalwa kanjani futhi bufakwa kanjani** kuyisethingi ehlukile: ifomethi yobude. Ayishintshi ijometri, ishintsha kuphela ukuthi izinombolo zibukeka kanjani nokuthi yini ongayibhala.

1. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze kungakhethwa lutho, ethebhini le-{{ui:Imodeli}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}}, khetha {{ui:Ifomethi yobude}}: {{ui:Amadesimali}} (`150.5`), {{ui:Ezokwakha}} (`12'-6 1/2"`), {{ui:Ubunjiniyela}} (`12'-6.5"`), {{ui:Amafrakshini}} (`150 1/2`) noma {{ui:Isayensi}} (`1.505E+02`).
3. Khetha {{ui:Ukunemba}}: izinhlamvu zamadesimali, noma ingxenye encane kakhulu (1/16 yisinqumo ejwayelekile kwezokwakha).

Noma bhala {{cmd:UNITS}} (`UN`) bese uphendula imibuzo yayo emibili.

Ngo-{{ui:Ezokwakha}} noma {{ui:Ubunjiniyela}}, iyunithi eyodwa yomdwebo iyinci eyodwa, njengakwi-AutoCAD: setha {{ui:Amayunithi}} ku-{{ui:Amainshi}} nawo. Ungabe usebhala izinde ngamafidi namainshi noma yisiphi isikhundla lapho ubude bucelwa khona, nasebhokisini le-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}:

| Ubhala | Usho |
|---|---|
| `12'6"` noma `12'-6"` | 12 amafidi, amainshi ayi-6 (150) |
| `12'6-1/2"` | 12 amafidi, amainshi ayi-6½. Sebenzisa uhlamvu lwe-hyphen ngaphambi kwengxenye: {{key:Space}} iyi-{{key:Enter}} komugqa womyalo. |
| `12'` | 12 amafidi |
| `6"` noma `6` | 6 amainshi: inombolo eyodwa ilula ibe ngamainshi |
| `1/2`, `6-1/2` | Amafrakshini asebenza kunoma iyiphi ifomethi |

Amaphoyinti athatha ubude bofanayo: `@12'6",0` noma `@10'<90`. Izingosi zihlala ngama-degree.

Ifomethi ibonakala emlandweni ({{cmd:DIST}}, {{cmd:ID}}, {{cmd:LIST}}, {{cmd:AREA}}: izindawo njengamainshi ayisikwele namafidi ayisikwele), ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}, ku-coordinates ebhalweni lesimo, nakuzilinganiso **ezintsha**. Amashidi agcina amayunithi wephepha (amamilimitha noma amainshi) njengamadesimali.

:::note
Ifomethi yobude igcinwa emafayeleni e-DWG ne-DXF, futhi isilinganiso ngasinye sigcina esaso: umdwebo olondoloziwe uvuleka kabusha njengoba wawushiya.
:::

## Usayizi wamaphepha

Amashidi nemishicilelo yemodeli asebenzisa lezi zinhlobo zosayizi, nganoma iyiphi indlela:

| Iphepha | Usayizi | Iyunithi eshidini |
|---|---|---|
| ISO A4 | 297 × 210 mm | amamilimitha |
| ISO A3 | 420 × 297 mm | amamilimitha |
| ISO A2 | 594 × 420 mm | amamilimitha |
| ISO A1 | 841 × 594 mm | amamilimitha |
| ISO A0 | 1189 × 841 mm | amamilimitha |
| Letter | 11 × 8.5 in | amainshi |
| Legal | 14 × 8.5 in | amainshi |
| Tabloid / ANSI B | 17 × 11 in | amainshi |
| ANSI C | 22 × 17 in | amainshi |
| ANSI D | 34 × 22 in | amainshi |
| ARCH C | 24 × 18 in | amainshi |
| ARCH D | 36 × 24 in | amainshi |
| ARCH E | 48 × 36 in | amainshi |

Amashidi amasha aqala ku-ISO A3 emidwebeni yemetrikhi nengenayunithi, nasemidwebeni ngamainshi noma amafidi aqala ku-Tabloid. Imishicilelo yemodeli iqala ku-ISO A4 noma ku-Letter ngendlela efanayo, bese ikhumbula ukukhetha kwakho kokugcina. Amashidi avela kumafayela avuliwe agcina usayizi wawo, abonakale njenge "Okwenziwe ngokwezifiso" uma engelona ohlwini lwalezi.

## Izikali

Isikali `1:n` sisho ukuthi iyunithi eyodwa ephepheni ikhombisa u-n wayunithi efanayo emodeleni, ngakho i-1:100 ikhombisa u-1 m wesakhiwo njengo-10 mm ephepheni. I-CadCraft ikubala lokhu kusukela emayunithini omdwebo nasemayunithini ephepha.

**Izikali zemetrikhi**, ezinikezwa kuyo yonke imidwebo: 10:1, 5:1, 2:1, 1:1, 1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:20, 1:25, 1:50, 1:100, 1:200, 1:250, 1:500, 1:1000, 1:1250, 1:2000, 1:2500, 1:5000, 1:10000.

**Izikali zezokwakha**, ezinikezwa uma amayunithi eyamainshi noma amafidi: 3" = 1'-0", 1-1/2" = 1'-0", 1" = 1'-0", 3/4" = 1'-0", 1/2" = 1'-0", 3/8" = 1'-0", 1/4" = 1'-0", 3/16" = 1'-0", 1/8" = 1'-0", 3/32" = 1'-0", 1/16" = 1'-0".

**Izikali zobunjiniyela**, ezinikezwa uma amayunithi eyamainshi noma amafidi: 1" = 10', 1" = 20', 1" = 30', 1" = 40', 1" = 50', 1" = 100'.

**Izikali zakho.** Bhala `SCALELISTEDIT` (noma `SCALELIST`) ukwengeza isikali sakho, njengo-1:75 noma isikali sendawo se-2 ephepheni : 300 somdwebo. [Engeza (A)] icela igama, bese amayunithi ephepha namayunithi omdwebo (igama njengo `1:75` linikeza izinombolo zalo; cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uzithathe). [Hlela (E)], [Susa (D)], [Uhlu (L)] no-[Setha kabusha (R)] zisebenza kuhlu lufanayo; [Setha kabusha (R)] ibuyela kuhlu lwejwayelekile. Izikali zakho zivela ngemva kwezijwayelekile kuhlu lwesikali sesibuko sokubuka kuIzakhiwo nasekuxoxeni {{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}}, isibuko sokubuka kulesi sikali sifunda ngegama lacho, futhi zigcinwa emdwebeni (i-DWG ne-DXF 2000) njengohlu lwezikali zomdwebo. Umdwebo ovulwe kolunye uhlelo uletha uhlu lwawo; izikali ezifana nezijwayelekile azibonakali kabili.

Uma isibuko sokubuka sigxilisiwe nge-{{ui:Bonisa imodeli yonke}} noma {{ui:Khetha iwindi kwimodeli…}}, i-CadCraft ikhetha isikali esikhulu esijwayelekile esilingana. {{ui:Lwanela imodeli yonke}} kunalokho isetha isikali esithile, esifundeka njengesilinganiso esilula.

## Usayizi ephepheni nasemodelini

Usayizi wombhalo nezilinganiso usemayunithini wesikhala osidweba kuso.

| Ufuna ephepheni | Kudwetshwe ishidini | Kudwetshwe emodelini ku-1:50 (umdwebo wamamilimitha) | Kudwetshwe emodelini ku-1/4" = 1'-0" (umdwebo wamainshi) |
|---|---|---|---|
| umbhalo ongu-2.5 mm | 2.5 | 125 | — |
| umbhalo ongu-3/32" | 0.09375 | — | 4.5 |

Ezilinganisweni, gcina usayizi njengosayizi wephepha bese usetha isikali sonke ({{cmd:DIMSCALE}}) kusikali sokushicilela: 50 ku-1:50, 48 ku-1/4" = 1'-0". Bona [Setha usayizi wezilinganiso](topic:dimension-settings).

## Chaza ngesikali

Dweba imodeli ngobukhulu obugcwele bese uvumela i-CadCraft yenze izibalo ezingenhla ngawe.

1. Khetha umbhalo noma izilinganiso ezidinga ukugcina usayizi wazo ephepheni. Ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}, chofoza ibhokisi le-{{ui:Okuchaza}}. {{ui:Usayizi ephepheni}} bese ufunda, njengesibonelo, `2.5 mm at 1:50`.
2. Bhala {{cmd:ANNOSCALE}} nesikali umdwebo ozoshicilelwa ngaso, njengo `1:100`.

Yonke imibhalo nezilinganiso ezichazayo ilinganiswa ukuze ishicilelwe ngosayizi ofanayo kwisikali esisha: umbhalo ongu-2.5 mm uphakama ngu-125 emdwebeni wamamilimitha ku-1:50 kanti uphakama ngu-250 ku-1:100. Ububanzi bepharagrafu buyashintsha nawo; umbhalo wesilinganiso, izinhloko zemicibisholo nezikhala zishintsha ndawonye (isikali sawo sonke). Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, futhi izinto ezingachazi azishintshwa ngokuqinile.

Isethingi igcinwa ku-DWG ne-DXF: ezinye izinhlelo zifunda uphawu lokuchaza, ne-CadCraft nayo igcina isikali ngasinye into elinganiselwe kusona.

Uma ihhovisi lakho lisetha ukuphakama kwamaphepha ku-Default Policies add-on (Izilungiselelo › Global › Default Policies › Usayizi wokuchaza ephepheni), {{cmd:ANNOSCALE}} iphinde isethe ukuphakama umbhalo omusha oqala ngakho ube ukuphakama kwehhovisi kumanothi kulowo sikali. Bona [Default Policies](topic:default-policies).

### Into eyodwa ezikalini eziningi

Ishidi ngokuvamile libonisa uhlelo lufanayo ku-1:100 nengxenye yalo ku-1:50. Nikeza amanothi nezilinganiso zombili izikali bese isibuko ngasinye sizibonisa ngosayizi wephepha lazo:

1. Khetha umbhalo nezilinganiso ezichazayo bese ubhala `OBJECTSCALE`.
2. Khetha [Engeza (A)] bese ubhala isikali, njengo `1:50`. ([Isikali samanje (C)] engeza isikali sokuchaza somdwebo; [Uhlu (L)] ibonisa ukuthi yiyiphi into egcina; [Susa (D)] isusa eyodwa.)

Isibuko sokubuka esinesikali esisohlwini lwento sibonisa yona ngosayizi walesi sikali: umbhalo ongu-2.5 mm udwetshwa uphakama ngu-250 ngesibuko se-1:100 futhi ngu-125 ngesibuko se-1:50, esikrinini nasekushicilelweni. Into ihlala lapho isiphi khona; usayizi wayo kuphela oyahluka. Esibukweni sokubuka esinesikali esingesona ohlwini lwento into ibonakala ngosayizi inawo, kuze ubhale `ANNOALLVISIBLE` bese ukhetha `OFF`: khona ishiywa lapho. {{cmd:ANNOSCALE}} ilinganisa kabusha izinto ezidwebe isikali esisha futhi ishiye ezinye njengoba zinjalo.

Uhlu lugcinwa ku-DWG ne-DXF njengedatha yeCadCraft uqobo. Olunye uhlelo lubona into ngayinye njengechazayo, ngesikali esisodwa elilinganiselwe kusona. Iswishi ye-ANNOALLVISIBLE ayigcinwa kufayela.

## Ubukhulu bomugqa nezinhlobo zemigqa

Ungqimba, noma into, ingaba **nobukhulu bomugqa** (0.05 kuya ku-2.11 mm) kanye **nohlobo lomugqa** (oluqhekiwe, olufihliwe, olumaphakathi, olufiphele, amachashazi…). Zisethe kuphaneli yesokudla: lapho ungakhethile lutho, {{ui:Uhlobo lomugqa}} ne-{{ui:Ubukhulu bomugqa}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}} yizinto ezintsha ezithola (ukuze alandele ungqimba lwazo {{ui:Ngongqimba}}), futhi ezimbili ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ungqimba …}} ziyizinto zongqimba lwamanje lwalo. Uma izinto zikhethiwe, lezi zimbili imigqa nomugqa we-{{ui:Isikali sohlobo lomugqa}} ziguqula lezo zinto. Imiyalo ngu-{{cmd:LINETYPE}} (`LT`), {{cmd:LWEIGHT}} (`LW`) kanye {{cmd:LTSCALE}} (`LTS`).

Izinhlobo zemigqa zibonakala esikrinini nasezishicilelweni. Izikhala zazo zisemayunithini omdwebo: umdwebo wemetrikhi usebenzisa usayizi we-ISO (DASHED: izikhala ezingu-12.7 mm), umdwebo wamainshi usebenzisa wamainshi (0.5"). Uma izikhala zibonakala zincane kakhulu noma zikhulu kakhulu, shintsha {{ui:Isikali sohlobo lomugqa}} (LTSCALE) womdwebo: yonke isikhala nesikhala siphindwa ngayo; isikali sento ngayo siphinda futhi. Iphethini encane kakhulu ukuthi ingabonakali idwetshwa njengomugqa oqinile.

Ubukhulu bomugqa buyashicilelwa ezishicilelweni, ngama-mm ephepheni noma yisiphi isikali. Imigqa enobukhulu bomugqa {{ui:Okuzenzakalelayo}} isebenzisa ububanzi bepeni okukhethwe kuxoxo {{ui:Shicilela ku-PDF}} (0.13 kuya ku-0.70 mm; 0.25 mm ukuqala ngayo); ubukhulu bomugqa ongu-0 bushicilela njengomugqa omncane kakhulu. Esikrinini umugqa ngamunye ubanzi kangangophikseli oyedwa kuze ucindezele {{ui:LWT}} kusilawuli sesimo (noma ubhale {{cmd:LWDISPLAY}}): khona ubukhulu bomugqa obungaphezu kwe-0.25 mm buba banzi (0.5 mm = amaphikseli amabili) emodelini, nasephepheni ngobubanzi bawo bangempela ishidini. Ukukhetha kugcinwa kulesi siphequluli.
