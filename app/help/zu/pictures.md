## Okuzokwenza

Ilogo yenkampani efakwe ebhulokhini lesihloko, equleywe ngengxenye ofuna yona, ne-PDF lapho ishicilelwa ngokucaca kwayo.

## Faka isithombe

1. Bhala {{cmd:IMAGEATTACH}} (noma `IAT`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Khetha ifayela le-PNG noma le-JPEG.
2. `Specify the lower-left corner`: chofoza, noma bhala iphoyinti.
3. `Specify its width`: ububanzi kumayunithi omdwebo. {{key:Enter}} igcina ububanzi obunikeziwe: iyunithi eyodwa ngephikseli, noma ububanzi owabunikeza leli fayela ngesikhathi esedlule (isaziso sithi yiluphi).
4. `Specify its rotation angle`: {{key:Enter}} ukuze ungabi nalutho.

Isithombe siyinto eyodwa: sikhethe ngohlaka lwaso, bese uyahambisa, ukopishe, ujikeleze, ulinganise noma ususe njengebhulokhi noma yiluphi.

:::note
Ifayela lesithombe lihlala nomdwebo uma livulekile. I-DWG elondoloziwe iqamba ifayela, njengoba nezinye izinhlelo zenza; alibambi amaphikseli. Gcina isithombe eduze komdwebo.
:::

## Namathisela isithombe-skrini noma isithombe okopishiwe

1. Kopisha isithombe (isithombe-skrini nge{{pick:windows=Windows key+Shift+S|mac=Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4|linux=ithuluzi lakho lokuthatha isithombe-skrini}}, noma Kopisha isithombe kolunye uhlelo noma kusiphequluli).
2. Khomba lapho kufanele kuye khona emdwebeni bese ucindezela {{key:Mod+V}}.

Ikhona elingaphansi kwesobunxele lesithombe lifika esikhombini (noma phakathi kokubuka), ububanzi bawo buyi-40 % kunalokho obukubonayo; hambisa, linganisa futhi uqamule njengoba nanoma yisiphi isithombe. Igama layo liyigama lomdwebo elinesijobelelo esithi `-picture-1.png` (bese `-2`, …), futhi umlando uyakusho lokho.
{{ui:Hlela}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Namathisela isithombe}} yenza okufanayo kusuka kumenyu (isiphequluli singabuza kuqala).

I-DWG igcina kuphela igama lefayela lesithombe, ngakho ukulondoloza umdwebo nakho kulondoloza isithombe njengefayela lso siqu. Gcina kokubili efolda elifanayo: umdwebo ubonisa isithombe kusukela lapho. Esiphequluleni kuba yikhophi yesibili elandwayo, nomlando uyakusho lokho.
Uma kungekho lutho ngaphandle kwezinto ze-CadCraft ebhodini lokopisha, {{key:Mod+V}} inamathisela lezo, njengakuqala.

## Namathisela indawo ekopishwe ku-MarkupCraft

{{ui:Kopisha indawo}} ku-MarkupCraft ikopisha ingxenye yeshidi njengesithombe esazi ubukhulu bayo. I-CadCraft iyinamathisela ngalolo usayizi.

1. Ku-MarkupCraft, kopisha indawo (unxande, indilinga noma isimo esikhululekile; {{ui:Kopisha njenge-overlay}} uma ufuna ukubona umdwebo wakho ngayo).
2. Ku-CadCraft, cindezela {{key:Mod+V}}, noma bhala {{cmd:PASTEAREA}} (noma `PAREA`), noma khetha {{ui:Hlela}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Namathisela indawo ekopishiwe}}.
3. `Specify the lower-left corner of the copied area`: chofoza, noma bhala iphoyinti. Uhlaka lobukhulu balendawo lulandela isikhombi.

Ukuthi ibe nobukhulu obungakanani:

- **Ishidi lalinesikali somdwebo:** ngobukhulu bayo bangempela, ngamayunithi omdwebo (incazelo ye-1:50 yesitebhisi se-2 m ibe ngu-2 m ubude emdwebeni wamamitha, 78.74 emdwebeni wamainshi). Akukho okunye okubuzwayo.
- **Ishidi alinasikali:** i-CadCraft iveza usayizi walo ephepheni ibuze isikali: bhala `100` ukuze uthole ishidi le-1:100, cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze usayizi wephepha, noma bhala `P`, chofoza amaphoyinti amabili esithombeni esinamathiselwe bese ubhala ukuthi ahlukene kangakanani empeleni (njengoba {{cmd:SCANSCALE}} yenza). Ikhona elingaphansi kwesobunxele lihlala lapho ulibeka khona.

Umdwebo ongenayo amayunithi uthatha amamilimitha, njengoba kwenza i-PDF underlay. I-overlay igcina iphepha lalo libonakala ngaphansi futhi iqale ifiphele njengoba yakopishwa; ngaphandle kwendilinga noma kwesimo esikhululekile nakho kubonakala ngaphansi. Indawo enamathisiwe iyisithombe njengoba kunjalo nanoma iyiphi enye: hambisa, jikeleza, linganisa, qamuka noma sula, futhi konke ukunamathisela kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Igcinwe njenge-DWG iyi-IMAGE enokufiphala kwayo nokubonakala kwayo.

{{key:Mod+V}} inamathisela indawo uma isithombe esebhodini lokopisha singesendawo, noma uma indawo ikopishwe ngemva kwanoma yini oyikopishile ku-CadCraft (nasemahoreni ayi-6 edlule). Ngaphandle kwalokho inamathisela njengakuqala. {{cmd:PASTEAREA}} inamathisela indawo eyikopishwe ekugcineni noma nini.

## Bonisa izithombe i-DWG ezisebenzisa

I-DWG igcina kuphela lapho izithombe zayo zazikhona, ngakho i-CadCraft ibonisa ngayinye njengohlaka negama layo lefayela.

1. Bhala {{cmd:IMAGE}} (noma `IM`). Umugqa womyalo ubala ifayela ngalinye lesithombe nokuthi liyabonakala yini.
2. Khetha amafayela (amaningana ngesikhathi). Ifayela elinegama eliqukethe elibizwa umdwebo nalo liyabalwa, ngoba ukukhishwa kuvame ukungeza isijobelelo sephrojekthi.

## Qamula isithombe

1. Bhala {{cmd:IMAGECLIP}} (noma `ICL`) bese ukhetha isithombe.
2. Khetha `R` bese uchofoza namakhona amabili, noma `P` bese uchofoza amaphoyinti e-polygon, bese {{key:Enter}}. `D` isusa ukuqanyulwa.

## Shicilela izithombe

Izithombe zingena ku-PDF njengezithombe, ngokucaca kwazo nangaphansi kwemigqa. Kuxoxo {{ui:Dweba}}, {{ui:Izithombe}} ingazinciphisa kuze kube ngu-600, 300 noma 150 dpi ukwenza ifayela libe ncane. I-JPEG ingena njengoba iyiyo.

## Izinto zezinye izinhlelo (OLE)

Umdwebo ungabamba izinto ezenziwe yenye inhlelo, njengesithombe esivela ohlelweni lokupenda noma indawo yohlelo lwezibalo (izinto ze-OLE). I-CadCraft ayiziqali noma ayihlele, futhi ayikwazi ukufaka ezintsha. Yenza lokhu:

- **Ku-DXF**, into ye-OLE ivula njengohlaka. Uma into ilondoloze isithombe sayo futhi lesi sithombe sibe yi-bitmap, isithombe sidwetshwa ngaphakathi kohlaka; kungenjalo uhlaka lunelebula lohlobo lwento, njengo "Microsoft Excel Worksheet (OLE)". Ungahambisa, ukopishe nokususa uhlaka njengebhulokhi. {{cmd:SAVEAS}} ku-DXF 2000 iyibhala njenge-OLE futhi, idatha yayo ingashintshiwe.
- **Ku-DWG**, izinto ziyagcinwa futhi ziyabhalwa uma ulondoloza njenge-DWG, ngebyte ngebyte, kodwa **azibonakali**: i-DWG igcina indawo yento ngaphakathi kwedatha yayo, futhi i-CadCraft ayikafundi. Umlando uyakusho ukuthi zingaki ezigciniwe. Bhala {{cmd:OLELIST}} ukuze ubone ukuthi zini (uhlobo, usayizi, noma ngayinye ilondoloze isithombe yini) futhi ubeke ikhophi yesithombe lapho ukhetha khona: **Beka** icela inombolo, ikhona elingaphansi kwesobunxele nobubanzi, bese idweba uhlaka lapho kukhona isithombe khona. Leyo khophi iwuhlaka ongalihambisa noma ususe; into ngokwayo ihlala emdwebeni futhi isabhalwa uma ulondoloza.
- Into eyavela ku-DXF ilondolozwa ku-DWG njengohlaka nelebula kuphela; umlando uyakusho lokho. Londoloza njenge-DXF 2000 ukugcina into ngokwayo.
- Izithombe ezingamafayela e-metafile (ezigciniwe ezinhlelweni eziningi) azidwetshwa: kubonakala uhlaka nelebula esikhundleni salokho.
