## Okuzakwenziwa

Yenza isitayela sombhalo esibizwa ngokuthi `Notes` esinefonti nobude saso, usisebenzise embhalweni omusha, bese kamuva ushintsha isitayela ukuze zonke izinothi ezisisebenzisayo zishintshe ngesikhathi esisodwa.

## Izinyathelo

1. Thayipha {{cmd:STYLE}} (`ST`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Text style}} ethuneni le-{{ui:Draw}}. Ingxoxo ye-{{ui:Text style}} ibala izitayela edwetshweni. Umdwebo ngamunye unawo `Standard`.
2. Chofoza {{ui:New…}}, thayipha `Notes` njengegama lesitayela esisha bese uchofoza {{ui:OK}}. Umugqa wesimo uthi "Isitayela sombhalo Notes senziwe futhi sasethwa njengamanje."
3. Ku-{{ui:Font}}, khetha noma uthayipha igama lefonti. Setha {{ui:Height (0 = ask each time)}} ibe yinombolo, njengo `2.5`, ukuze wonke umbhalo omusha uthole lobo bude. Shiya kungu-`0` ukuze ubuzwe njalo.
4. Setha {{ui:Width factor}} no-{{ui:Oblique angle (°)}} uma ufuna izinhlamvu ezincane, ezibanzi noma ezithambekile. Umugqa wesampula ubonisa umphumela.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Close}}. Dweba umbhalo nge-{{cmd:TEXT}} noma {{cmd:MTEXT}}: umbhalo omusha uthatha isitayela samanje.
6. Ukuze usebenzise isitayela osenze ngaphambili, vula ingxoxo futhi, uyikhethe ohlwini bese uchofoza {{ui:Set current}}.
7. Ukuze ubeke isitayela embhalweni osuvele udwetshiwe, khetha umbhalo, vula ingxoxo, khetha isitayela bese uchofoza {{ui:Apply to selected text}}.

## Shintsha isitayela kamuva

Shintsha ifonti, ukuphakama, ububanzi noma ukuthambekela kwesitayela engxoxweni, bese yonke imbhalo esiyisebenzisayo iyalandela, ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa ({{key:Mod+Z}}). Umugqa wesimo uthi imbhalo emingaki ilandele.

## Izinothi

- {{ui:Delete}} iyenqaba `Standard`, nesitayela umbhalo esisasisebenzisa: shintsha lowo mbhalo uye kwesinye isitayela kuqala.
- Izitayela zigcinwa ethebhleni le-STYLE womdwebo, ngakho amafayela e-DXF ne-DWG ziyazigcina.
- Emugqeni womyalo, `-STYLE` yenza okufanayo ngezinketho (Current, New, Font, Height, Width, Oblique, Delete, List).
- Ifonti engatholakali kukhompyutha efunda umdwebo ibonakala ngobuso obunye.
