## Okuzokwenza

Ungenisa ifayela kudwebo bese uqinisekisa ukuthi lingena ngosayizi ofanele.

## Izinyathelo

1. Lingenise njengokujwayelekile: `INSERT` › File, `XATTACH`, `POINTCLOUDATTACH`, noma {{ui:Amafayela}} ephaneli yoMtapo.
2. Uma ifayela likhomba amayunithi alo, akukho okubuzwayo. Umugqa womyalo utshela amayunithi alo ifayela elikhombayo, nesikali esisetshenzisiwe uma ehlukile kwamayunithi odwebo.
3. Uma lingakhombi, ingxoxo ibuza **Amayunithi efayela**. Khetha ukuthi iyunithi eyodwa kufayela iyini, bese uchofoza {{ui:Sebenzisa lezi zinyunithi}}. Akukho okubekwayo ngaphambi kokuthi uphendule.
4. {{ui:Khansela}} (noma {{key:Esc}}) akulethi lutho.

## Amafayela akhomba amayunithi alo

- **DWG ne-DXF**: amayunithi okufaka odwebo (`$INSUNITS`). Ifayela elingawanaki libuzwa ngawo.
- **Amafu wamaphoyinti**: i-LAS ne-LAZ enohlelo lwamaphuzu, i-E57, i-PTX ne-PLY zikhomba amayunithi azo. Ezinye ziyabuzwa.
- **OBJ**: ayikaze ikhombe amayunithi. Uyabuzwa.
- **glTF ne-GLB**: amamitha, ngomthetho wefomethi ngokwayo.
- **Izithombe**: azinakho usayizi wazo. I-`IMAGEATTACH` icela ububanzi, futhi inikeze ububanzi obunikezwe lelo fayela ngaphambili.
- **Ukuskena**: ukuskena okukhomba ukuminyana kwakho (amachashazi ngeyinishi) kungena ngosayizi wangempela ngamayunithi odwebo; i-`SCANATTACH` isaqhubeka ibuza ububanzi.
- **I-add-on yamafu wamaphoyinti**: ibuza uma wakha ifu elifayela lalo lingakhombi iyunithi, futhi ibhale izinombolo zelifu ngeyunithi lalo ligcina.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ukuvula i-DWG noma i-DXF njengodwebo lwayo akubuzi lutho: umugqa womyalo utshela amayunithi afayela akhomba, noma ukuthi awakhombi lutho. I-{{ui:Izakhiwo}} isetha amayunithi odwebo.
- Uma nalo udwebo lungenawo amayunithi, ifayela alikwazi ukuguqulwa: libekwa inombolo ngenombolo futhi umugqa womyalo uyakusho lokho.
- Impendulo igcinwa kulelo fayela kulesi khompyutha, kuzo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft. Ngesikhathi esilandelayo lapho lingena awubuzwa, nomugqa womyalo utshela ukuthi amayunithi agciniwe asetshenzisiwe.
- Ukuze ubuzwe futhi: i-command palette ({{key:Mod+K}}) › **Buza futhi amayunithi amafayela angeniswe**.
