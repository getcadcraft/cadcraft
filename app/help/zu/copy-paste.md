## Okuzofunda

Ukukopisha izinto ku-clipboard, ukunamathisela lapho uchofoza khona noma endaweni yazo ngezixhumanisi zazo, ukuphinda ngophoyinta, ukusika, nokuzisa emdwebweni omunye. Konke lokhu sekukhona nakwiqembu {{ui:Hlela}} lethuluzi: {{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Namathisela}} nemenyu {{ui:Okuningi}}.

## Kopisha bese unamathisela lapho uchofoza

1. Khetha izinto: zichofoze, noma udonse iwindi.
2. Cindezela {{key:Mod+C}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Kopisha}} eqenjini {{ui:Hlela}}. Umugqa womyalo uthi zingaki izinto ezikopishiwe. Zihlala zikhethiwe.
3. Cindezela {{key:Mod+V}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Namathisela}}. Ikhophi ivela ku-cursor, ilenge kusukela ekhoneni elingaphansi-kwesobunxele lezinto ezikopishiwe.
4. Chofoza lapho leyo khona iya khona, noma thayipha iphoyinti njenge `2000,500` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Object snap nama-Ortho asebenza njengokujwayelekile.

Izinto ezinamathisiwe zikhethiwe, ngakho ungazinyakazisa noma uzishintshe ngokushesha. Isinyathelo esisodwa se-{{key:Mod+Z}} sisusa konke ukunamathisela.

:::tip
Ufuna ikhophi ilenge kuphoyinti oyikhethile, njengekhoni lodonga? Thayipha `COPYBASE`, chofoza iphoyinti lesisekelo, khetha izinto, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ukunamathisela kuzobeka leyo phoyinti lapho uchofoza khona. Ukuze zonke izikopi zilenge esikhungweni sazo esikhundleni sekhoni elingaphansi-kwesobunxele, shintsha {{ui:Iphoyinti lokunamathisela}} kuka [izilungiselelo ze-CadCraft](topic:settings).
:::

## Namathisela endaweni

Cindezela {{key:Mod+Shift+V}}, noma khetha {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}} kumenyu {{ui:Okuningi}}. Ikhophi ingena ezixhumanisweni zayo ezifanele ngqo ekukopishweni, ngaphandle kokuchofoza. Isetshenziswa ukuletha izinto kwesinye isidwebo endaweni efanayo, noma usuka kwishidi uye kwelinye.

## Phinda bese uhambisa

1. Khetha izinto.
2. Cindezela {{key:Mod+D}}, noma khetha {{ui:Phinda bese uhambisa}} kumenyu {{ui:Okuningi}}.
3. Ikhophi ihlala phezu kwelokuqala futhi inyakaza nophoyinta. Chofoza lapho iya khona. Ungathayipha futhi ukunyakaza okufana ne `@0,-600`, noma ibanga eliya ku-cursor.

{{key:Esc}} ngaphambi kokuchofoza akushiyi lutho. Ukuphinda kuyisinyathelo esisodwa se-undo futhi akuthinti i-clipboard.

## Sika

Cindezela {{key:Mod+X}}, noma khetha {{ui:Sika}} kumenyu {{ui:Okuningi}}. Izinto ziya ku-clipboard futhi ziyasulwa emdwebweni, ngesinyathelo esisodwa se-undo. Zibuyisele ngokucindezela {{key:Mod+V}} noma {{key:Mod+Shift+V}}.

## Phakathi kwemidwebo nezithebhu

I-clipboard igcina ikhophi yayo uma uvula enye idrowing, futhi yabelwana ngayo yonke ithebhu ye-CadCraft kulesi sibhrawuza. Kopisha kwenye ithebhu, bese ucindezela {{key:Mod+V}} kwenye.

- Amabhulokhi asetshenziswa yizinto aya nazo. Uma idrowing onamathisela kuyo sekunebhulokhi ngegama elifanayo, kusetshenziswa incazelo yawo, njengoba kwenza i-AutoCAD.
- Izingqimba ezisetshenziswa yizinto ziyangezwa, nombala nezilungiselelo ezazinazo.
- Ikhophi ayilinganiswa kabusha uma le midwebo isebenzisa amayunithi ahlukene: i-CadCraft iyasho lokho, futhi ungasebenzisa {{cmd:SCALE}} emva kwalokho.
- Ama-viewport ahlala ephepheni lawo; ayasuswa ekukopisheni.

:::note
I-clipboard ingeyokuqala ye-CadCraft. Awukwazi ukunamathisela izinto ze-CadCraft kwenye inhlelo, noma ukunamathisela ezivela kwenye inhlelo ku-CadCraft: londoloza njenge-DWG noma i-DXF. Ikhophi enkulu kakhulu ingase ingabelwani phakathi kwezithebhu; umugqa womyalo-ke uthi yithebhu yamanje kuphela engayinamathisela.
:::

## Izinkinga ezivamile

- **{{key:Mod+V}} inamathisele umbhalo emugqeni womyalo.** Ubuthayipha okuthile lapho, ngakho izinkinobho zasebenza kumbhalo. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze ususe, bese ucindezela {{key:Mod+V}} futhi.
- **Akukho okwenzekile, nebha yesimo ithi qeda umyalo wamanje.** Omunye umyalo uyasebenza. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} kuqala.
- **Ukunamathisela kufike kude.** Ilenge kusukela ekhoneni elingaphansi-kwesobunxele kwalokho okukopishile, okungase kube into ekude oyikhethe ngephutha. Sebenzisa {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}}, noma kopisha nge-`COPYBASE`.
- **"Nothing pasted" nesizathu.** Okuthe ithebhu yangaphambili yayishiye ku-clipboard akufundeki (kwonakele, noma kwashintshwa ngesandla). Kopisha izinto futhi.

Bona uhlu olugcwele lwezinkinobho ku-[Ikhibhodi nembewu](topic:keyboard), nayo yonke imibuzo ku-[Incwadi yemiyalo](topic:commands#clipboard).
