## Okuzofunda

Okubonwayo nokwenzeka ezintweni zakho lapho abantu abaningi besebenza emdwebeni munye ngesikhathi esifanayo. I-CadCraft Studio iqalisa futhi ijoyine iseshini ye-Live TeamWork (Yabelana ngeLive, Xhuma); leli khasi libheka umdwebo ngokwawo.

## Bonke badweba ngesikhathi esifanayo

Dweba futhi uhlele njengokujwayelekile. Izinguquko zakho zibonakala ngokushesha, ngisho nangaphambi kokuba abanye bazithole, futhi ezabanye zivela ekuskrinini lakho emzuzwini ngemva kokuba zenziwe. Yonke imiyalo iyasebenza: ukudweba, ukuhlela, izingqimba, amabhulokhi, amashidi, izilinganiso, izikhawulo.

Abantu ababili bangashintsha into efanayo. Uma ushintsha izinto ezihlukile, njengombala nephoyinti lokuphela komugqa oyedwa, zombili izinguquko ziyagcinwa. Uma ushintsha into efanayo, ushintsho lwamuva luyanqoba.

Okuyokuwena wedwa: ingqimba yamanje, umbala nokuphakama kombhalo wezinto ezintsha, izilungiselelo zakho zezilinganiso, ukubuka kwakho, ukwahlukanisa kwesikrini nokuhlelwa kwe-UCS. Umdwebo wabelwana ngawo; indlela oyibona ngayo ayibelwani.

## Abanye abantu emdwebeni wakho

- **Amakhesha:** isikhombi sangabanye sibonakala negama labo ngombala wabo, nomyalo abawusebenzisayo. Ubona amakhesha abantu abasebenza eduze kwalokho okusekuskrinini lakho.
- **Ukukhetha:** okukhethwe ngumuntu kuphawulwa ngombala wakhe.
- **Okuhlelwayo:** into ekhethwe ngumuntu inelebula elifana no-"Ana uyayihlela". Ungayishintsha umbala noma uyihambise, kodwa awukwazi ukuyisusa, uyiqhephulele noma uyitayiphe kabusha size bayiyeke. Ukukhetha kukutshela ukuthi ubani ayibambile.
- **Owayishintsha ekugcineni:** khetha into eyodwa abanye ababe bayishintshile kule seshini bese umugqa womyalo utshela ukuthi ubani nokuthi ushintsho luyini ("Ben ukushintshe ekugcineni: CIRCLE."). Ipaneli ye-Live ye-Studio ibala zonke izinguquko zakamuva ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izinguquko zakamuva}}. Akukho lokhu okugcinwa emdwebeni: kuhlala njengeseshini.

## Bamba ungqimba noma indawo ngenkathi usebenza lapho

Emdwebeni omkhulu onabantu abaningi, tshela ukuthi iyiphi ingxenye engeyakho okwamanje:

1. Thayipha `LIVEHOLD` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Khetha `L` bese uthayipha igama lengqimba ({{key:Enter}} igcina ingqimba yamanje), noma khetha `A` bese ukhetha amakhona amabili endawo.
3. Ukuhlelwa kwabanye kulowo mqimba, noma kwezinto ezisendaweni leyo, kuyabuyiswa emuva futhi batshelwa ukuthi usebenza lapho. Ubona indawo yakho njengohlaka oluqhekeziwe; abanye bayibona ngombala wakho negama lakho, nezingqimba oziqinile zibhalwe ngakwesobunxele okungenhla komdwebo wabo.
4. `LIVEHOLD`, bese `R`, ikhulula konke okubambile. Ukushiya iseshini nakho kukhulula.

Noma ubani kuseshini angabamba, kuhlanganise nomsingathi; izikhiyo zomsingathi zisebenza phezu kwakho. Ukubamba akusoze kugcinwe efayeleni.

## Lapho ushintsho luthathwa emuva

Ikhophi yomsingathi yomdwebo ihlola ushintsho ngalunye. Ezinye azingeni, futhi i-CadCraft iyazithatha emuva ekuskrinini lakho ichaze isizathu emugqeni womyalo, isibonelo:

- omunye umuntu ususe into, noma ingqimba noma ibhulokhi edingekayo ngaphambi kwakho;
- omunye umuntu uhlela leyo nto, noma ubamba ingqimba yayo noma indawo okuyo;
- umsingathi ukhiyile;
- ibhulokhi oyifakayo isusiwe ngokuphelele, noma ingqimba oyisusayo isenezinto kuyo.

## Ukuhlehlisa kuhlehlisa umsebenzi wakho

{{key:Mod+Z}} no-{{ui:Hlehlisa}} zihlehlisa umyalo wakho wokugcina, wonke, bese zishiya umsebenzi wabanye ungathinteki. Uma kukhona umuntu oshintshe into efanayo selokhu, akukho ohlehliswayo futhi umugqa womyalo utshela ukuthi ubani oshintshile. {{key:Mod+Y}} yenza kabusha.

## Ukukhiya (umsingathi)

Umsingathi angavikela izinto noma ingqimba yonke ukuze kungabikho omunye oziguqulayo, kuhlanganise nomsingathi, kuze kukhishwe:

1. Khetha izinto bese uthayipha `LIVELOCK` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Noma uthayipha `LIVELOCK` ungakakhethi lutho bese ukhetha `L` ukuze ukhiye ingqimba ngegama: izinto ezengezwa kuleyo ngqimba kamuva nazo ziyakhiyeka.
2. Izinto ezikhiyiwe zidwetshwa ngomugqa oqhekeziwe nelebula elithi "Ikhiywe umsingathi" ekuskrinini sabo bonke.
3. `LIVEUNLOCK` iyakhulula ngendlela efanayo.

Izikhiyo zingeseshini: ziphela lapho iseshini iphela, futhi azisoze zigcinwe efayeleni.

## Joyina nefayela elifanayo

Uma uvule ifayela elifanayo nalo umsingathi avule (ifolda eyabelwana noma ikhophi efanayo), joyina kusuka kuwindi lakho le-CadCraft ku-Studio: vula iphaneli ye-{{ui:Live}} bese usebenzisa {{ui:Xhuma lapha}} nekhodi lomsingathi. Kuhamba kuphela ushintsho owenziwe kusukela umsingathi evule ifayela, ngakho umdwebo omkhulu ulungele masinyane. Uma ifayela lakho liyelinye, umdwebo womsingathi uyalishintsha ekuskrinini lakho (ifayela lakho lihlala njengalo). Lokhu kusebenza kuphela uma umdwebo wakho ungashintshiwe kusukela uwuvule.

## Abasizi kuseshini ephilayo

Umsizi oxhunyiwe kuwindi lakho le-CadCraft (bona usizo ngokusebenzisa abasizi be-AI) ujoyina iseshini njengomhlanganyeli wawo, obonakala kubo bonke njengo "Umsizi (nge-<igama lakho>)". Umsingathi uyamvumela, njengomuntu; umsizi womsingathi ujoyina ngokushesha. Izinguquko zakhe zinokuhlehlisa kwazo: i-{{key:Mod+Z}} yakho ihlehlisa umsebenzi wakho, nokuhlehlisa komsizi kuhlehlisa okwakhe kuphela. Uyashiya iseshini uma uyixhumula noma uyishiya, futhi usebenza kuseshini kuphela uma ungahlela.

## Joyina kusiphequluli

Isisebenzisi esingenayo i-Studio singaskena isithombe esingaphansi kwekhodi lomsingathi ngefoni, ithebulethi noma ikhompyutha kwinethiwekhi efanayo. I-CadCraft ivula kusiphequluli, icele igama lakho emugqeni womyalo, bese igcwala ngomdwebo womsingathi uma umsingathi ekuvumela ungene. Isixhumanisi sokumemela sivumela umuntu ukujoyina kwenye inethiwekhi: umsingathi udinga isihlalo se-Team Cloud Plan kwi-app, abanye bonke bajoyina mahhala, futhi isixhumanisi sisebenza kuphela uma iseshini yomsingathi ivulekile.

## Abantu abaningi emdwebeni owodwa

Konke ukushintsha kuya kubo bonke kuseshini, ngakho abantu abaningi, isikhathi eside kuthatha ushintsho ukufika kwabo ngabanye, futhi amakhompyutha ahamba kancane nokuxhumana okubi kuhlehla kuqala. Usayizi ojwayelekile weseshini ngu-20 abantu; umsingathi angavumela abaningi kuseshini eyodwa (bona usizo lokusebenza ndawonye ngokuphila). Uma umdwebo uzwakala ngathi uhamba kancane nabantu abaningi, abantu abancane, abaningi abahlela ngesikhathi esifanayo kancane, noma uxhumo oluhle kusiza.

## Lapho iseshini iphela

Umsingathi kuphela ogcina umdwebo. Uma umsingathi equnga iseshini, bonke abanye bagcina ikhophi engagcinwanga: yigcine uma ufuna ukuyigcina.

:::note
Ushintsho oluthathwe emuva ludwetshwa phezu kwezinto ezizungezile kuskrini sakho kuze kuvulwe kabusha umdwebo. Ama-hatch nezinto ezigcwele ezihlanganayo zingabonakala ngokwahlukile kunekhophi yomsingathi; umdwebo ngokwawo ufanayo.
:::
