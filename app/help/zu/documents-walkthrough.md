## Okuzokwenza

Ungu-Ana, usebenza ku-Riverside Clinic nomngane wakho, u-Ben. Amadrowing ephrojekthi ahlala kufolda eyodwa enifinyelela kuyo nombili (idrayifu ehlanganyelwe, ifolda evumelanisiwe, noma ifolda yelifu). Ekugcineni, ifayela eliye lasuka kwikhophi yalo yokuqala laya kwinguqulo ekhishiwe **A**, lashintshwa futhi njenge-**B.1**, ubonile ukuthi ubani wenzani, futhi ubonile ukuthi u-Ben ubona yini uma ubamba ifayela.

Konke kugcinwa kufolda efihliwe ethi `.ccvault` eduze kwamafayela akho: akukho seva, akukho okulayishwa (ifolda yelifu iyisicelo, bona isinyathelo sokugcina). Ukuze uthole zonke izinkinobho nemithetho endaweni eyodwa, bona isihloko ethi *Amadokhumenti: phuma, izinguqulo nokukhishwa* lapho i-app yakho inaso.

## 1. Setha ifolda yephrojekthi

1. Khetha {{ui:Amadokhumenti…}} kumenyu {{ui:Okuningi}} ethebhu ethi **View** (Ukubuka), noma thayipha `DOCUMENTS` (noma `DOCS`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:Khetha ifolda yephrojekthi}} bese ukhetha ifolda equkethe amadrowing ephrojekthi. (Amafolda ephrojekthi asebenza ku-Studio nakumabhrawuza afana ne-Chrome ne-Edge, lapho avula ifolda. Kwamanye amabhrawuza iphaneli iyasho lokho.)
3. Khetha {{ui:Shintsha}} eduze kwe-**You:** bese uthayipha igama lakho, `Ana` (uma ibuza, negama lalelikhompyutha). Iphaneli manje ithi *You: Ana on* nekhompyutha yakho. Igama lakho lihamba nakho konke okwenzayo.
4. Khetha {{ui:Qala isitoreji kule folda}} bese uthayipha igama lephrojekthi, `Riverside clinic`. Iphaneli ithi *Vault started for Riverside clinic.* (Isitoreji siqalisiwe ku-Riverside clinic.)

## 2. Beka ifayela ngaphansi kolawulo

1. Vula **Files not in the vault** (Amafayela angekho esitoreji) bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza}} eduze kwe-`plans/A-101.dxf`.
2. Livela ngaphansi kwe-**Files in the vault** (Amafayela esitoreji) ku-inguqulo **A.1**, isimo **Work in progress** (Umsebenzi uyenziwa). A.1 yikhophi layo lokuqala; isitoreji sigcina ikhophi yenguqulo ngayinye.

## 3. Phuma, shintsha, ngenisa

1. Khetha {{ui:Vula}} eduze kwe-`plans/A-101.dxf`. Idrowing ivula ku-CadCraft iphaneli ivaleke ukuze uyibone. Iphaneli isekude umyalo owodwa: `DOCUMENTS`.
2. Vula iphaneli futhi khetha {{ui:Phuma}}. Umugqa manje uthi **Checked out to you** (Iphumele kuwe). Ifayela lokukhiya lenziwe ku-`.ccvault/locks`; akekho omunye ongaphuma ngalelo fayela kuze ungenise.
3. Dweba okuthile: engeza umugqa, hambisa udonga. Akukho okungagcinwa phezu kwefayela lesitoreji uma idrowing ingaphumelelwe wena; {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} isebenza njalo ngekhophi yakho.
4. Vula iphaneli, khetha {{ui:Ngenisa}} bese uthayipha okushintshile (`Moved the door`). Iphaneli ithi *checked in as A.2* (ingenisiwe njenge-A.2). Ifayela ku-folda yephrojekthi manje yidrowing owayevulile, nokukhiya kuyasuswa. Ukungenisa ngaphandle koshintsho kugcina inguqulo.

Uma ushintsha umqondo, {{ui:Hlehlisa ukuphuma}} inikezela ifayela ngaphandle kwenguqulo entsha.

## 4. Buyekeza bese ukhipha

1. Khetha {{ui:Thumela ukuze ibukwe}}. Isimo sishintsha sibe **For review** (Ukuze ibukwe) futhi ifayela lifundeka kuphela (**Read-only**).
2. Ngaphambi kokuvuma, khetha {{ui:Izilungiselelo zephrojekthi}} bese uhlu abangavuma (igama elilodwa ngomugqa ngamunye), isibonelo `Ben` no-`Carla`, bese uthikha umthetho wokuthi umvumi kufanele abe omunye kunomlobi. Khetha {{ui:Londoloza izilungiselelo}}. Manje isitoreji sivimbela noma ubani omunye.
3. U-Ben (noma u-Carla) akhethe {{ui:Vuma}} bese uthayipha igama lomvumi. Isimo sishintsha sibe **Released** (Ikhishiwe) nenguqulo ibe **A**, nokuthi *approved by* negama. Ifayela elikhishiwe lifundeka kuphela. {{ui:Buyisela}} bekuyibuyisela ku-**Work in progress** nesizathu.

Igama lomvumi ngelithayipha ephaneli; liwukurekhodwa kwabavumile, hhayi ubufakazi bokuthi ungubani.

## 5. Inguqulo entsha

1. Ekuphumeni ifayela elikhishiwe, khetha {{ui:Inguqulo entsha}}. Uyibambe ukuze uthole uhlamvu olulandelayo.
2. Yishintshe, bese {{ui:Ngenisa}}. Inguqulo ibe **B.1**, umsebenzi uyenziwa. Ikhiphe ngendlela efanayo futhi iba **B**. {{ui:Hlehlisa ukuphuma}} ngaphambi kokungenisa kokuqala kushiya ku-**A**.

Izinhlamvu zenguqulo zilandelana A, B … Z, AA, AB. Uma istandard yakho ishiya ezinye izinhlamvu, izilungiselelo zephrojekthi ethi *Use the ASME Y14.35 sequence* zishiya I, O, Q, S, X no-Z ezinguqulweni ezilandelayo.

## 6. Bona okwenzekile

Khetha igama lefayela ukuze uvule imininingwane yalo:

- **{{ui:Umlando}}**: yonke imicimbi, eyakamuva kuqala, nokuthi ubani, ikhompyutha nesikhathi: *Checked in A.2* nguAna, *Released* nguBen, njll.
- **{{ui:Izinguqulo}}**: ithebula lezinguqulo. {{ui:Vula ikhophi}} ivula inguqulo njengekhophi evamile engeke ibhale phezu kwefayela lesitoreji; {{ui:Qhathanisa nalokhu}} idweba okungeziwe, okususiwe nokushintshiwe ngokuqhathaniswa nenguqulo yangaphambili, noma nanoma iyiphi enye.
- **{{ui:Kusetshenziswa}}**: amadrowing axhumene nalo (ama-xref).
- **{{ui:Kusetshenziswa ngu}}**: uhlu lwawo wonke amanye amadrowing esitoreji axhumene nalo: shintsha ipulani eyisisekelo bese wazi amashidi okufanele ahlolwe.

## Inguqulo ekhasini

Ngedrowing elivuliwe (nokwakheka kuyo), khetha {{ui:Ibhulokhi yokuguqulwa}}: ithebula lezinguqulo ezikhishiwe lidwetshwa phezulu ngakwesokudla kweshidi. Khetha {{ui:Gcwalisa amabhulokhi wesihloko}} ukugcwalisa izici zebhulokhi yesihloko ezimakwe `REV`, `VAULT.REV`, `VAULT.STATE`, `VAULT.DATE` noma `APPROVED_BY` ngenguqulo yefayela, isimo, usuku nomvumi. Uma uvuma ukukhishwa okulandelayo, ibhulokhi yokuguqulwa iyavuselelwa.

## 7. Okubona u-Ben lapho ubamba ifayela

Manje phuma ifayela futhi, bese ucela u-Ben ukuthi avule ifolda efanayo yephrojekthi kwikhompyutha yakhe bese evula iphaneli. Esigabeni sefayela ubona **Checked out by Ana on** ikhompyutha yakho **since** isikhathi, futhi akukho inkinobho ethi {{ui:Phuma}}. Uma ezama noma kanjalo, isitoreji siyamenqaba: ukukhiya kuyifayela esistimu engayenzi kabili, ngakho abantu ababili abakwazi ukubamba ifayela ngasikhathi sinye, nakuba kwesekhondi elifanayo, lapho ifolda isendisikhilini ku-Studio. (Kwibhrawuza ifolda ivumela okusondelene nakho kuphela, futhi iphaneli iyasho lokho ngaphansi kwe-**Folder** (Ifolda).)

Uma u-Ana engekho futhi u-Ben edinga ifayela, {{ui:Phula ukuphuma}} liyikhulula. Kumele athayiphe isizathu; sifakwa ku-log kanye nokuthi ukuphuma kwakukabani. Uma iphrojekthi ibhala abaphathi, kuphela bona abangakwenza. Ukuphuma okudala kakhulu kunomkhawulo wesikhathi (izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokuzenzakalela) kuphawulwa njenge-*isikhathi eside*. Ngemva kokuphula, ukungenisa kuka-Ana okwephuzile kuyenqatshwa, ngakho nina nidwebana ninganqandi ukubhalana phezu kwalokho.

## 8. Amafolda elifu

Ifolda yelifu iyifolda yephrojekthi egcinwa esitoreji sefu seqembu lakho, ngakho iqembu alidingi idrayifu ehlanganyelwe. Ku-Studio, ungene ku-akhawunti yakho ye-CadCraft, iphaneli ibonisa {{ui:Vula ifolda yelifu}} eduze kwe-{{ui:Khetha ifolda yephrojekthi}}. Khetha bese ukhetha ifolda, ebhalwe njenge-workspace / folder. Umnikazi wesikhala somsebenzi wenza ifolda kuqala, futhi isitoreji sefu seqembu kumele sisebenze. Konke okungenhla kusebenza ngendlela efanayo; iseva yenza ukukhiya, okuqinile phakathi kwabantu. Uma ungene ngemvume, iphaneli ikutshela ukuthi ungene. Kuthebhu evamile yebhrawuza inkinobho yelifu ayitholakali; sebenzisa i-Studio.

## Zama

Yenza izinyathelo eziyisishiyagalombili ngalapha ngekhophi yefayela langempela. Ezinyathelweni zabantu ababili udinga omunye umuntu, noma amawindi amabili nemigomo emibili. Uma uzama ngefolda yangempela ehlanganyelwe, ikhasi lokuhlola lomphakathi ethi **Community testing** linokuhlola okubuza lokhu ngqo, futhi lamukela imiphumela.
