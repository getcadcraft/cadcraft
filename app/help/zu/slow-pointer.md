I-CadCraft isebenza ebhrawuzini (i-CadCraft Studio nayo iyibhrawuza), ngakho akukho okwakhelwe ikhadi lezithombe elithile. Intanda idwetshwa yi-app, okusho ukuthi ingahamba kuphela ngesivinini i-app engadweba ngaso uhlaka.

## Vumela i-CadCraft inciphise okwengeziwe

1. Vula {{ui:Settings}} (ugiya ongaphezulu kwesokudla) bese uhlala kuthebhu {{ui:CadCraft}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:Smoother pointer on slower computers}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:On}}.

Uma sekuvuliwe, kusetshenziswa intanda yekhompyutha yakho esikhundleni sentanda edwetshiwe nebhokisi lokukhetha (nentanda ephelele esikrinini), futhi into ephansi kwesikhombi iyakhanya lapho esikhombi siphumule esikhundleni salapho sihamba. Ukubamba kwezinto, ukulandela nomugqa wokuhudula kusebenza njengakuqala.

{{ui:Automatic}} yindlela i-CadCraft eqala ngayo: ilanda isikhathi izinhlaka ezithatha ngaso uma usuka isikhombi, bese iyivula yodwa uma zihlala zinensimbi isikhathi esithile. Iyakutshela kanye lapho ikwenza lokho. {{ui:Off}} igcina intanda edwetshiwe noma ngasiphi isivinini.

## Uma isahamba kancane

- Hlola ukuthi ukusheshisa ngezingxenye kuvuliwe kubhrawuza yakho: vula `chrome://gpu` (noma `edge://gpu`) ethebhu entsha. Uma imigqa yokuqala ithi "Software only" noma "Disabled", ibhrawuza idweba ngaphandle kwekhadi lezithombe. Ukubuyekeza idrayivu yekhadi lezithombe kuvame ukukubuyisela.
- Vala amathebhu namaphrogramu anzima, bese uzama iwindi lebhrawuza elincane.
- Sitshele ngo-{{ui:Help}}, {{ui:Report a problem}}: chaza ukuthi usebenzisa ibhrawuza noma i-CadCraft Studio, inguqulo yayo, nokuthi ikhasi le-gpu lithini.
