## Okuzofunda

Indlela yokukhetha izinto umyalo ozisebenzisa ngayo. I-CadCraft inezindlela ezimbili zokusebenza, zombili zilungile:

- **Khetha kuqala, bese umyalo** (noun-verb). Khetha izinto, bese uthayipha `M` ye-Move. Umyalo uthatha ukukhetha kwakho bese uqhubeka ngombuzo wawo olandelayo.
- **Umyalo kuqala, bese ukhetha** (verb-noun). Thayipha `M`; umyalo ubuza `Khetha izinto:`; ukhetha, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

## Khetha into eyodwa ngesikhathi

1. Ngaphandle komyalo ogijima, chofoza umugqa. Uyakhanyiswa futhi kuvela izibambo zawo (izikwele ezincane). Iphaneli elingakwesokudla libonisa izakhiwo zawo.
2. Chofoza into enye. Iyangezwa ekukhetheni.
3. Bamba {{key:Shift}} bese uchofoza into ekhethiwe. Iyasuswa ekukhetheni.
4. Cindezela {{key:Esc}}. Akukho okukhethiwe.

## Iwindi nokuhlanganisa

Chofoza kanye endaweni engenalutho, hambisa, bese uchofoza futhi: unxande. Ukucindezela nokudonsa kudweba i-lasso yesandla esikhululekile, ngezinhlobo ezifanayo ezimbili (bheka [Lasso selection](topic:lasso-selection); izilungiselelo zingenza ukudonsa kudwebe unxande). Yiziphi izinto ozithola kuncike ekuqondeni:

| Uhamba | Ibhokisi | Ikhetha |
|---|---|---|
| Kusuka kwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla | Iwindi | Izinto **eziphelele ngaphakathi** kwebhokisi kuphela |
| Kusuka kwesokudla kuya kwesobunxele | Ukuhlanganisa | Izinto ezisebhokisini **nezinto ezithinta** lona |

1. Dweba imigqa nemijikelezo embalwa ehlanganayo.
2. Chofoza indawo engenalutho ngasekhohlo kwezinye izinto, hambela kwesokudla, bese uchofoza futhi. Izinto eziphelele ngaphakathi kuphela zikhethwa.
3. Cindezela {{key:Esc}}.
4. Chofoza indawo engenalutho ngakwesokudla, hambela ngakwesobunxele uwelule ezinye izinto, bese uchofoza futhi. Konke okuthintwa yibhokisi kuyakhethwa.
5. Bamba {{key:Shift}} bese udweba ibhokisi noma i-lasso phezu kwezinto ezikhethiwe. Ziyaphuma ekukhetheni.

## Khetha, bese usebenzisa umyalo

1. Khetha imijikelezo emibili.
2. Thayipha `M` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Umlando uthi `2 found`, futhi umugqa womyalo uya ngqo ku-`Cacisa iphoyinti lesisekelo`. Qeda ukuhambisa (bheka [Hambisa, kopisha, phendula, linganisa nokubuyisa ngasemuva](topic:modify-objects)).

Lapho umyalo uphela, ukukhetha kuyasulwa, ukuze umyalo olandelayo uqale kusha.

Imiyalo ethatha ukukhetha ngalendlela: {{cmd:ERASE}}, {{cmd:MOVE}}, {{cmd:COPY}}, {{cmd:ROTATE}}, {{cmd:SCALE}}, {{cmd:MIRROR}}, {{cmd:EXPLODE}}, {{cmd:BLOCK}} no-{{cmd:LIST}}. I-{{cmd:OFFSET}}, i-{{cmd:FILLET}} ne-{{cmd:CHAMFER}} zihlale zikucela ukuthi ukhethe izinto ngazinye; i-{{cmd:TRIM}} ne-{{cmd:EXTEND}} zithatha ukuchofoza noma indlela odonsa ngayo ezingxenyeni (bheka [Sika futhi welule](topic:trim-and-extend)).

## Yenza umyalo, bese ukhetha

1. Qinisekisa ukuthi akukho okukhethiwe ({{key:Esc}}).
2. Thayipha `E` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Umugqa wokuthayipha uthi `Khetha izinto:`.
3. Chofoza izinto, noma udonse amawindi nokuhlanganisa. Umlando ubala ngayinye oyikhethayo (`1 found`, `3 found`).
4. Cindezela {{key:Enter}}, cindezela {{key:Space}} noma chofoza ngakwesokudla ukuqeda ukukhetha.

Izinto ziyasulwa, bese umlando ubonisa ukuthi zingaki.

Ku-prompt ethi `Khetha izinto:` ungathayipha futhi:

- `ALL`, bese {{key:Enter}}: wonke izinto ongazihlela kuthebhu evulekile.
- `L` (noma `LAST`), bese {{key:Enter}}: into eyadwetshwe muva nje.
- `P` (Previous): izinto umyalo wokugcina owazisebenzisa, uma zisekhona.
- `F` (Fence): chofoza noma uthayipha amaphoyinti emgqeni; yonke into umugqa ohlanganisa kuyo iyangezwa. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda ifensi; `U` ithatha iphoyinti layo lokugcina emuva.
- `W` (Window) noma `C` (Crossing): amakona amabili, nganoma iyiphi indlela odonsa ngayo.
- `R` (Remove): kusukela manje, ukuchofoza, amawindi nezifensi kuthatha izinto **ngaphandle**. Ipromptu ithi `Susa izinto:`. `A` (Add) ibuyisela emuva.
- `?`: uhlu lwalezi zinketho emlandweni.

Lezi zinketho azibonakali kuprompt, njengakuAutoCAD.

## Khetha into eyodwa phezu kwezinye

Lapho izinto zigxumene noma zithinta, ukuchofoza kuthatha eseduze kakhulu. Ukuthola enye:

1. Mbambe isikhombi endaweni ingasunduzi.
2. Cindezela {{key:Tab}}. Into elandelayo lapho iyakhanyiswa, nebha yesimo ikhomba ukuthi iyiphi ("2 of 3: Line on layer Walls").
3. Cindezela {{key:Tab}} futhi uze kube yilena ofuna ukuyo ikhanyiswe, bese uchofoza ungayinyakazisi.

Ukunyakaza kwesikhombi kuqala kabusha. Lokhu kusebenza ngaphandle komyalo ogijima, ku-`Khetha izinto:` nalapho umyalo ukucela ukuthi ukhethe into eyodwa.

## Khetha ngomthetho: Ukukhetha okusheshayo

{{cmd:QSELECT}} (`QS`, noma {{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Quick select}}) ikhetha yonke into ehambisana nomthetho, njengawo wonke amasekhele anenhlanganisela ende engaphansi kwama-50, noma wonke amabhulokhi ebizwa ngokuthi DOOR-900.

1. Vula {{ui:Quick select}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:Object type}} (uhlu lubala uhlobo ngalunye), bese {{ui:Property}}: {{ui:Layer}}, {{ui:Color}}, nezinye izinhlobo {{ui:Length}}, {{ui:Radius}}, {{ui:Area}}, {{ui:Text height}}, {{ui:Text contents}}, {{ui:Block name}} noma {{ui:Hatch pattern}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:Operator}} no-{{ui:Value}}. `*` ifana nezinhlamvu ezihlanganyelwe (`DOOR-*`); {{ui:Select all}} ithatha yonke into yohlobo. Inani elisezansi libonisa ukuthi maningi angaki afanayo njengoba ukhetha.
4. {{ui:How to apply}}: faka okufanayo, noma konke okunye. {{ui:Apply to}} {{ui:Current selection}} inciphisa lokho owakukhethile; {{ui:Append to current selection}} ikwengeza.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Select}}. Bese usebenzisa umyalo ofunayo: usebenzisa ukukhetha.

## Khetha okufanayo

Khetha into eyodwa noma ngaphezulu bese uthayipha {{cmd:SELECTSIMILAR}} (`SIM`), noma khetha {{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Select similar}}. Yonke into ohlobo olufanayo kulayeri ofanayo nombala ofanayo ikhethwa; izinkomba zamabhulokhi zidinga ibhulokhi elifanayo, nokufiphaza kudinga iphethini efanayo. Thayipha `SE` ku-prompt yayo `Khetha izinto` ukuze ukhethe okufanele kufane.

## Khetha manje, sebenzisa kamuva

Thayipha `SELECT` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, khetha izinto ngenye yezindlela ezingenhla, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ziqhubeka zikhethiwe komyalo olandelayo, futhi `P` iyazikhetha futhi ku-prompt `Khetha izinto:` kamuva.

## Khetha konke

Ngaphandle komyalo ogijima, cindezela {{key:Mod+A}}, noma khetha {{ui:Select all}} kumenyu ye-{{ui:Edit}} › {{ui:More}} ebhalweni lamathuluzi, ukuze ukhethe yonke into ongayihlela kuthebhu evulekile. Uma sewuthayiphile okuthile emugqeni womyalo, {{key:Mod+A}} ikhetha lobo bhalo: cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze uqale ususe. Ku-prompt `Khetha izinto:`, thayipha `ALL`. {{ui:Select none}} kulowo menyu, noma {{key:Esc}}, isula ukukhetha.

:::tip
Uma uthanda ukuthi ukuchofoza ngalinye kuthathe indawo yokukhetha (futhi {{key:Shift}} ikwengeze), setha {{ui:Clicking objects}} ku-{{ui:Replaces the selection}} ku-[izilungiselelo ze-CadCraft](topic:settings).
:::

## Izinto ezingakhethwa

- Izinto ezisendaweni ecimiwe, efriziwe noma ekhiyiwe. Izinto ezikhiyiwe ziyahlala zibonakala, nawe ungazibamba, kodwa awukwazi ukuzikhetha noma ukuzishintsha.
- Ethebhini yeshidi, imodeli ebonakala ngaphakathi kwesibuko sokubuka. Ukhetha izinto zeshidi ngokwalo (ibhulokhi yesihloko, amanothi, izinhlaka zesibuko sokubuka). Ukuhlela imodeli, iya kuthebhu ethi {{ui:Model}}.

## Sula ngokushesha

Khetha izinto, bese ucindezela {{key:Delete}} (noma {{key:Backspace}} uma umugqa womyalo ungenaye ikhesa). Ekhibhodini ye-Mac, ukhiye obhalwe delete uyasebenza uma uchofoze emdwebeni; kuthi umugqa womyalo unekhesa, cindezela fn no-delete ndawonye. Lokhu kusebenzisa i-{{cmd:ERASE}} ekukhetheni. Uma kungenzeki lutho, uthayiphe okuthile emugqeni womyalo: cindezela {{key:Esc}}, noma thayipha `E` bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} esikhundleni salokho.

## Izinkinga ezivamile

- **Iwindi lithathe kancane.** Udonse ngakwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla, okuthatha okuphelele ngaphakathi kuphela. Donsa ngakwesokudla kuya kwesobunxele ukuze uthole ukuhlanganisa.
- **Ukuchofoza endaweni engenalutho kuqalise ibhokisi.** Ukuchofoza kanye endaweni engenalutho kuqala iwindi; ukuchofoza kwesibili kuliqeda. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} noma chofoza ngakwesokudla ukuliyeka.
- **Ukuchofoza endaweni engenalutho kukhiphe ukukhetha.** Yenza kanjalo, ngaphandle uma ubamba {{key:Shift}}. Ukuze ugcine ukukhetha ekuchofozeni okunjalo futhi ukusule nge-{{key:Esc}} kuphela, setha {{ui:Clicking an empty spot}} ku-{{ui:Keeps the selection}} ku-[izilungiselelo ze-CadCraft](topic:settings#clicking-an-empty-spot).
- **Umyalo usebenzise izinto ongazifuni.** Kukhona okwakusakhethwe lapho uqalisa umyalo. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ngaphambi komyalo ukuze uqale ungakhethile lutho.
- **Akukho okukhethiwe ngemva komyalo.** Lokho kwenziwe ngenhloso: umyalo owuphelile usula ukukhetha.
