## Okuzokwenza

Ifolda yephrojekthi lapho yonke idrowing inenguqulo (A.1, A.2 … bese A, B …) nesimo (Isisebenzi esiqhubekayo, Ukuze kubukwe, Ikhishiwe, Ingasasetshenziswa), lapho umuntu oyedwa kuphela ngesikhathi esisodwa engashintsha khona ifayela, nalapho konke ukuphuma, ukungenisa, ukuvunywa nokuphula ukuphuma okwenziwe kubhalwa phansi ngegama nesikhathi somuntu owenzile.

Konke kugcinwa efolda uqobo, esikhwameni esifihliwe `.ccvault` ngasemadrowingeni akho. Ifolda esendaweni eyabelwanwa noma efakwe ukuvumelanisa isebenza ithimba lonke. Asikho iseva futhi akukho okulayishwa.

## Vula Amadokhumenti

Thayipha `DOCUMENTS` (noma `DOCS`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ipaneli ivula ecaleni kwedrowing.

Khetha {{ui:Choose the project folder}} bese ukhetha ifolda equkethe amadrowing ephrojekthi. Amafolda ephrojekthi asebenza ku-CadCraft Studio nasezisekhomputheni ezingavula ifolda (Chrome, Edge). Kwezinye izisekeli ipaneli iyakutshela lokho.

Okokuqala, thayipha igama lakho (lihamba nakho konke okwenzayo) negama lale khompyutha. Uma ungene ngemvume, igama lakho seliphelile.

## Qala isitoreji

Ifolda engakayi kuba yisitoreji iniketela {{ui:Start a vault in this folder}}. Thayipha igama lephrojekthi. Bese uvula {{ui:Files not in the vault}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Add}} ecaleni kwefayela ngalinye ofuna ukulilawula. Ifayela oliyengezayo liqala ku-inguqulo **A.1**, Isisebenzi esiqhubekayo.

## Shintsha idrowing

1. Khetha {{ui:Open}} ecaleni kwedrowing. Ivula ku-CadCraft. Uma lingaphumile kuwe, libheke kuphela ungalishintshi: {{ui:Save}} ikwenqaba ukulibhalela phezu kwalo futhi ikutshele ngenxa yalokho (sebenzisa {{ui:Save as}} ukugcina ikhopi yakho).
2. Khetha {{ui:Check out}}. Akekho omunye ongaphuma kulo manje; ipaneli iyakutshela ukuthi ulaphi, kweyiphi kompyutha, kusukela nini.
3. Dweba.
4. Khetha {{ui:Check in}} bese uchaza ukuthi yini eshintshile. Idrowing ovulile ilondolozwa efolda yephrojekthi ngefomethi yayo futhi iba inguqulo elandelayo (A.1, bese A.2). Ukungenisa ngaphandle kokushintsha kugcina inguqulo.

{{ui:Undo check-out}} ibuyisela ifayela kuwe ngaphandle kwenguqulo entsha.

## Hlola nokukhipha

- {{ui:Send for review}}: ifayela lifundeka kuphela ngenkathi likhangelwa.
- {{ui:Approve}}: thayipha igama lomvumi. A.3 iba **A**, Ikhishiwe. Amafayela akhishiwe afundeka kuphela.
- {{ui:Send back}}: ibuyela ku-Isisebenzi esiqhubekayo, nesizathu.
- {{ui:New revision}} efayeleni elikhishiwe liphuma ukuze lithole inhlamvu elandelayo: ukungenisa kwalo kokuqala kuba **B.1**. {{ui:Undo check-out}} ngaphambi kwalokho liyashiya lisesi-A.
- {{ui:Make obsolete}} yenza ifayela elikhishiwe lingasasetshenziswa.

Iphrojekthi ingagcina uhlu lwabantu abangavuma, futhi ingadinga ukuthi umvumi akasiye umuntu owangenisa ifayela (bheka Izilungiselelo zephrojekthi ngezansi).

## Izilungiselelo zephrojekthi

Vula {{ui:Project settings}} kwipaneli:

- **Abavumi**: igama elilodwa ngomugqa ngamunye. Uma kukhona, kuphela bona abangavuma ukukhishwa.
- **Umvumi kufanele abe umuntu ongaye umbhali**: umthetho we-"amehlo amane".
- **Abalawuli**: igama elilodwa ngomugqa ngamunye. Uma kukhona, kuphela bona abangaphula ukuphuma kukamuntu omunye noma baguqule lezi zilungiselelo.
- **Izinhlamvu zezinguqulo okufanele zishiywe**: {{ui:Use the ASME Y14.35 sequence}} ikhipha I, O, Q, S, X no-Z, noma uthayipha izinhlamvu zakho. Izinhlamvu ezikhona zihlala; ukukhetha kusebenza kwezinguqulo ezilandelayo.
- **Ukuphuma kuba ngalahlekile emva kwezinsuku**: inani lezinsuku (14 ngokuzenzakalelayo).

Khetha {{ui:Save settings}}. Ushintsho luloglwa ngegama lomuntu owenze lo.

## Umuntu ushiye ifayela liphumile

{{ui:Break check-out}} ikhulula ifayela okuphume kulo omunye umuntu (bakhona kude, noma ikhompyutha yabo yayonakala). Kufanele uthayipha isizathu: siloglwa kanye nokuphuma kwalowo muntu. Uma iphrojekthi inabalawuli, bona kuphela abangakwenza lokhu. Ukuphuma okudlule izinsuku ezingu-14 kuphawulwa njengesikhathi eside.

## Umlando, izinguqulo, okusetshenziswa, okusetshenziswa ngu

Khetha igama lefayela ukuze ubone:

- {{ui:History}}: yonke imicimbi, eyokugcina kuqala, nomuntu, ikhompyutha nesikhathi.
- {{ui:Revisions}}: yonke inguqulo egcinwe. Ikhopi yenguqulo ngayinye isesitoreji (okuqukethwe okufanayo kugcinwa kanye). {{ui:Open copy}} ivula leyo nguqulo njengekhopi evamile engeke ibhalele phezu kwefayela lesitoreji. {{ui:Compare with}} ihlu okungezwe, okususiwe nokushintshiwe phakathi kwalena nenguqulo ngaphambilini (noma enye oyikhethile), bese iyibonisa ku-drowing.
- {{ui:Uses}}: amadrowing axhunywe kuwo (xrefs), amadokhumenti imodeli ye-IFC ekhomba kuwo, izingxenye zomhlangano we-PartCraft.
- {{ui:Used by}}: wonke amafayela esitoreji asebenzisa lona.

{{ui:Bill of materials (CSV)}} igcina uhlu lwakho konke ifayela elisebenzisayo, ngamazinga, ngezinani, ngenguqulo nangezimo, ukuze uvule ku-spreadsheet.

## Ibhulokhi yokuguqulwa esithombeni

Ngedrowing evuliwe enelayout, khetha {{ui:Revision block}}. Itafula lezinguqulo ezikhishiwe (inguqulo, usuku, ngubani, ivunyiwe, incazelo) lidwetshwa ngaphezulu ngakwesokudla kwe-sheet obukayo. Lapho uvuma ukukhishwa, itafula liyabuyekezwa.

## Inguqulo ebhulokhini yesihloko

Nika izici zebhulokhi yakho yesihloko enye yalezi tegi bese ukhetha {{ui:Fill title blocks}} kudrowing evuliwe:

| Itegi | Ugcwaliswa ngu |
|---|---|
| `VAULT.REV`, `REV`, `REVISION`, `REV_NO` | inguqulo (B, noma B.2 uma isasetshenziswa) |
| `VAULT.STATE` | isimo (Ikhishiwe …) |
| `VAULT.DATE`, `REV_DATE`, `REVDATE` | usuku lwenguqulo yakamuva |
| `VAULT.APPROVER`, `APPROVED_BY` | ubani avume ukukhishwa kwakamuva |

## PartCraft ne-BimCraft

I-PartCraft ne-BimCraft zinepaneli efanayo ye-Documents yamafayela awo: izingxenye nemihlangano ku-PartCraft, nemodeli ye-IFC ku-BimCraft. Ukungenisa kulondoloza imodeli evuliwe efolda yephrojekthi, nomhlangano ukhombisa ukuthi yiziphi izingxenye esebenzisa kuzo, nohlu lwezinto.

## Okufanele wazi

- Abantu ababili efolda eyabelwanwa kabili abakwazi ukuphuma ngefayela elifanayo: ukuphuma kuyifayela uhlelo olunqaba ukulidala kabili. Kwisiphequluli, nakuStudio enesheli edlule lesi sici, lokhu kuqinile njengoba ifolda ivumela: ukuphuma okubili ngesikhashana esifushane kungaphumelela bobabili. Ipaneli ikutshela ukuthi yiyiphi esebenzayo.
- Ungahleli noma usule ifolda `.ccvault` ngesandla. Ilogi kuyo iwumlando; isitoreji siyakha kabusha uhlu lwaso kuyo uma kudingeka.
- Ukushintsha ifayela efolda ngaphandle kokuliphuma kuboniswa njenge-{{ui:Changed in the folder without a check-out}}.
