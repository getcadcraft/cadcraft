## Ezilinganisweni ozozenza kulandela

1. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze kungakhethwa lutho: iphaneli elingakwesokudla lifunda {{ui:Izilungiselelo zamanje}}.
2. Skrola phansi kuphaneli ufike ku-{{ui:Izilinganiso ezintsha}}. Thayipha ukuphakama ofuna ephepheni ku-{{ui:Ukuphakama kombhalo}} (isibonelo `2.5` ngo-2.5 mm, noma
   `3/32"`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
3. Setha {{ui:Isikali sonke}} kusikali sokuphrinta (`50` ngo-1:50, `48` ngo-1/4" = 1'-0"): yonke usayizi iphindaphindwa ngaso.

Komugqa womyalo, {{cmd:DIMTXT}} isetha ukuphakama, ne-{{cmd:DIMSCALE}} isetha isikali sonke.

## Ezilinganisweni eseseyikhona emdwebeni

1. Khetha izilinganiso (eyodwa, noma eziningana: khetha {{ui:Isilinganiso}} oluhlwini oluphezulu kwe-Properties).
2. Thayipha {{ui:Ukuphakama kombhalo}} omusha (kwisigaba se-{{ui:Umbhalo}}), noma {{ui:Isikali sonke}} esisha (kwisigaba se-{{ui:Fit}} ngaphansi kakhulu), bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Ziphinda zidwetshwe ngokushesha, ngenyathelo elilodwa lokuhlehlisa. Isilinganiso esivela kwefayela lenye iprogram sidwetshwa ngesitayela se-CadCraft uma usishintsha.

:::tip
Umbhalo oqinile kuskrini kodwa omncane kakakhulu esheshini eliphrintiwe ngokuvamile usho ukuthi isikali sonke singu-1: setha sibe isikali seshidi.
:::
