Ishidi lingavame ukubonisa imodeli efanayo ngaphezu kwesisodwa: ipulani enefenitsha nepulani ngaphandle kwayo, noma ipulani yokukhanya lapho okukhanya kuphela kunombala. Ukushintsha kweklasi yesibonelo kushintsha indlela [iklasi](topic:classes) eboniswa ngayo **kuleso sibonelo kuphela**.

1. Vula ishidi bese ukhetha isibonelo esisodwa.
2. Vula iphaneli Yamaklasi (`CLASSES`) bese ukhetha iklasi.
3. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Kulesi sibonelo}}, thika {{ui:Fihla kulesi sibonelo}}, noma usethe {{ui:Umbala lapha}}, {{ui:Ububanzi bomugqa lapha}} noma {{ui:Ukugcwalisa lapha}}.

Uhlu olungezansi lukhombisa wonke amaklasi alo sibonelo ashintshayo. {{ui:Sula ukushintsha kwalesi sibonelo}} ususa wonke. Isenzo ngasinye siyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

## Lokho ukushintsha okuguqulayo

- **Fihla** ifihla iklasi nawo wonke amaklasi angaphansi kwalo kuleso sibonelo.
- **Umbala, ububanzi womugqa nokugcwaliswa** kushintsha izinto zeklasi ezingasethelwe ngqo kuzo, okusho ukuthi zithatha kweklasi noma kungqimba. Into enombala wayo iyawugcina, njengakushintsha kungqimba kwesibonelo.

Ukushintsha kubonakala ekuskrinini nasekuphrintweni nasemaPDF, kuhlanganise nesethi yeshidi eshicilelwe ku-PDF. Zisebenza ndawonye nezingqimba ezikhenjiwe zesibonelo nokushintsha kwezingqimba.

Ukushintsha kugcinwa kanye nomdwebo ku-DWG ne-DXF (nohlu lwamaklasi, ku-block `CADCRAFT_CLASSES`) futhi kubuya kwalo sibonelo uma ifayela livulwa ku-CadCraft.
