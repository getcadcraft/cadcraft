Khetha **Ukubuka › Okuningi › Hlelela amashidi ngami** (noma thayipha {{cmd:AUTOSHEET}}). I-CadCraft ithola izingxenye zomdwebo wakho (imibono yakho enegama, noma izinqwaba ezihlukene zemodeli), ikhethe usayizi wephepha nesikali esivamile lapho zilingana, bese izibeka emashidini amasha anebhokisi lesihloko, isichazo nesikali ngaphansi kwengxenye ngayinye.

Ngaphambi kokuthi isebenze ungashintsha okwenzayo: **Izicucu** (imibono enegama, izinqwaba, womdwebo wonke, noma vumela ukuthi kukhethe), **Iphepha** (ngokuzenzakalelayo noma usayizi onjengo `iso-a1`), **Isikali** (isikali esisodwa sazo zonke, noma esakhe kwesakhe) no-**Amashidi** (izicucu ezingaki zabelana ngeshidi elilodwa).

Amashidi ajwayelekile: hambisa i-viewport, shintsha isikali sayo noma susa ishidi njengoba bekwenzeka njalo. Cindezela {{key:Ctrl+Z}} kanye ukuze uthathe wonke amashidi amasha abuyele emuva.

## Izinkomba eziyinhloko

Ku-{{cmd:AUTOSHEET}} khetha **Izinkomba eziyinhloko** ukuze wengeze uhlu lwezinkomba olunenombolo ngaphansi kwemibono yeshidi ngalinye. Lubala izingqimba ezidwetshwe kulezo zibukwa, umugqa oyedwa onombolo ngamunye, njengombhalo osobala ongahlelwa njengombhalo wanoma yiluphi olunye. Khetha **Ifayela** ukuze ufunde uhlu lwakho lwezinkomba eziyinhloko (inombolo namagama ayo ngomugqa ngamunye, ahlukaniswe ngethebhu, ikhoma noma isemikholoni): ungqimba onegama elihambisana nenombolo oluhlwini lwakho uthatha amagama ayo. Azikho uhlu lwezinkomba eziyinhloko olufika nohlelo.
