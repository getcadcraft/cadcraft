## Okuzokwenza

Ukubuka inguqulo yangaphambili yomdwebo, nohlu lokuthi yini engafani nokwamanje: yini eyengeziwe, yini esusiwe nokuthi yini eshintshile, ekhonjiswe ngombala emdwebweni.

## Vula umlando

Thayipha `HISTORY` (noma `HIST`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma khetha {{ui:History…}} kumenyu {{ui:More}} eqenjini {{ui:View}}. Iphaneli ivuleka ecaleni komdwebo ihlale ivulekile lapho usebenza, ukuze ukwazi ukushelelisa nokusondeza umdwebo nemibala esemdwebweni.

Umlando usebenza ku-CadCraft Studio. Kusiphequluli, iphaneli isho lokho nje.

## Lapho izinguqulo zivela khona

- **Kulo kompyutha.** Njalo lapho uvula umdwebo kufayela ku-Studio, nanjalo nalapho ulondoloza, i-Studio igcina ikhophi yefayela ngaphakathi kwesitoreji sayo kulo kompyutha. Ikhophi yokuqala yi-{{ui:As opened}}: ifayela njengalokhu lalinjalo ngaphambi kokuqala ukulilondoloza. I-Studio igcina amakhophi amasha angu-25 komdwebo ngamunye, futhi okudala kakhulu kuqala uma wonke amakhophi ndawonye edlula ku-400 MB. Ukulondoloza okungashintshi lutho akugcini lutho. Akukho okulayishelwa.
- **Elifini.** Uma uvula umdwebo elifini ku-Studio, iphaneli ibuye ibonise {{ui:In the cloud}}: inguqulo ngayinye elifu liyigcinayo, nenombolo yayo, ukuthi ubani owayilondoloze, nenothi yayo. Iphaneli ifunda kuphela. I-CadCraft ayikasikhipheli ukulondolozwa kwayo elifini, ngakho lezi yizinguqulo elondoloziwe kwamanye amadivayisi nakusiphequluli. Ngena futhi ku-Studio uma ithi isessiyo sokungena siphelile.

Umdwebo ongakalondolozwa awunazo izikopi: ulondoloze kuqala.

## Vula noma buyisela inguqulo

- {{ui:Open}} ibeka leyo nguqulo ethebhini yayo. Umdwebo wakho awuthintwa.
- {{ui:Restore}} ithatha lo mdwebo ngalo mdwebo ngale nguqulo. Iyabuza kuqala. I-Studio igcina ikhophi yomdwebo njengoba unjalo manje emlandweni (imakwe ngokuthi {{ui:Before a restore}}) ngaphambi kokwenza lokho, futhi akukho okubhalwa efayelini kuze ulondoloze. Ukubuyisela akukwazi ukuhlehliswa nge-{{key:Mod+Z}}; sebenzisa leyo kopi.

## Qhathanisa

1. Chofoza {{ui:Compare}} enguqulweni. Iqhathaniswa ne-{{ui:The drawing as it is now}}; ukuze uqhathanise nenye inguqulo, khetha leyo kuqala ephaneli {{ui:Compare with}}.
2. Funda umphumela ngaphansi kohlu: ingaki imi-objekthi eyengeziwe, esusiwe nashintshiwe, nohlu lwazo. Chofoza into ohlwini ukuze ukubuka kuyofika kuyo.
3. Emdwebweni:
   - izinto **eziluhlaza** zengeziwe,
   - izinto **ezibomvu** zisusiwe (zisekuguqulweni okudala kuphela),
   - izinto **ezi-orange** zishintshile: isimo esisha sigcwele, esidala sinamadashi.
4. {{ui:Show on the drawing}} ivula futhi icime imibala; {{ui:Clear the colours}} isusa umphumela. Ukuvala iphaneli nakho kususa imibala.

Umphumela ubonisa ukuthi yini eshintshile enkintweni eshintshile: umbhalo wayo, ukuthi yahamba, isimo sayo, ungqimba, umbala, uhlobo lomugqa noma ububanzi bomugqa. Ishidi ngalinye liqhathaniswa lodwa; imibala ibonakala kushidi elivulekile.

:::note
Umdwebo awunayo inombolo ehlala njalo yento ngayinye, ngakho i-CadCraft ihlanganisa izinto ngokuthi zinjani: into efanayo ezinhlangothini zombili ayishintshile; into yohlobo olufanayo nongqimba olufanayo eseduze kosayizi wayo wangaphambili iyinto efanayo eshintshile; konke okusele kuyengeziwe noma kuyasuswa. Into ehanjiswe kude kakhulu kusuka lapho yayikhona ibonakala isusiwe kwelinye indawo nengeziwe kwelinye. Ireferensi yebhulokhi ibala okuqukethwe yibhulokhi yayo.
:::

## Amaphutha avamile

- **"Azikho izinguqulo okwamanje."** I-Studio igcina ikhophi ngayinye uma uvula noma ulondoloza umdwebo onefayela kulo kompyutha. Umdwebo ovulwe kuthebhu lesiphequluli noma ongakaze ulondolozwe awunazo.
- **Uhlu lwelifu alutholakali.** Luvela umdwebo ovulwe elifini ku-Studio; ifayela elivulwe kudiski akusiyo umdwebo welifu.
- **Konke kubukeka kushintshile.** Izinguqulo ezimbili ezilondolozwe izinhlelo ezihlukene zibhala umdwebo ngezindlela ezihlukene (amagama amabhulokhi angenagama, ukuzungeza). Qhathanisa izinguqulo ezilondolozwe yi-CadCraft.
