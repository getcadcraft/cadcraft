CadCraft waxaa loo sameeyay maawuse leh tuubbo. Wax kasta waxay sidoo kale ku shaqeeyaan trackpad, oo leh dhowr caado oo la baranayo. Shaashadaha taabashada waxay ku shaqeeyaan doorashada, laakiin xadud leh.

## Maawuse tuubbo leh

- **Zoom:** rogo tuubbada. Naqshaddu waxay zoom-gareysaa iyada oo crosshair-ka ku wareegsan, sidaas darteed u tilmaam waxa aad rabto inaad aragto.
- **Pan:** hay tuubbada hoose oo jiid, ama jiid badhanka midig.
- **Zoom ilaa wax walba:** laba jeer guji tuubbada, ama guji {{ui:Extents}}.
- **Ku noqo muuqaalkii hore:** guji {{ui:Previous}} kooxda Muuqaal, ama ku qor `Z`, {{key:Enter}}, `P`, {{key:Enter}}. Silsilad tallaabooyin tuubbo ah waxay u tirsan tahay hal muuqaal, sidaas darteed hal {{ui:Previous}} ayaa ka noqda. Muuqaalada aad dib ugu noqonaysid ku kaydi {{cmd:NAMEDVIEW}} (`NV`).
- **Enter:** guji midig.

## Trackpad

:::mac
- **Pan:** dhaqaaq laba farood trackpad-ka, dhinac kasta: naqshaddu wuu raacaa farahaaga. Waxaad sidoo kale jiidi kartaa laba-farood-guji oo hayso (jiid badhanka midig).
- **Zoom:** ku pinch-garee laba farood. Zoom-ku wuxuu raacaa crosshair-ka.
- **Guji midig (Enter):** guji laba farood. Oggolow "Secondary click" ku jira **System Settings**, kadibna **Trackpad**, haddii aanay waxba sameynin. Control-guji waxaa lagu tiri karaa guji caadi ah CadCraft-ka gudihiisa halkii guji midig, sidaas darteed isticmaal laba-farood-guji.
- **Zoom aag:** guji {{ui:Window}} kooxda Muuqaal, kadibna guji laba geesood.
:::

:::windows linux
- **Pan:** dhaqaaq laba farood touchpad-ka, dhinac kasta: naqshaddu wuu raacaa farahaaga. Waxaad sidoo kale jiidi kartaa adigoo badhanka midig hayaya.
- **Zoom:** ku pinch-garee laba farood. Zoom-ku wuxuu raacaa crosshair-ka.
- **Guji midig (Enter):** guji laba farood, ama guji geeska hoose ee midig ee touchpad-ka, iyadoo ku xidhan dejinta touchpad-kaaga.
- **Zoom aag:** guji {{ui:Window}} kooxda Muuqaal, kadibna guji laba geesood.
:::

Hayida {{key:Space}} oo jiidida **ma** pan-gareyso CadCraft: {{key:Space}} waa Enter.

Tuubbada maawuseed weli waa zoom-gareysaa, hal tallaabo mar. Touchpad-ka oo u diraya socodka tallaabooyin oo dhan, sida tuubbada, wuxuu zoom-gareysaa laba-farood-scroll halkii uu pan-gareyn lahaa: zoom ka bax, u tilmaam aagga aad rabto, kadibna dib u zoom gal. Tab-ka 3D wuxuu hayaa dhaqdhaqaaqyadiisa trackpad-ka (eeg [Moodeelka 3D](topic:3d-solids#look-around)).

## Shaashadaha taabashada iyo tablet-yada

CadCraft wuxuu farta u akhriyaa sida guji maawuse:

- Taabasho wuxuu doortaa barta ama walxda, ama wuu doortaa.
- Jiidida farta meel bannaan wuxuu sameeyaa lasso (dhinac midig marka hore daaqad, dhinac bidix marka hore crossing; eeg [Doorashada lasso](topic:lasso-selection)).
- {{cmd:TRIM}} iyo {{cmd:EXTEND}}, jiidida farta marta qaybaha wuxuu gooyaa ama fidiyaa dhammaantood.
- Jiidida gripka walxda la doortay wuxuu dhaqaajiyaa gripka (gripsyada iyo sanduuqa doorashada waa weyn yihiin farta).
- **Pinch** laba farood si aad zoom-gareyso iyada oo ku saleysan barta u dhexaysa; dhaqaaq laba farood wada-jir si aad **pan**-gareyso. Farta labaad ayaa joojisa waxa kowaad bilaabday, sidaas darteed pinch weligeed uma reebo calaamad.
- **Long press** (hay si joogto ah ilaa nus ilbiriqsi) waa guji midig: {{key:Enter}}. Waxay joojisaa amar, dhammeysaa doorasho, ama soo celisaa amarka ugu dambeeya.

Hoosta command line-ka waxaa soo muuqda **safka furayaasha**: {{ui:Esc}}, Undo, Redo, xarfaha kiiboodhka tablet-ka qarsada (`@` `,` `<` `'` `"` `-`), {{ui:Snap}}, {{ui:Ortho}}, badhanka kiiboodhka, iyo {{ui:Enter}} oo weyn. Taabo badhanka kiiboodhka (ama command line-ka) si aad amar ama tiro u ku qorto.

Hover ma jiro, sidaas darteed calaamadaha snap waxay muuqdaan kaliya meesha farta ku taabanayso.

:::touch
Waxaad isticmaalaysaa shaashad taabasho. Wax ka badan arag degdeg ah, kiiboodh ku xidhan iyo maawuse ama trackpad ayaa CadCraft ka dhigaya mid aad uga fudud.
:::

## Browserada

CadCraft wuxuu isku mid u shaqeeyaa Chrome, Edge, Firefox iyo Safari, Mac, Windows iyo Linux. Kala duwanaanshaha waa yar:

| Mawduuc | Waxa la ogaado |
|---|---|
| Fur DWG | Naqshado waaweyn waxay qaataan dhowr ilbiriqsi si ay u akhriyaan browser kasta; status line-ku wuxuu muujinayaa "Reading …" intaas dhexdeed |
| Soo deji | Browser kasta wuxuu go'aaminayaa meesha soo dejinta ay tagayso iyo in la weydiiyo ama lama weydiiyo; eeg [Browserada iyo kumbuyuutarada](common:browsers) |
| Daabac furan | Wuxuu furaa PDF tab cusub. Haddii browser-ku xiro pop-up, CadCraft wuxuu soo dejiyaa PDF-ka halkii |
| F7 | Firefox waxay bixin kartaa caret browsing; dooro No |
| Ka bax isbedel aan la kaydin | Browser kasta wuxuu muujiyaa su'aashiisa "Leave site?", taas oo CadCraft aan beddeli karin |

:::safari
Safari, PDF laga furay {{ui:Open to print}} wuxuu ku muuqan karaa tab-ka iyada oo aan la daabicin: isticmaal **File**, kadibna **Print**.
:::

:::firefox
Firefox waxay weydiin kartaa waxa la sameeyo fayl kasta oo la soo dejinayo. Dooro in faylka la kaydiyo, ama beddel hal mar dejinta Firefox ee hoos ku yaal Downloads.
:::
