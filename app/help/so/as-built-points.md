{{cmd:ASBUILT}} (magac kale ASB) waa qaybta kale ee {{cmd:EXPORTPOINTS}}: waxaad u dhoofisaa barta naqshadda goobta, kooxda ayaa dhigta oo cabbirta wixii la dhisay, ASBUILT ayaa barbardhigaa faylkooda naqshadda.

1. Ku qor {{cmd:ASBUILT}} oo dooro barta iyo bloogyada isku mid ah ee aad dhoofisay (Enter wuxuu qaadaa dhammaan POINT-yada, sida EXPORTPOINTS).
2. **Isku-xidh barta as-built** **Lambar** (kooxdu waxay hayaan lambarradaada barta: bilow lambarka aad ka dhoofisay) ama **Ugu dhow** (iyagu lambarradooda ayay siiyeen: barta naqshad kasta waxay qaadataa midka ugu dhow).
3. Bixi unugyada faylka iyo isku-xidhka sida aad u dhoofisay (Drawing, Metres ama Feet; World ama UCS), iyo **dulqaadka** unugyada naqshadda.
4. Dooro faylka CSV ama TXT ee kooxda. CadCraft wuxuu u akhrinayaa sida CivilCraft: koomo, tab, semicolon ama meel banaan, safka madax oo magacaabaya tiirarkiisa, ama PNEZD marka la tiriyo tiirarka.

Khadka amarku wuxuu sheegaa inta barta la isku xidhay, inta dulqaadka gudihiisa ku jirta, inta ka baxsan, inta aan la cabbirin iyo inta dheeraadka ah, iyo masaafada ugu weyn. **Ma calaamadiyaa naqshadda?** Haa wuxuu dhigaa lakabka **AS-BUILT** isla hal tallaabo oo ka noqda: barta mid kasta oo cabbir ah, lambarkeeda iyo masaafadeeda agtiisa, iyo xariiq casaan ah oo ka sii dhex marta barta naqshadda ilaa cabbir kasta oo ka baxsan dulqaadka. Warbixin CSV ah ayaa la soo dejiyaa oo leh barta kasta: isku-xidhka naqshadda iyo as-built, kala duwanaanshaha bari, woqooyi iyo joogga, iyo natiijada.

Eeg sidoo kale [Barta barnaamijyada BIM](topic:points-for-bim).
