Gaabiyeyaasha waxa lagu muujiyaa kumbuyuutarkaaga. Kumbuyuutarka Mac-ka, furayaasha {{key:Mod}} oo dhan waxay isticmaalaan Command; furaha Control ayaa sidoo kale shaqeeya. Sida furayaasha loo qoro, iyo sida F-furayaasha loo isticmaalo Mac-ka, waxaa ku jira [Furayaasha, maawisyada iyo trackpads-ka](common:keys).

## Qorista amarrada

| Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|
| Xaraf kasta, lambar ama calaamad | Wuxuu gaadhaa sadarka amarka, meel kasta oo maawisku joogo, marka laga reebo sanduuq panel-ka ku jira oo cursor-ku ku jiro |
| {{key:Enter}} | Wuxuu diraa wixii aad qortay. Prompt madhan: wuu soo celinayaa amarkii u dambeeyay, wuxuu dhammeeyaa amar, wuxuu dhammeeyaa doorasho, ama wuxuu aqbalaa default-ka `<…>` |
| {{key:Space}} | Sida {{key:Enter}} oo kale, marka laga reebo markii CadCraft qoraal weydiinayo (qoraal, magacyada blockyada, qoraalka cabbirka), halkaas wuxuu qoraa meel-bannaan |
| {{key:Esc}} | Wuu joojiyaa amarka. Amar socda ma jiro: wuxuu joojiyaa guurka qabsiga ama sanduuqa doorashada, kadibna nadiifiyaa doorashada |
| {{key:Tab}} | Wuxuu aqbalaa amarka lagu soo jeediyay ee midig ka muuqda sadarka amarka. Wax aan la qorin, maawisku shay isku dhacaya ku yaal: wuxuu iftiimiyaa shayga xiga (guji si aad u doorato) |
| {{key:Up}} {{key:Down}} | Wuxuu ku dhaqaajiyaa khadadka aad hore u qortay (100-ka ugu dambeeya) |

## Naqshadaynta iyo tafatirka

| Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|
| {{key:Mod+Z}} | Wuxuu ka noqdaa amarkii u dambeeyay |
| {{key:Mod+Y}} ama {{key:Mod+Shift+Z}} | Samee mar kale |
| {{key:Mod+A}} | Wuxuu doortaa shay kasta oo aad tafatiri karto tabkii furnaa ({{cmd:SELECTALL}}). Haddii aad wax ku qortay sadarka amarka, qoraalkaas ayuu doortaa |
| {{key:Delete}} | Wuxuu tirtiraa walxaha la doortay ({{cmd:ERASE}}), marka laga reebo haddii aad wax ku qortay sadarka amarka |
| {{key:Backspace}} | Wuxuu tirtiraa walxaha la doortay marka sadarka amarka uusan cursor lahayn; sadarka amarka wuxuu tirtiraa wixii aad qortay |
| {{key:Esc}} | Amar socda ma jiro: nadiifi doorashada ({{cmd:DESELECT}}) |
| {{key:Mod+S}} | Ku keydi nuqul qaabka naqshadda (DWG ama DXF) |
| {{key:Mod+Shift+S}} | U kaydi sida: dooro DWG ama DXF, kadibna keydi nuqul |
| {{key:Mod+O}} | Fur DWG ama DXF |
| {{key:Mod+P}} | Daabac PDF |

Ka noqo, samee mar kale, dooro dhammaan iyo daabacaadda ma shaqeeyaan inta amar socda: marka hore riix {{key:Esc}}.

## Koobiyee, jar iyo dhaji

| Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|
| {{key:Mod+C}} | Wuxuu koobiyeeyaa xulashada clipboard-ka ({{cmd:COPYCLIP}}). Wax la doortay ma jiro: CadCraft wuxuu weydiiyaa walxaha |
| {{key:Mod+X}} | Jar: koobiyee xulashada, kadibna tirtir ({{cmd:CUTCLIP}}) |
| {{key:Mod+V}} | Dhaji: nuqulku wuxuu raacaa cursor-ka; guji ama ku qor barta si aad u dhigto ({{cmd:PASTECLIP}}) |
| {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} | Dhaji meeshii la mid ahayd: isla meelaha laga koobiyeeyay ({{cmd:PASTEORIG}}) |
| {{key:Mod+D}} | Labiyee: nuqul xulashada ah ayaa maawiska la socda; guji si aad u dhigto, {{key:Esc}} wax ma uusan dhigin ({{cmd:DUPLICATE}}) |

Isla falalkan waxa ku jira kooxda {{ui:Tafatir}} ee toolbar-ka ({{ui:Koobiyee}}, {{ui:Dhaji}}) iyo menu-ka {{ui:Wax dheeraad ah}}. Inta amar kale socdo, dhammee ama riix {{key:Esc}} marka hore; {{key:Mod+V}} ama {{key:Mod+D}} mar kale riixida nuqul maawiska la socda ayaa bilaabaya mar kale. Markaad wax ku qortay sadarka amarka, ama amar qoraal weydiinayo, furayaashan waxay koobiyeeyaan, jaraan oo dhajiyaan qoraalkaas. Eeg [Koobiyee, dhaji iyo labiyee](topic:copy-paste).

## Furayaasha beddelka

| Furaha | Beddel | Badhanka status bar |
|---|---|---|
| {{key:F3}} | Object snaps daar ama damee | {{ui:Snap}} |
| {{key:F7}} | Shabakad daar ama damee | {{ui:Shabakad}} |
| {{key:F8}} | Ortho daar ama damee | {{ui:Ortho}} |
| {{key:F10}} | Polar tracking daar ama damee | {{ui:Polar}} |
| {{key:F11}} | Object snap tracking daar ama damee | {{ui:Track}} |
| {{key:F12}} | Dynamic input daar ama damee | {{ui:Dyn}} |

:::mac
Mac-ka, F3, F7 iyo F8 badanaa waa furayaasha Mission Control iyo warbaahinta. Ku hay **fn** marka aad riixayso, ama daar furayaasha function-ka caadiga ah System Settings-ka, sida lagu sharxay [Furayaasha, maawisyada iyo trackpads-ka](common:keys). Badhamada status bar-ka had iyo jeer way shaqeeyaan.
:::

:::firefox
Firefox-ka, F7 wuxuu sidoo kale weydiin karaa inuu shido caret browsing. Dooro **No**, oo isticmaal badhanka {{ui:Shabakad}} haddii uu sii weydiiyo.
:::

## Caawimaad

| Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|
| {{key:F1}} | Wuxuu furaa ama xiraa caawimaadan dhinaca naqshadda. Wuxuu shaqeeyaa xitaa amar socda, mana gaadho sadarka amarka. |
| {{key:Esc}} | Cursor-ku panel-ka caawimaadda ku jiro: nadiifi raadinta ama xir panel-ka |

Badhanka {{ui:Caawimaad}} ee bar-ka sare wuxuu sameeyaa wixii {{key:F1}} sameeyo. Inta aad ku qorayso sanduuqa raadinta caawimaadda, waxba ma gaadhayo sadarka amarka. Guji naqshadda si aad furayaasha ugu celiso CadCraft; panel-ku wuu furan yahay dhinacdeeda.

:::mac
Kiiboodhka Mac-ka, ku hay **fn** oo riix F1, ama guji {{ui:Caawimaad}}.
:::

## Shaashadda

| Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|
| {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} | Wareejiyaa: caadi, shaashad buuxda, iyo shaashad buuxda oo xarriqyada la qariyay. Wuxuu isticmaalaa Control kumbuyuutar kasta, Mac-ka oo ku jira |
| {{key:Left}} {{key:Right}} | Tab-ka hoose naqshadda oo diiradda ku jirta: tab-ka hore ama xiga |

Shaashad buuxda, browser-ku wuxuu isticmaali karaa {{key:Esc}} ugu horreeya si uu uga baxo shaashadda buuxda. Riix {{key:Esc}} mar kale si aad u joojiso amarka.

## Sanduuqyada hadalka

| Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|
| {{key:Esc}} | Wuxuu xiraa sanduuqa ({{ui:Daabac PDF}}, ama su'aal sida "Isbeddel aan la keydin", halkaas oo micnaheedu yahay {{ui:Jooji}}) |
| {{key:Tab}} | Wuxuu u dhaqaajiyaa dejinta sanduuqa iyo badhamada |

Sanduuq furan ayuu jiraa, furayaasha naqshaddu way damaan.

## Goobaha panel-ka

Sanduuq ku yaal panel-ka midig ama bidix:

| Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|
| {{key:Enter}} | Wuxuu dabaqaa qiimaha oo ka baxaa sanduuqa |
| {{key:Esc}} | Wuxuu soo celinayaa qiimihii hore oo ka baxaa sanduuqa |
| {{key:Tab}} | Wuxuu dabaqaa oo u gudbaa sanduuqa xiga |

Sanduuq kasta oo cursor-ku ku jiro, xarfuhu halkaas ayay ku qoraan, ma aha sadarka amarka.

## Maawiska

| Falka | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|
| Guji | Wuxuu doortaa barta marka amarku weydiiyo barta; wuxuu doortaa shay marka weydiiyo walxaha; haddii kale wuxuu doortaa shay ama qabsi |
| Shift+click | Wuxuu ka saaraa shay la doortay doorashada (marka {{ui:Riixida shayada}} la dejiyo {{ui:Wuxuu beddelaa xulashada}} ee [Dejinta](topic:settings): wuxuu ku daraa). Markii barta la doortayo: wuxuu iska indha-tiraa object snaps gujintaas |
| Guji meel bannaan, kadibna mar kale guji | Sanduuq doorasho: bidix ilaa midig waa daaqad (window), midig ilaa bidix waa isgoyn (crossing) |
| Jiid meel bannaan | Lasso: haddii marka hore midig u dhaqaajiso waa daaqad, bidix waa isgoyn; {{key:Space}} inta aad jiidayso wuxuu wareejiyaa daaqad, isgoyn iyo fence; {{key:Esc}} wuu tuuraa (LASSOMODE 0, ama Dejinta, ayaa mar kale ka dhigaa sanduuq) |
| Jiid qaybaha TRIM ama EXTEND dhexdooda | Wuxuu jaraa (ama dheereeyaa) qayb kasta oo jidku gudbo; ku hay {{key:Shift}} si aad u hesho kii kale |
| Jiid qabsi | Wuxuu guuraa qabsiga (ama guji qabsiga, kadibna guji meesha uu tagayo) |
| Laba jeer guji qoraal | Wuu doortaa, cursor-kana wuxuu geliyaa sanduuqa {{ui:Qoraal}} ee panel-ka midig |

| Jiid badhanka midig | Wuxuu guuraa muuqaalka |
| Kaabka | Wuxuu weynaysaa iyo yareeyaa meesha crosshair-ku ku yaal |
| Jiid kaabka (badhanka dhexe) | Wuxuu guuraa muuqaalka |
| Laba jeer guji kaabka | Wuxuu weynaysaa naqshadda oo dhan (dhammaan xadka) |

Trackpad ama shaashad taabasho, eeg [Maawiska, trackpad-ka iyo taabashada](topic:mouse-trackpad-touch).

## Furayaasha browser-ku hayo

{{key:Mod+N}}, {{key:Mod+W}} iyo {{key:Mod+T}} had iyo jeer waa kuwa browser-ka. CadCraft ma isticmaalo: isticmaal {{ui:Cusub}} ama ku qor `NEW` naqshad cusub. Clipboard-ka CadCraft waa mid gaar ah: {{key:Mod+C}} iyo {{key:Mod+V}} waxay koobiyeeyaan walxaha inta u dhexaysa naqshadaha iyo tabs-ka CadCraft, ma aha kuwa u gudba ama ka yimaada barnaamijyada kale.

:::mac
Haddii {{key:Mod+Y}} furo taariikhda browser-kaaga halkii uu dib-u-samayn lahaa, isticmaal {{key:Mod+Shift+Z}} ama badhanka {{ui:Samee mar kale}}.
:::

## Meesha ay Move ku taal

Move waa amarka `MOVE` (furaha {{key:M}}): dooro walxaha, kadibna barta salka iyo barta labaad ama masaafo la qoro. `ALIGN` iyo `DISTRIBUTE` ayaa sidoo kale jira. BimCraft wuxuu isticmaalaa magacyo isku mid ah shuqullo isku mid ah oo ku saabsan qaybaha dhismaha, laakiin {{key:M}} wuxuu leeyahay Measure: Move-kiisa waa {{key:Shift+M}}.
