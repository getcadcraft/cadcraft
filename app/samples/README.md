# CadCraft's sample drawings

Drawn by CadCraft (0.6 DF.9), dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: use them for anything, no credit needed.
Made once by `tools/cadcraft-test/make-samples.mjs` (repo root of the site); File › Open a sample (SAMPLES) reads
`index.json`. The same files are CadCraft's showcase drawings (`fixtures/showcase/cadcraft-sample-*.{dwg,dxf}`).

- `sample-house.dwg` / `.dxf` — a small house in feet and inches: plan, front elevation, section, wall detail, four ARCH D
  sheets with the `TB-ARCH-D` title block (attributes PROJECT, TITLE, SCALE, DATE, DRAWN, SHEET).
- `sample-asbuilt.dwg` / `.dxf` — an office measured from a scan, in millimetres: a scan slice (POINTs), traced walls, revision
  clouds, dimensions in millimetres and in feet and inches, one A3 sheet at 1:25 with the `TB-A3` title block.
